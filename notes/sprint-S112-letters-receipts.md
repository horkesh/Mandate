## Implementation choices for the lead [I]

- Absent saved collections are canonically empty; eager empty arrays would
  change legacy hashes. No hash algorithm or scheme was changed.
- A correlated report selector resolves the authored letter's exact **first
  dispatch**, not a silently moving latest origin. An omitted correlation
  selects an uncorrelated report. Another retry-specific selector is not built.
- A deliberate retry repeats an already-dispatched instruction and its met
  prerequisite. It is not a new doctrine action; a new action rechecks bounded
  claims. Retaining every discarded report or automatically retrying is rejected.
- Selector and per-paper claim limits are pack rules, with no new kernel
  gameplay default. Fictional fixtures choose their own bound.

Also reject post/date suppression for opted-in letters, implicit latest-date
replacement, issue-as-receipt, cross-seat/private-truth gates, split-paper claim
joins, invented values and historical report injection. These choices implement
0121/S110; no new accepted decision is authored here.
