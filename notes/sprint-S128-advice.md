questions are offered to the principal, and the history rule seat asks them by
their doctrine dates. Each adviser gets an ordinary authored courier packet and
answers in his own name. The packet binds question, subject and course keys to
the round; altered terms and a failed destination file no receipt. Sender copies
are bounded by round/recipient, with an explicit linked retry. No sending-as-
receipt, automatic answer/event or grant of authority is introduced.

resource papers. Ties without a settled stance remain undetermined. Feasibility
exposes needs, known means, readiness/deadline and paper provenance; missing
amounts remain null, and a new partial paper cannot borrow old missing fields.
March 15 asks about wisdom assuming physical possibility. Existing stances and
memoranda remain. Schema 6 adds optional enclosures/copies with an identity
migration; all older fixtures are preserved. FOUNDATION section 10 lists the
writers. One answer's course projections share one register credit and count as
one raw act.
