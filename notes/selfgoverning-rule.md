# The `selfGoverning` rule (decision 0069)

Branch `selfgoverning-rule`, cut from main 52600e3. Built by the lane 0.8 session, as the lead asked after 0066 (the war-flag checks have the same shape).

## What is built

| Piece | Where |
|---|---|
| `departmentKinds[].selfGoverning` (default false): schema, kernel type, published JSON Schemas; the pack validator refuses a self-governing kind that is not a government (`self-governing-not-government`) | `pack-format/src/schemas.ts`, `validate.ts`, `kernel/src/world/world.ts` |
| **Pack validator:** a `department_side` or `government_closed` proposal to `command` whose subject is a self-governing department is an error (`self-governing-decision`) unless it is in a notice's effects: a decision option, inaction or a locked cost is refused | `pack-format/src/situations.ts` (`commandProposal`, called with `def.kind === 'notice'`) |
| **Kernel guard:** `applyCommandProposals` refuses the same at run time with the typed reason `self_governing_decision`. A proposal carries its `origin` (`notice` or `decision`), set where the situation applies its effects (`tick/situations.ts`); refusals are `{ reason, why }` (`REFUSAL_REASONS`), and the `proposal_refused` map event carries the reason | `kernel/src/command/command.ts`, `tick/context.ts`, `tick/forces.ts` |
| **Region moves:** `regionMoveProblem(map, from, to)`: a region held by a self-governing department passes only to another department of the same kind (a nation's own rival governments, never annexation to a state, a territory or a military department) | `kernel/src/command/command.ts` |
| Tests | `kernel/test/self-governing.test.ts` (units and end to end through the tick, notice passes and decision refused); `pack-format/test/command-proposals.test.ts` (each place a decision can put it, the notice that passes, the ordinary government left alone, the non-government kind) |

## Choices

1. **`origin` is a field of the proposal, set at the source** (`def.kind` where the effects are applied), not looked up later in the tick. *Rejected:* looking the situation up in `command`'s phase (it needed the proposal's situation to exist in the World, which a test double did not have, and a lookup can drift from what applied the effect).
2. **Typed refusals replace the strings** (`refused` is `{ reason, why }[]`), so a later guard adds a reason, not a sentence to match. Existing tests compare `why`.
3. **The kernel cannot tell an option from inaction or a locked cost, and need not**: all three are a decision's, and 0069 refuses them alike. The pack validator names which (`options[i].effects[j]`, `inaction.effects[j]`, `options[i].lockedCosts[j]`).
4. **The testbed carries no self-governing kind** (no pack data changes, as 0069 says): the kernel fixture's vocabulary gains a `nation` kind, and the validator controls mark `province` self-governing in memory.

## Not built, and why

- **No proposal moves a region's government in Phase 0.** `regionGovernments` has no run-time writer, so the "moves its regions" half of 0069 has a guard function (`regionMoveProblem`, tested) but nothing to refuse yet. Whatever later writes a region's government (the map split, a situation proposal) must call it, and the pack validator must then name that field alongside the other two. A one-writer test cannot find an unwritten field: this is the lead's to track.
- **The generated `data/world.json` of both packs gains `"selfGoverning": false` on each department kind** (the schema's default, regenerated by `pack:generate`; no source file changed).
- **The testbed save fixture was regenerated** (`npx tsx tools/make-save-fixture.ts`, as its test says to when the World changes before a public build): only its `worldHash` differs, because the World gained the default field; its State, log and both of their hashes are identical.
