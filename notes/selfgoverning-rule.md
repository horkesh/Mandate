# The `selfGoverning` rule (decision 0069)

Branch `selfgoverning-rule`, cut from main 52600e3. Built by the lane 0.8 session, as the lead asked after 0066 (the war-flag checks have the same shape).

## What is built

| Piece | Where |
|---|---|
| `departmentKinds[].selfGoverning` (default false): schema, kernel type, published JSON Schemas; the pack validator refuses a self-governing kind that is not a government (`self-governing-not-government`) | `pack-format/src/schemas.ts`, `validate.ts`, `kernel/src/world/world.ts` |
| **Pack validator:** a `department_side` or `government_closed` proposal to `command` whose subject is a self-governing department is an error (`self-governing-decision`) unless it is in a notice's effects: a decision option, inaction or a locked cost is refused | `pack-format/src/situations.ts` (`commandProposal`, called with `def.kind === 'notice'`) |
| **Kernel guard:** `applyCommandProposals` refuses the same at run time with the typed reason `self_governing_decision`. A proposal carries its `origin` (`notice` or `decision`), set where the situation applies its effects (`tick/situations.ts`); refusals are `{ reason, why }` (`REFUSAL_REASONS`), and the `proposal_refused` map event carries the reason | `kernel/src/command/command.ts`, `tick/context.ts`, `tick/forces.ts` |
| **Region moves:** `regionMoveProblem(map, from, to)`: a region held by a self-governing department passes only to another department of the same kind (a nation's own rival governments, never annexation to a state, a territory or a military department) | `kernel/src/command/command.ts` |
| Tests | `kernel/test/self-governing.test.ts` (units and end to end through the tick, notice passes and decision refused); `pack-format/test/command-proposals.test.ts` (each place a decision can put it, the notice that passes, the ordinary government left alone, the non-government kind) |

## Choices

1. **`origin` is a field of the proposal, set at the source** (`def.kind` where the effects are applied), not looked up later in the tick. *Rejected:* looking the situation up in `command`'s phase (it needed the proposal's situation to exist in the World, which a test double did not have, and a lookup can drift from what applied the effect).
2. **Typed refusals replace the strings** (`refused` is `{ reason, why }[]`), so a later guard adds a reason, not a sentence to match. Existing tests compare `why`.
3. **The kernel cannot tell an option from inaction or a locked cost, and need not**: all three are a decision's, and 0069 refuses them alike. The pack validator names which (`options[i].effects[j]`, `inaction.effects[j]`, `options[i].lockedCosts[j]`).
4. **The testbed carries no self-governing kind** (no pack data changes, as 0069 says): the kernel fixture's vocabulary gains a `nation` kind, and the validator controls mark `province` self-governing in memory.

## Not built, and why

- **No proposal moves a region's government in Phase 0.** `regionGovernments` has no run-time writer, so the "moves its regions" half of 0069 has a guard function (`regionMoveProblem`, tested) but nothing to refuse yet. Whatever later writes a region's government (the map split, a situation proposal) must call it, and the pack validator must then name that field alongside the other two. A one-writer test cannot find an unwritten field: this is the lead's to track.
- **The generated `data/world.json` of both packs gains `"selfGoverning": false` on each department kind** (the schema's default, regenerated by `pack:generate`; no source file changed).
- **The testbed save fixture was regenerated** (`npx tsx tools/make-save-fixture.ts`, as its test says to when the World changes before a public build): only its `worldHash` differs, because the World gained the default field; its State, log and both of their hashes are identical.

## Decision 0070 additions (a nation's act must be its own)

Both reviewers showed two ways round 0069; 0070 closes them, and the same-kind region rule's third.

| Piece | Where |
|---|---|
| **`actingGovernment`**: an optional field of a situation (absent keeps every existing item's sign-off hash). A notice that moves a self-governing department names whose act it is, and the value must equal the proposal's subject: `acting-government-missing` and `acting-government-mismatch`. Such a notice must carry a gate topic and a sign-off (`self-governing-gate`) | `kernel/src/situations/types.ts`, `pack-format/src/situations.ts` (schema), `situation-layer.ts` (`nationActs`) |
| **No principal's choice moves a nation, by any route.** `choiceSeeds` takes the first step (an item that reads that a decision was decided, a flag one of its options, locked costs or inaction sets, or a chain stage after it); `dependenceTaint` takes it to a fixed point with 0060's own dependence rules, so a flag laundered through a notice, or the firing of a tainted notice, is found (`self-governing-choice`, naming the route). `atrocityTaint` is now a wrapper over the same function. News of a decision, or its firing, is not a choice: it is allowed, and the sign-off must carry the reviewer's note on that route, keyed `route|<situation id>` (`self-governing-route-note`) | `pack-format/src/gate.ts` (`dependenceTaint`, `choiceSeeds`, `choiceTaint`, `triggerDependencies`), `situation-layer.ts` |
| **`polity`**: an optional generic id on departments (an optional `polity` column of `departments.csv`; absent keeps every other department's record and the World's hash). A self-governing department without one is refused (`self-governing-no-polity`); two polities never govern one region in any scenario start (`polity-shared-region`) | `kernel/src/world/world.ts`, `pack-format/src/{schemas,layout,generate,validate}.ts` |
| **The polity check in the guard:** `regionMoveProblem` now allows a region to pass only to a department of the same kind **and the same polity** (a nation's own rival governments, never another nation's); the reserved refusal reason is `self_governing_region`, to be returned by whatever writes the field | `kernel/src/command/command.ts` |
| **The tripwire:** a test fails if any kernel code writes `regionGovernments` outside setup and the State type, and its message names `regionMoveProblem` and the validator's list of command fields | `kernel/test/region-governments-tripwire.test.ts` |

Choices: (1) a *choice route* seeds the taint only through reading, flags or chain, never news or firing, because 0070 allows what a nation answers (events, news) and refuses what it follows (the principal's option); once an item is tainted, every route out of it carries the taint, so a notice cannot launder a choice. (2) Route notes are per direct dependency, in the sign-off's own `notes`, as review-severity wording notes are, so a note on one route never covers another. (3) A decision that moves a nation is left to 0069's refusal, not checked again. *Rejected:* refusing any notice that follows events or news (0070); a boolean "depends on a decision" with no route (the reviewer cannot read what it cannot see).

Tests: `pack-format/test/nation-acts.test.ts` (both reviewers' probes, the flag laundered through a notice, a side's notice with no matching acting government; the route-note cases; polity on the testbed with in-memory edits) and `kernel/test/self-governing.test.ts` (the polity check in `regionMoveProblem`).

## Gate re-check at b985c96 (approve, R1, R2, N4, N5 closed)

- **R1, clearing a flag is a route.** `flagsSetBy` now counts a `clearFlag` as a write, so a condition reading `not flag` follows an option that clears it. This is in the shared dependence function, so **0060's atrocity taint changes too** (a clear now carries an atrocity's dependence on, as a set does); all existing gate tests pass, and a control in `nation-acts.test.ts` shows it in both. The lead may want to confirm that is wanted for 0060.
- **R2, State predicates.** A nation-moving notice that reads a standing goal is refused (`self-governing-goal`: the goal is the principal's own order); one that reads a headquarters needs a route note keyed `route|state:headquartersAt:<post>:<region>`.
- **N4:** a two-hop choice control, with ids ordered so a one-pass fixed point would miss it.
- **N5:** the cross-start polity check also refuses a region one polity holds in one start and a department of no polity holds in another (a department of no polity counts as none; an `unknown` government is not a department and is left alone).

## Gate re-check at a841041 (approve; R3 closed)

- **R3, a goal read laundered through a middle notice.** `choiceSeeds` also seeds any item whose trigger or option reads a standing goal (origin `goal:<post>`), so the principal's order is tainted to a fixed point like any other choice: a notice that reads a goal and sets a flag, and a nation notice that reads the flag, is `self-governing-choice` even with a `route|...` note. A direct read stays its own error (`self-governing-goal`), so it is not reported twice. A headquarters through a middle item stays news, covered by a note. Controls in `nation-acts.test.ts`.
