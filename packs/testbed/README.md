# Testbed pack: the Two Rivers

This is a fictional, setting-free two-faction war used to exercise the kernel. It belongs to no real history, so it can be changed freely to provoke mechanisms.

## Format

The pack is **world format v2** (lane 0.2; `notes/lane-0.2-world-format.md`): `pack.json`, `PROVENANCE.json`, `strings/en.json` and the tables under `source/` are hand-written; `data/` is generated by `npm run pack:generate -- testbed`, and `npm test` fails when it is stale. Play it in the console with `npm run play -- testbed`. (The M2 scenario file, `scenario.json`, retired with M2 in lane 1.13.)

## The cast

The cast was chosen so the first turns exercise each M1 mechanism:

- `n_organiser`: a famous, cautious grandee holding the strongest army, who distrusts the leader. He objects or refuses (P2, P6).
- `n_appointee`: a political appointee, uncandid and far away by courier. He says yes and slow-walks (P4, P8).
- `n_fighter`: an aggressive junior with no post, waiting for a command (P7 promises).
- `n_war_office`: an office holding the reserve, for transfers (P1).
- Blocs `n_hawks`, `n_moderates` and `n_machine`, with a midterm mandate test on day 120 (P10).

## The v2 graph

Thirty regions (16 land, 6 key points including an island fort, 5 river and sea regions, 3 off-map boxes) and 51 edges (31 road, 6 rail, 6 river, 8 sea). It carries what the planner spike (lane 0.9) needs, and `test/world-v2.test.ts` fails if any of it is edited away:

- **A direct road and a flank route.** Westwatch to Southvale is 8 days through the Harrow salient and Corran Crossing, and 11 days by the Wending Valley and Highcairn (37% longer).
- **Limited rail.** The Brenn and Westwatch Branch has capacity 1 and a break of gauge at Brenn Junction; every rail edge belongs to a named line, and the trunk line's president holds a post.
- **A river and a fort.** The Mere is navigable from the Wending Valley to Gull Sound, upstream slower than downstream, with low water in autumn. Corran Fort commands it and must be reduced; it obstructs the river at the start.
- **Two posts around a salient.** The Armies of the Westwatch and the Eastwatch stand either side of the Harrow Bulge, which the South holds.
- **Depots.** A forward depot at Brenn Junction and supply sources at both capitals and Kestrel Haven.
- **A joint relief by sea.** Gull Rock, a northern fort in southern waters, is reachable only by sea from the naval base at Kestrel Haven. The relief is two temporary posts (troops under the War Ministry, ships under the Admiralty) linked as one joint expedition, with troops afloat aboard a ship.
- **The navy notes and 0040.** A river flotilla that passes from the War Ministry to the Admiralty on a date; standing orders and a local truce on posts; a detachment below its brigade; labourers who eat a garrison's rations; provincial militia serving under another department's general; a runners' port off the map.

Two starts: `opening` (April, before the shooting war) and `summer_front` (a year later, the salient gone and Corran Fort under siege).

## Design parameters in `pack.json`'s rules (0064)

These rules are design parameters, chosen for play and not sourced facts. Every pack states them, and the kernel has no default for any of them. The values here are the ones lane 0.5 tuned.

| Rule | Value | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `stores.menPerCapacityUnit` | 20000 | Men, and non-combatants, that one unit of the capacity scale feeds for a day |
| `stores.capDays` | 30 | The most days of stores a block refills to |
| `stores.lowDays` | 7 | Stores below this many days are low: a truth event a report may tell |
| `occupation.occupyPermille` | 100 | Control a side alone in held ground gains a day, at full strength |
| `occupation.occupyFullMen` | 5000 | The men at which occupation reaches full rate; fewer gain in proportion |
| `combat` | (a group) | The battle model (lane 1.3): base K+W rates per battle type, works thresholds, frontage and a loss term per terrain, the additive terms' weights and caps, commits, killed and missing shares, the flank break, morale and the rout, experience, equipment, the commanders' decision shares, the battle's day cap, field control, stragglers' return, commanders hit, and the day shape's phases. The combat note's starting values, inside Livermore's and Fox's bands; `packs/union-1861/test/livermore.test.ts` runs them against Livermore's tables |
| `movement.marchMinCapacityPermille` | 1000 | The least capacity an edge needs this month to carry a march: one whole unit |
| `movement.courierPermille` | 333 | A courier's days on an edge, as a share of a division's: about a third, rounded up |

### The planner and plans (lane 1.2, 0062)

Design parameters, stated by every pack with no kernel default (0072). The planner's thresholds start from lane 0.9's spike; the weights are its scorer's, to be calibrated with compliance (FOUNDATION section 12).

| Rule | Value | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `planner.dailyDraftCap` | 3 | Plans drafted in one day, oldest request first; the rest wait (lane 0.8 measured the worst day on the Union graph) |
| `planner.assaultMargin` | 1500 | His demand for an assault on held ground, permille of his demand in the open (lane 0.9 finding 1) |
| `planner.shortfallTimid`, `shortfallBold` | 10, 50 | The shortfall, permille of the men he needs, past which he asks for men and waits: 1% for a man of boldness 0, 5% for one of 1000 (finding 8) |
| `planner.assaultDays`, `reducePerLevel` | 1000, 10 | Days on enemy-held ground on a route: an assault where garrisoned, a reduction where fortified (finding 9) |
| `planner.partnerHonours`, `partnerOffends` | `duty`; `caution` | Values a cooperating axis honours or offends in the partner asked; with `partnerRegardWeight` 300 and `partnerValueWeight` 100, his willingness (finding 7) |
| `planner.flank*`, `dedupe*`, `topN`, `maxSearches` | as the spike | The flank's overlap (600) and length (1600) limits, deduplication, four moving candidates and a holding action, 64 searches a request (0062) |
| `planner.weights` | 24 numbers | The profile (risk weight, supply care, concentration, axes) and the score terms' weights and divisors |
| `plans.goalDefaults` | risk 500, 20 days, rail only, approve as drafted | The goal terms an order carries when it names only a posture and an objective (a situation's option, the generic principal) |
| `plans.compliance*` | 800, 500, 500, 500, 500 | STAND-IN until the people recast: the candour, standing, resources, oversight and credibility compliance reads when a commander answers an edit |
