# Testbed pack: the Two Rivers

This is a fictional, setting-free two-faction war used to exercise the kernel. It belongs to no real history, so it can be changed freely to provoke mechanisms.

## Two formats, side by side

- `scenario.json` is the **M2** scenario that today's kernel plays (`npm run run:testbed`). It stays until lanes 0.4 and 0.5 replace the M2 theatre.
- Everything else is **world format v2** (lane 0.2; `notes/lane-0.2-world-format.md`): `pack.json`, `PROVENANCE.json`, `strings/en.json` and the tables under `source/` are hand-written; `data/` is generated by `npm run pack:generate -- testbed`, and `npm test` fails when it is stale.

## The M2 cast

The cast was chosen so the first turns exercise each M1 mechanism:

- `n_organiser`: a famous, cautious grandee holding the strongest army, who distrusts the leader. He objects or refuses (P2, P6).
- `n_appointee`: a political appointee, uncandid and far away by courier. He says yes and slow-walks (P4, P8).
- `n_fighter`: an aggressive junior with no post, waiting for a command (P7 promises).
- `n_war_office`: an office holding the reserve, for transfers (P1).
- Blocs `n_hawks`, `n_moderates` and `n_machine`, with a midterm mandate test on day 120 (P10).

## The v2 graph

Thirty regions (16 land, 6 key points including an island fort, 5 river and sea regions, 3 off-map boxes) and 51 edges (31 road, 6 rail, 6 river, 8 sea). It carries what the planner spike (lane 0.9) needs, and `test/world-v2.test.ts` fails if any of it is edited away:

- **A direct road and a flank route.** Westwatch to Southvale is 8 days through the Harrow salient and Corran Crossing, and 11 days by the Wending Valley and Highcairn (37% longer).
- **Limited rail.** The Brenn and Westwatch Branch has capacity 1 and a break of gauge at Brenn Junction; every rail edge belongs to a named line, and the trunk line's president holds a post.
- **A river and a fort.** The Mere is navigable from the Wending Valley to Gull Sound, upstream slower than downstream, with low water in autumn. Corran Fort commands it and must be reduced; it obstructs the river at the start.
- **Two posts around a salient.** The Armies of the Westwatch and the Eastwatch stand either side of the Harrow Bulge, which the South holds.
- **Depots.** A forward depot at Brenn Junction and supply sources at both capitals and Kestrel Haven.
- **A joint relief by sea.** Gull Rock, a northern fort in southern waters, is reachable only by sea from the naval base at Kestrel Haven. The relief is two temporary posts (troops under the War Ministry, ships under the Admiralty) linked as one joint expedition, with troops afloat aboard a ship.
- **The navy notes and 0040.** A river flotilla that passes from the War Ministry to the Admiralty on a date; standing orders and a local truce on posts; a detachment below its brigade; labourers who eat a garrison's rations; provincial militia serving under another department's general; a runners' port off the map.

Two starts: `opening` (April, before the shooting war) and `summer_front` (a year later, the salient gone and Corran Fort under siege).
