# S13 readings and limits

Research evidence is retained outside every worktree at `C:/Pyrrhic/mandate-research-sources/S13/`. Individual citations are in the situation JSON, with source declarations in `sources.json`. Readings below are selected passages, not a claim to have read entire volumes. Cached sources in S4–S7 were read only; the S13 extracts and downloaded copies are retained separately.

| Official Naval Records I-6 | 233–234 | Welles’s appointment and proposed division; actual assumption remains separate. |
| Treasury report for 1862 | 7, 9; also selected 12–15 and appendix material during the search | Chase’s later explanation of the 1861 suspension. It is not evidence that his December 1862 report reached Lincoln in December 1861. |
| Statutes at Large XII | 589–592, 597–600, 627, 1264–1265, 1267–1268 | Confiscation, militia service and unequal pay, explanatory resolution, Hunter revocation and preliminary proclamation. No January 1863 final proclamation is authored. |
| Welles, *Diary*, I (1911) | 61–63; 78–81; 94–99; 142–144; 157–158 | 61–63 is a retrospective opening account. 78–81 is August material, excluded as evidence for the May Norfolk visit. Welles is an interested participant; his judgments are attributed. |
| Carpenter, *Six Months at the White House* (1866) | 20–23 | Later reported conversation; Lincoln could not remember the July date. Its September chronology differs from Welles and the proclamation. No verbatim July cabinet minute is claimed. |
| Mahan, *The Gulf and Inland Waters* (1883) | 21, selected 94–108 | Secondary retrospective account. Page 21 says July transfer, p. 107 says 1 October; the underlying transfer instrument is still needed. No Welles instruction is inferred from the account of withdrawal on 104–105. |
| Haupt, *Reminiscences* (1901) | 80, 83, 86–87 | Memoir and reproduced wires. Pages 81–82 are illustrations. Haupt’s claimed effect on reinforcements is not independently established. |
| Senate Executive Document 8, 37th Congress, second session (1862) | 5, 10–13, selected passages | Seward’s 26 December reply. The earlier provisional British Parliamentary locator was rejected. |
| *Richmond Daily Dispatch*, 9 December 1861 | p. 2, “Latest Northern”, report passage and “The suppressed Dispatch” | Richmond reprint of a New York *Tribune* account; read full relevant article, not merely a search snippet. Neither an official instruction nor a cabinet transcript. The surrounding paper is Confederate partisan reporting. |
| *Congressional Record*, 6 January 1886 | p. 456 (PDF 15), reproduced 1 November 1862 Cooke circular | Later reproduction of a dated notice. It establishes the agent’s announcement, not a precise appointment day or commission. |

The initial OCR filenames were not always image leaf numbers. Printed headers and the actual text corrected the locators. Superseded search extracts, failed downloads and illustrative pages remain external but are not evidence for the corrected claims. The final evidence inventory will distinguish them. Historical source signatures are not copied into draft review fields.
