# Pack: the Union, 1861–62 (decision 0004)

## Two formats, side by side

- **World format v2 (lane 0.3):** `pack.json`, `PROVENANCE.json`, `strings/en.json`, `source/world/` and `source/scenarios/march_1861/` are the data of record; `data/` is generated from them (`npm run pack:generate -- union-1861`; `npm test` fails when it is stale). One start, 5 March 1861; the pack is a `draft`, so values no source gives are `unknown`. `source/drafts/` holds the three interim drafts the import started from, kept as the archive (columns the format has no field for, such as the people's prewar careers, stay there). `source/import/` is the one-time conversion record, not a build step: from the merge on the source CSVs are the source of truth, and re-running the importer would overwrite hand edits (it refuses to run without `UNION1861_REPRODUCE_IMPORT=1`). Read `notes/lane-0.3-union-data.md` for every rule and judgement.
- **The M2 pack below** (`scenario.json`, `source/generate.mjs`, `test/union-pack.test.ts`) is what today's kernel plays, from 1 August 1861. It stays until lanes 0.4 and 0.5 replace the M2 theatre.

## The M2 pack

You are Abraham Lincoln, from 1 August 1861 (day 0, after First Bull Run) to 31 December 1862 (day 517). Every army, department, ministry and governorship is held by someone else.

```bash
npm run play -- union-1861       # play in the text console
npm run run:pack -- union-1861 75  # watch the generic scripted leader play it
```

## What's in it

- **27 people.** The Union cast is Lincoln, Scott, McClellan, Halleck, Frémont, Butler, Banks, McDowell, Pope, Burnside, Grant, Buell, Stanton, Cameron, Seward, Chase and Governors Curtin, Andrew and Morton. The Confederate cast is Davis, J. E. Johnston, Beauregard, Lee, Jackson, A. S. Johnston, Bragg and Longstreet.
- **19 posts:**
  - field armies and departments, which hold the troops;
  - the General-in-Chief, War, Treasury and State;
  - three governorships and two war departments, which raise regiments weekly that stay put until you order them moved.
- **10 blocs.** Union: Radical Republicans, Moderate Republicans, War Democrats, Peace Democrats, Border-state Unionists, Abolitionists, and the army. Confederate: planter establishment, fire-eaters, states' governors.
- **The 1862 midterm elections** as a mandate test on day 440 (threshold 100; the scenario starts at about 245).
- **37 coarse regions** with land, river and sea-lane adjacency. Washington and Richmond are fortified.
- **Telegraph latency:** Washington-area commands 0 days; Fort Monroe, the West and the governors 1 day; the Gulf 2 days.

## Sources

Everything comes from [`docs/research/2026-11-14-union-1861-62.md`](../../docs/research/2026-11-14-union-1861-62.md), which cites each fact. **Every trait number is our interpretation**, anchored in a cited behaviour. For example, McClellan's candour of 200 rests on his inflated enemy estimates and his private letters calling Lincoln "the original gorilla". Many citations come from search summaries and are marked for spot-checking in the dossier.

The scenario is generated by `source/generate.mjs`. Edit that, not the JSON.

## Deliberate simplifications

- **Compressed appointments:**
  - Buell holds the Department of the Ohio from day 0 (historically from November 1861).
  - Jackson holds the Valley District from day 0 (from November 1861).
  - Lee holds the Confederate War Department as Davis's adviser (from March 1862; in August 1861 he was in western Virginia).
  - Bragg holds a Gulf-coast department at New Orleans (historically at Pensacola).
- Butler starts at Fort Monroe, as in August 1861. The New Orleans command and its one-to-two-week sea latency appear only if you send him there.
- Force numbers are in thousands, and several are estimates the dossier marks as unsourced (Frémont, Buell, Banks, Butler).
- Kentucky starts as Union-held (its legislature turned Union in September 1861). Southeast and southwest Missouri start Confederate.
- Senators (Wade, Sumner) and the Joint Committee on the Conduct of the War are represented through the Radical bloc, not as people.
- Emancipation is not a decision yet. Policy decisions need the event system (see `docs/design/KERNEL.md`).

## Calibration status

The pack is uncalibrated. With the generic scripted leader, 75 turns end on day 517 with the Union holding 27 of 37 regions, which is more than history. Washington needed its fortifications to survive an aggressive scripted Lincoln.

## Design parameters in `pack.json`'s rules (0064)

These rules are design parameters, chosen for play and not sourced facts. Every pack states them, and the kernel has no default for any of them. The values here are the ones lane 0.5 tuned.

| Rule | Value | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `stores.menPerCapacityUnit` | 20000 | Men, and non-combatants, that one unit of the capacity scale feeds for a day |
| `stores.capDays` | 30 | The most days of stores a block refills to |
| `stores.lowDays` | 7 | Stores below this many days are low: a truth event a report may tell |
| `contest.occupyPermille` | 100 | Control a side alone in held ground gains a day, at full strength |
| `contest.occupyFullMen` | 5000 | The men at which occupation reaches full rate; fewer gain in proportion |
| `contest.contestPermille` | 200 | Control the stronger side gains a day, times its margin over the next |
| `movement.marchMinCapacityPermille` | 1000 | The least capacity an edge needs this month to carry a march: one whole unit |
| `movement.courierPermille` | 333 | A courier's days on an edge, as a share of a division's: about a third, rounded up |
