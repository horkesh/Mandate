# 0037: Commercial, but prove the hook before spending

Status: accepted (2026-11-14). The owner chose "Commercial, prove it first" over "all in" and "passion project", on the lead's recommendation.

## Context

- The games survey's reading of Steam review counts suggests a small market for Civil War grand strategy (Ultimate General: Civil War about 5,700 reviews, Grand Tactician about 2,000, the 2024 re-release of No Greater Glory 11). That is a proxy, not market research.
- The game's hook is not the setting but its people: generals who lie, argue and run against you. It might reach beyond wargamers, but that is unproven.

## Decision

- *By Order of the President* is a commercial product, run on a **prove-it-first** budget:
  - **No art or marketing money before the hook is proven.** The procedural staff map (0013), a text-led desk, public-domain portraits and open-licence typefaces (0035) carry the game until then.
  - **Demand is tested early and cheaply:** a Steam page and a short playable demo of the audience and plan review, as soon as there is something playable (Phase 1's console loop at the earliest, Phase 2's desk more likely). Wishlists are the go or no-go signal.
  - **A market-research lane runs before any money is spent on art or marketing:** comparable games' sales estimates and wishlist conversion, and which games reached past their niche with political or character-driven play.
- Spending decisions remain the owner's (CLAUDE.md: money is outside the lead's authority).

Rejected: all in now (accepts the niche risk before the hook is tested); passion project (the owner wants a product).

## Consequences

- Phase 2's done check gains a demo-ready build of the audience and plan review.
- The market-research note informs the go or no-go before art and marketing spend.
