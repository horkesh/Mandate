# 0112: The pushback registers, and what 1A requires of them

Status: accepted (2026-11-23). From S88's registers and proposal (`docs/research/2026-11-23-S88-pushback-registers-and-coverage.md` and `docs/research/S88/` on `sprint/pushback` at `ef689569`), under 0111. Decided by the lead under the working style in `CLAUDE.md`; the owner can overturn it. Amends 0099.

## Context

- **The registers.** 0111 made pushback a matter of covering a register of named acts. S88 built both registers under its unit (side, actor, role, matter, act):
  - a Union register of 47 candidate rows;
  - a Confederate register of 27 candidate rows, from correspondence read for 5 March to 31 July 1861.
- **The catalogue.** All 41 Appendix C rows now have a disposition. Harney's agreement is excluded, because 21 May precedes the presidential direction of 27 May (OR I-III pp.374–376). The Sumter cabinet answers are individual signed acts (Nicolay and Hay, *Complete Works* II pp.11–22). N3-06 and N3-07 keep their exclusions under 0109 and 0111.
- **What S88 proposes:**
  - 33 eligible obligations: Union 2 reservations, 2 slow walks and 14 plan objections; Confederate 12 reservations and 3 plan objections;
  - none for relay objections, relay holds, filter holds or trims;
  - three further plan objections held back as source-qualified: Welles and Bates on the blockade, and Toombs on Sumter, all read only in Burlingame;
  - each obligation is covered by one matching model act, by actor, role and matter.
- **S82's reference scores** 1 match (Morgan's April call), 8 mismatches, 9 unresolved, 4 exposure mismatches and 11 not observed.
  - **Unresolved:** eight of the nine are cabinet advice on Sumter. The model records that a member took part in a consultation, not what he advised. The ninth is Pickens's organisation terms.
  - **Exposure mismatches:** the model never issued the order the act answered. Patterson got hold goals rather than the recorded attack-or-demonstrate directions, and Johnston got "take Manassas" rather than the June garrison question.

## Decision

1. **The registers are accepted as S88 classified them:** 33 eligible rows, its exclusions, and its nearest-type interpretations, Morgan's included. The three source-qualified rows stay out until their own sources are read.
2. **The obligation is each eligible row once.** A model act covers at most one row, and only for the same actor, role and matter. There is no fungible total.
3. **The 1A requirement covers only what the model can represent.**
   - Rows whose matter the model doesn't yet represent are reported as **representation gaps**, each naming the mechanism that would carry it. Examples: the content of cabinet advice, a demonstrate-or-attack direction, organisation terms in a transfer.
   - A representation gap is neither a pass nor a pushback failure.
   - Every other eligible row must be matched for the pushback criterion to pass. A mismatch, an exposure mismatch the model's own orders could have avoided, or a row not observed is a failure, reported with its cause.
4. **No excess ceiling yet.** A ceiling on unregistered pushback needs a historical denominator and its own ruling. The raw counts by side, actor, role, kind and rung are published beside the score and are never compared with it.
5. **The old count bands go when the coverage scorer arrives.** The bands of 0099 item 6, as amended by 0109 and 0111, stay binding until the coverage scorer and its diagnostics are built. They then retire for the pushback criterion.

Rejected:
- **A fraction of the rows as the pass mark.** Any fraction is arbitrary, and it lets the easy rows pay for the hard ones.
- **Counting representation gaps as failures now.** It would fail 1A for mechanisms nobody has built, and hide which parts of the people model are actually wrong.
- **Counting representation gaps as passes.** It would claim behaviour the model doesn't have.
- **Admitting the three qualified rows on Burlingame's account alone.** Their cited letters and diaries haven't been read.
- **Keeping the count bands beside the coverage test.** They measure a different population (0111).

## Consequences

- **The build.** A request builds the coverage scorer, with the representation-gap classification and the raw diagnostics, against the two registers. It retires the count bands for pushback and reports the new criterion on the current July branch.
- **The gaps become a list of mechanisms,** each a candidate for later work, starting with the content of cabinet advice for the Sumter consultations.
- **Mismatches are findings about the people model.** The eight are governors answering against the record. They go to diagnosis alongside S89's work on governors whose judgments are unknown, never to trait fitting.
