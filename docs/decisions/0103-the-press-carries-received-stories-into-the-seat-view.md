## Decision

1. **The editor receives an ordinary public closing account.** Battle reporting sends the result and named commander to the editor through the existing wire/courier route. When the force cannot send a return, the principal's staff authors the public account from the field, without casualty figures. Printing reads the editor's own received reports in the interval after the previous printing day and through the present printing day, excluding reports written later. It does not read all seats' knowledge or the true closed-battle records. Late arrival can defer the story and its political effects to the next issue. Sources: the lead's R54 item 1; “R54 / Proposed rulings” and its opening paragraph; `packages/kernel/src/politics/press.ts / closedBattles` and `tick/battle-reports.ts` at the pinned head; R63 P1/P2.

2. **Battle identity and the named commander travel in immutable claims.** Optional `ReportClaim.claimBattle`, `claimBattleDays` and `claimCommander` carry identity, covered days and the person named by the source. The view projects them as `battle`, `battleDays` and `commander`, without asking today's truth who commanded. The body is carried by `messages` in transit and held by `knowledge` on receipt; `ReportClaim` remains an immutable registered record. FOUNDATION section 10 records the extension. No root State field or pack rule is added, and rendered prose is not a new source of truth. Sources: “R54,” its metadata paragraph and proposed ruling; `knowledge/report.ts` and `view/view.ts`; the lead's R54 write-scope ownership requirement and R71 item 4.

3. **Figures come only from the principal's held returns by printing day.** The editor's public account supplies the result and commander; its figures are assembled from its side's principal's casualty returns for the same battle and covered days. For each day, prefer a received full return, otherwise the first return. Both written and received dates must be no later than printing. Enemy-loss claims never supply own losses. Identity prevents two battles at the same place, even on the same covered day, sharing figures. A printed page is a received record and does not change when later returns arrive. Sources: “The press's figures from the returns,” the lead's ruling on `0ffcc56`; “R54,” opening paragraph; `returnedLosses`; R63 P3, whose two battles share region and day; R71 item 4.

4. **A missing return does not erase a surrender or invent a figure.** A received closing result still prints with confidence 0 and no displayed figure when there is no return. A first return gives confidence 500; full returns for every covered day give 900. Captured men count among the missing at first and print separately only once every day's full return has arrived. A force taken whole may send no return at all; its received result still says lost. Sources: “The press's figures from the returns / The gate reviewer's two conditions”; 0084's inherited G-1.6a/b account; `frontPage` and `RETURNED_CONFIDENCE` at the pinned head. The result is the public reading of who left the field, including surrender or destruction, not a leak of exact battle losses.

5. **The page remains a report route, with existing owners for its effects.** The editor prints on the pack's cadence from received accounts. Each battle's result moves the side's blocs through `politics` and the named commander's standing through `people`, scaled by the paper's existing reach. The page still travels by ordinary mail to its side's principal and serving post holders. An editor no longer serving on that side is silent. Sources: 0084; the hand-over's “The press,” “Choices made,” item 5, and “R54”; `politics/press.ts` and `tick/politics.ts`. R54 changes the input route to received stories, not the recipient policy, cadence or number of papers. Enemy readership and editorial slant remain later work.

6. **Both desks render received stories from pack strings.** CLI and browser use headlines and short copy from the twelve added generic strings per pack. The week's papers lists each press story with source and written date and opens its received paper. Battle dispatches group by battle ID and link the latest received dispatch in written order, rather than making one line per phase. Legacy fixtures without identity use place/day as a presentation fallback; that cannot reliably identify one battle across days and is not the rule for matching live returns. Sources: the lead's R54 items 2–3; “R54,” presenter paragraph; its adapter/story/summary checks. Zero-confidence copy omits the figure, and separate captured claims remain associated with their battle.

7. **CI typechecks the browser desk on both platforms.** The root command `typecheck:desk` runs the separate desk's typecheck. `.github/workflows/ci.yml` installs its dependency tree and runs that command on Ubuntu and Windows. This closes the type-boundary gap without adding a separate dependency install to every staged-hook snapshot, which currently supplies root dependencies only. Sources: the lead's R54 item 4; “R54 / Proposed rulings” and CI verification paragraph; the pinned root `package.json` and CI workflow. CI passed on `f8f74da`.

8. **Keep the two presenters equal without widening source serving.** R63 retains the byte-identical local CLI/desk `press.ts` modules and adds a test of their actual bytes. The desk server deliberately denies sibling `packages/` sources; a shared import would require changing that boundary. A deliberate divergence fails the equality test, alongside the existing rendered-story equivalence test. Source: the lead's R63 item 3 and “R63,” presenter paragraph. This records the authorised equality fallback, not a new server-access exception.

9. **The press must fit the existing tick budget with behavior preserved.** R68 requires a fair back-to-back Union at-war 75-day comparison, a branch median within 5% of main, timing-enabled tests, the full no-git check and the done check. The 40 ms median / 160 ms p95 limits remain. Daily/reference hashes, save fixture, bands and report behavior must not be changed to buy speed. Sources: the lead's R68 items 1–5; “R68 / Measurement and diagnosis” and “Verification and preservation”; R71 item 4.

   The built change gives ordinary projected claims without battle metadata a direct frozen-object path, avoiding three conditional empty spread operands. Metadata claims keep their previous projection. The regression covers all 16 optional-field combinations, presence and order of keys, null commanders, copied frozen day arrays and untouched sources. There is no change to messages, receipts, return matching, page selection or State. Source: “R68,” opening paragraph; the pinned `view/view.ts` and projection regression.

The median of the three medians/p95s is main **32.6303/68.5064 ms**, branch **31.6048/68.1612 ms**: the branch median is 3.14% below main, meeting the 5% target. Rejected:

- Printing from true closed-battle records, every seat's reports, or a report not yet received by printing day. Sources: R54's route ruling and R63 P1/P2; each deliberate re-break fails its targeted test.
- True losses delayed by a fixed lag, a paper's invented estimate in 1A, or figures matched only by place/day. Sources: the earlier returns ruling, R54 and R63 P3.
- Suppressing a no-return surrender, displaying a numeric zero as a known figure, or splitting captured men before every full return arrives. Sources: the retained gate conditions and R54 presenter behavior.
- Storing presentation prose as truth, adding another State owner or replacing the source's named commander with today's holder. Source: R54 proposed metadata ruling.
- Installing the separate desk tree in each staged snapshot, or widening the development server to share a presenter module. Sources: R54's CI choice and R63's equality fallback.
- Reducing mail, changing behavior, bands or time limits to satisfy timing; indexing return scans without sampled evidence; or treating local timing as proof of Linux CI success. Sources: the lead's R68 constraints and its measured diagnosis.

## Consequences

The seat view can display what a paper actually printed while preserving separate knowledge sources for the public result and casualty figures. The immutable page does not improve retroactively. Press effects can wait for ordinary editor receipt, and the UI's story links point to held sources.

R63's three regressions each fail on its intended re-break: flattening seats, admitting a report after printing, and cross-matching two battles at one place/day. Its Git-writing LFS fixtures were also moved to a separate file excluded only by `MANDATE_NO_GIT=1`; ordinary CI/hook collection retains them. That is supporting test hygiene, not a new press rule. R68 preserves all 195 recorded daily hashes, reference summaries, the save and 153 mechanics bands per pack. Its eight view snapshots retain 968 successful matching hashes and 136 identical inherited errors; the errors are not successful view coverage. Sources: “R63” and “R68 / Verification and preservation.”

The lead's later receipt supplies the branch CI and standing approval. The merge-with-main checks, inherited view failures, optional map-data checks and full 1A calibration retain their owners. The record neither promises a broader speedup nor treats the unfinished checks as waived.

Ruling source gaps: none. R54's receipt/identity/CI choices, the inherited held-return rule, R63's authorised fallback and R68's budget/preservation target are all sourced. The branch CI figure and approval are explicitly attributed to the later lead receipt, not represented as R71 measurements.
