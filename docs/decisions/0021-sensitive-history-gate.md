# 0021: A sensitive-history gate; no Lost Cause framing; enslaved people as agents

Status: accepted (2026-11-14). AWWV's founding brief for this project recommends a gate from day one and states, "Recommended default, which I confirm: no Lost Cause framing", which the owner forwarded as guidance. The representation of enslaved people is the lead's recommendation, open to the owner's review like every record. Amended by 0026 (wider scope, placement rule, Black regiments, a playable Confederacy needs its own record).

## Context

The Civil War was fought over slavery. A game about it can launder that cause through its text or its mechanics: by treating enslaved people as a resource, by making the Confederacy's cause a neutral "states' rights" abstraction, or by staying silent about massacres of surrendered Black soldiers, guerrilla war, civilians and prison camps.

## Decision

- **No Lost Cause framing.** Neither text nor mechanics may present the war's cause as anything other than slavery, or present slavery as benign.
- **Enslaved people are agents of their own freedom**, never a resource either side manages: self-emancipation toward Union lines, the effect of Union policy (the confiscation acts, the Emancipation Proclamation), and Black regiments.
- **The gate covers:** slavery and emancipation, Black soldiers and their treatment, massacres of surrendered soldiers, guerrilla war, treatment of civilians, prison camps.
- Any change touching the gate gets a second, separate reviewer lane for the gate (`docs/LANES.md`) in addition to the normal reviewer, and its historical claims come from a research lane with sources.

Rejected: leaving the topic out (that is itself a framing); a single reviewer for gate changes (AWWV needed independent seats for its equivalent gate).

## Consequences

- Situations and copy touching the gate are written and reviewed under it.
- A short gate checklist lives with the situations schema when it is built (Phase 0, the situations lane: lane 0.7 after the re-sequencing in 0026).
