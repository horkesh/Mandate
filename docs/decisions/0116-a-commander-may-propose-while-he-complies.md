# 0116: A commander may propose while he complies

Status: accepted (2026-11-23). From S96's build of 0114 (`notes/lane-s96-discretion.md` on `sprint/discretion` at `e89b03d0`) and S94's register (`docs/research/S94/` on `sprint/research-military` at `c7aa662d`). Decided by the lead under the working style in `CLAUDE.md`; the owner can overturn it. Amends 0114.

## Context

- **0114's trigger is too narrow.** 0114 item 1 lets the kernel draft a commander's proposal only when his response to the goal is an objection or a reservation.
- **Johnston proposed while he complied.**
  - His memorandum of 26 May proposed a mobile course "unless otherwise instructed" (OR I-II pp.880–881).
  - His letter of 6 June asked whether his force was a garrison or a corps of observation, and proposed employing it elsewhere once delay was no longer prudent (pp.907–908).
  - He was doing what he was told, and saying what he thought of it.
- **In S96's build Johnston complies fully with the hold** (willingness 187), so no proposal is drafted. His received picture in June also shows no enemy in the regions he watches, so nothing in his own knowledge would make him doubt the position. The second is the larger gap. The record's threats were Patterson's advance to Williamsport and the raid on Romney in mid-June (OR I-II pp.470–472), and the model doesn't yet produce them by their causes.

## Decision

1. **A proposal may accompany compliance.**
   - As well as on an objection or a reservation (0114 item 1), the kernel drafts a commander's proposal when he complies with a goal but his own reading of his position meets one of 0114's conditions, `turned` or `outmatched`, and his planner's best candidate differs from the ordered goal.
   - He carries out the goal meanwhile. The proposal travels as an ordinary paper, correlated with the order.
2. **The reading is his own.** The condition is judged on his own received knowledge, as 0114 requires, never on the truth.
3. **Everything else in 0114 stands:** what a proposal carries, the principal's three answers, receipt before authority, and the condition vocabulary.

Rejected:
- **Proposals at any difference of opinion.** It would flood the principal with papers, and every proposal must have a cause the commander can name.
- **Giving Johnston knowledge of threats the model doesn't produce.** The threats must come from the Union commanders' own sourced orders and moves, never from injected reports.

## Consequences

- **The build adds the trigger** with paired tests: the same commander complying, with and without a believed threat to his position.
- **The threats come from research and doctrine.** Patterson's June advance and the Romney raid come from Scott's and Patterson's sourced June orders, as doctrine rows gated on received papers. Johnston's picture then follows from ordinary reporting.
