## Decision

1. **D1, the grounds for an undertaking: research first, by one rule for both sides.**
   - A research lane establishes the following, from contemporary evidence dated before the scenario's start and under 0085 and 0087:
     - whether Confederate actors held a commitment to the Confederacy as a cause, distinct from `state_autonomy` and `slavery_defence`, as `union` is distinct on the Union side;
     - each Confederate actor's cell for it, row by row, unknown where the evidence is silent;
     - Letcher's cells.
   - **If the evidence supports the value:**
     - The vocabulary gains it.
     - Every Confederate call profile is recoded by the rule the Union profiles already follow: a call for troops honours its side's war purpose unless its own text says otherwise.
     - The rule applies by kind of call, never by register row.
   - **The result is measured, not promised.** If the evidence doesn't support the value, C10 and C11 stay mismatches, and 1A's pushback criterion fails for that named cause.
   - **A judgment that changes during play stays parked.** There is no date-triggered change to a person's values. A general, caused mechanism is a later design question.
2. **D2, advice: approved.** One protocol addresses advice to a course and a round.
   - A consultation round has its own identity, and each adviser's answer names the course and the round it answers.
   - **A feasibility inquiry** is a subcase. Its answer carries a typed payload, grounded in what the adviser has received: what the course needs, what he knows is available, and by when.
   - Existing stances and memoranda (0052) stay.
   - Content is generated from the adviser's own stance and knowledge, never from a table of historical answers.
   - The round's question travels as an authored letter under 0121, once that identity exists.
3. **D3, gates and offers: approved, after M2.**
   - **A gate** names one stage of an undertaking and one prerequisite. It opens on the actor's own receipt of that prerequisite (0121's receipt witness), never on a date or on the truth.
   - **An offer** names the clauses it changes and the party whose acceptance it waits for. The changed undertaking starts only when the offerer receives that acceptance.
   - An unsolicited offer may accompany a willing undertaking of the original request (C12).
   - **Each stage keeps its own owner.** Recruitment, transfer, enlistment and command each run their own stage, and their writers are unchanged. A reservation refers to their events and holds no copy of their state.
   - Recording an offer earns no match. The existing selector of alternatives is not the offer protocol.
4. **D4, operational goal forms: approved in principle, built one at a time.**
   - **The four forms:** attack or demonstrate; a change of depot; interception at a dock; withdrawal conditioned on a landing.
   - **Each form is a typed goal given to a person.** It has its own tests of beliefs, authority and execution, and its own design note to the lead before any code. Attack-or-demonstrate comes first.
   - **Limits:**
     - there is no open language of conditions;
     - a demonstration isn't a date;
     - an interception at a dock isn't an island assault;
     - a change of depot isn't a free change of objective.
5. **D5, missing originals: recover them first.**
   - A row whose incoming paper or identity is missing waits for archival recovery. That covers C04, C09, C14 and C16's further clauses.
   - This record allows no reconstruction: no substituted source, no issuer's terms asserted from a reply, no invented target.
   - If recovery fails, the row stays as it is. A later record may allow a reconstruction bounded by the reply, with its unknowns kept.
6. **D6, civil and naval matters: parked to 1B.** U25a, U25b and U40 stay representation gaps in 1A. A ship isn't an infantry block, and a mayor isn't a governor answering a call.
7. **D7, an unsolicited programme: approved, without authority.**
   - A person may send an unsolicited proposal, and the principal may answer it.
   - Acceptance grants no authority. Carrying the proposal out needs an order or a mandate (0121).
   - Recommendation, acceptance and execution stay three separate acts.
   - It is built after D2's advice papers.
8. **The order of the builds:**
   1. M5's exposure audit and M2's adapters, which cover seven binding rows;
   2. 0121's letter identity and receipt witnesses, alongside the first where the files allow;
   3. D1's research, alongside both;
   4. M1 (under D2), then M9 (under D7);
   5. M3 (under D3), then M4's forms one at a time (under D4);
   6. M10, the Union's June chain of cause, under 0121's design of authority.

   No matcher is loosened to accept a different request, or declared compliance alone.

Rejected:
- **Fitting C07, C10 or C11:** a `duty` cell, a regard for Davis, a new weight or a threshold.
- **Parking D1 outright.** It would leave three binding rows failing for a cause no lane is examining. The asymmetry in the call profiles is a question about the model, and it deserves an answer.
- **Adding a Confederate value without evidence for each person,** or adding it for the three rows only.
- **One undifferentiated consultation, or scripted objections.**
- **Credit for recording an offer.**
- **An open language of conditions, or demonstrations triggered by date.**
- **Reconstructing missing originals now.**
- **A naval subsystem for one row in 1A.**
- **Treating acceptance of a recommendation as delegated authority.**
