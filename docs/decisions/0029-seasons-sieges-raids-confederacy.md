# 0029: Seasons through commanders, sieges as operations, raids and insurgency, and a Confederacy on the same machinery

Status: accepted (2026-11-14). The owner chose the lead's recommendation on all four questions. Amended by 0041 (clarifications).

## Decision

**Seasons and weather work through commanders.**
- Mud, rivers in flood and winter change edge lengths, capacities and supply along the map graph (0019).
- Commanders want winter quarters. Ordering a winter campaign is a counter-order they resist, and weather appears among their stated reasons. Whether the roads really are impassable is something the player usually cannot check (0008).
- Weather is exogenous noise from a named seeded stream (0007); how a commander reacts to it is not.

**Fortifications and sieges are operations.**
- Fortification and entrenchment are region state with one writer.
- A siege is an operation run by a commander: it takes time, supply and patience, and his progress reports are shaded like any other. The player's decisions are whether to keep feeding it, whether to believe him, and whether to replace him.
- No step-by-step siege mechanics (approaches, mines).

**Raids and insurgency.**
- Raids are operations a commander can launch to damage rail and river edges (`infrastructure`, FOUNDATION section 10) or strike depots.
- Guerrilla war is a pressure on a region's loyalty and supply, not a unit the player moves.
- Under the sensitive-history gate (0021, 0026), atrocities are narrated and costly, never a lever that pays; each feature is placed before it is authored.

**The Confederacy runs on the same machinery** (0009, 0015).
- Davis is a principal whose generals push back on him: feuds, slow-walking, reinterpretation.
- Cotton diplomacy, blockade running and inflation run through the same economy and pressure clocks as the Union's.
- His rule seat plays to break Union will (0028).
- It is not playable yet: a playable Confederacy still needs its own record under the gate (0026).

Rejected: seasons as movement modifiers only, or ignored; fortification as a defence modifier only, or detailed siege mechanics; raids without guerrilla war, or both abstracted to attrition; a simplified Confederate opponent whose generals always obey, or a playable Confederacy now.

## Consequences

- Weather, fortification and raids are Phase 1 mechanics; the world format (Phase 0, lane 0.2) reserves their state fields and owners.
- Confederate people data (the dossier already has a Confederate cast) is needed for Phase 1's history run.
