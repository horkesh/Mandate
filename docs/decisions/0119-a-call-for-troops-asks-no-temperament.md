# 0119: A call for troops asks no temperament

Status: accepted (2026-11-23). From S107's proposal (`docs/research/2026-11-23-S107-call-boldness.md` and `docs/research/S107/` on `sprint/pushback` at `dadcd597`). Decided by the lead under the working style in `CLAUDE.md`; the owner can overturn it. Clarifies 0118.

## Context

- **What the temperament term measures.** It takes four tenths of the gap between an order's boldness and the replier's, as a preference mismatch: too rash, or too timid.
  - For a plan, it reads the requested course against the commander's own best plan (0062, 0075).
  - For a field goal, the pack gives advance 600 and hold 400.
  - Transfers and appointments already carry a null boldness, so being an order to a person doesn't by itself bring a temperament charge.
- **What calls get instead.** Every call takes boldness 500 from one pack-wide constant, which no call's text sets. That holds even under S102's per-call profiles.
- **What the calls and replies show.** S107 read the calls and the governors' replies on both sides (37 printed pages, OR IV-I and III-I):
  - the calls specify men, service, organisation, authority, destinations and means;
  - none of the replies objects to how bold a call was. They object to its terms, its means, its authority or its purpose.
- **What the term did.** Brown's answer to the call of 22 April (C02) has a temperament term of −118 and deterrence of +45, and nothing else. The term came from a constant compared with his drawn boldness.

## Decision

1. **A call whose undertaking is raising or sending troops has no temperament term.** No `too_rash` or `too_timid` ground comes from it either.
   - The rule applies by kind of request, on both sides, and to every reading of the call along its chain. It is never applied by name or by register membership.
   - The adapter records the term as inapplicable, never as 500 and never as the recipient's own number.
2. **Operational courses keep the term.** A field goal, a plan or an edit keeps its temperament term as now.
3. **Everything else is unchanged.** Sourced values, judgment on the evidence, structural conditions, dated received resource reports and requests for information stay as 0118's grounds. Unknown judgments stay unknown (0113). Every weight stays as it is.

Rejected:
- **Keeping calls at 500.** That is an unsupported mismatch with each person's draw.
- **Taking boldness from each call's text.** No independent scale exists. Coding urgency, quota or service term as boldness would hide the clause and charge it twice.
- **Setting a call's boldness to the recipient's own.** A paper's content can't depend on who reads it.
- **Removing the term everywhere.** Goals and plans have a real operational comparison.
- **Changing traits, weights or thresholds to fit the four rows.**

## Consequences

- **The build.** It marks call compliance as having no temperament term, with paired tests: the same governor under a call and under a field goal, and a governor of unknown boldness, whose result doesn't change.
- **The arithmetic, measured not promised.** On S102's recorded terms, removing the term makes these answers admissible:
  - C02 (45);
  - C03's absolute call (40);
  - C10's cavalry (22);
  - C11's extra regiments (13).

  The build measures the real result.
- **The reserve residual stays.** C10's reserve (−178) and C11's reserve (−187) still carry the −200 of an offended `state_autonomy` against the President's field appointments. In the record, both governors undertook the reserve while protesting. Whether the vocabulary lacks a positive term for a governor's commitment to his own government is its own question. The values vocabulary has `union` for one side and nothing equivalent for the other.
