# 0058: The format gaps lane 0.5 found: trait stand-ins, filters and intelligence chiefs, and each region's civil government

Status: accepted (2026-11-14). From lane 0.5, which found four things lane 0.6's stand-in comms World supplies, or 0055 needs, that the v2 World cannot carry:
- people's boldness, regard and values;
- which posts filter, and who is a post's intelligence chief;
- a faction's objective;
- which government holds each region, so that a neutral state's closing flag knows which regions it closes.

Decided by the lead under the working style in `CLAUDE.md`; the owner can overturn it. Amends 0050 and 0055.

## Decision

**Traits wait for the Phase 1 people recast.**
- `standin/traits.ts` stays until Phase 1 recasts the people core, as lane 0.6's stand-ins table says. Lane 0.5 deletes `standin/world.ts` and `from-scenario.ts`.
- On a v2 pack, a small adapter labelled as a stand-in feeds the trait rules:
  - **boldness** comes from the person's cited `nerve` centre: high 800, above 650, average 500, below 350, low 200, unknown 500;
  - **regard** starts at 0 (regard is State);
  - **values** are empty.
- The mapping is a design parameter, not a sourced fact.

**A faction's objective is the other faction's capital** in Phase 0, as the adapter already does. Goals replace it in Phase 1 (0016). No format change.

**Three format fields (amends 0050):**
- **`PostKindTerm.role`:** `filter`, `intelligence` or null. Pack vocabulary names the kinds, and the engine reads only the role, so no pack kind is named in code (hard rule 3).
- **`PostStart.intelligenceChief`:** a person id or null. The validator checks that the chief is on the post's side.
- **`governments` in the scenario start:** each land region's civil government on the start date, as a department, or `unknown`.
  - It becomes State owned by `command`, beside departments, and situations change it through proposals. Northwest Virginia's restored government is the model case.
  - It is jurisdiction, not ownership. Control stays with `control`, so 0050's rejection of "a department owning a region" stands.

**Neutral passage, made precise (amends 0055):**
- A land region that no side traces is passable to every side when its government is a no-faction department that is not closed.
- A region whose government is `unknown` is not neutral ground, and 0054's rule applies.
- The closing flag, `closedGovernments`, is State written by `command` and proposed by situations. It needs no format change.

**Who builds what:**
- Lane 0.7's session builds the three fields now, in parallel with lane 0.5. That covers the schemas, the validator, the published JSON Schemas and the testbed data.
- The same session fills the Union pack's `governments`:
  - each region gets its state's or territory's department, from the map draft's own region lists;
  - the Indian Territory nations are `unknown` until a research lane sources their governments in 1861;
  - post kind roles and intelligence chiefs stay null until research sources who filtered for whom.
- Lane 0.5 wires all three once that change merges. Until then it uses no stand-in for governments. Neutral passage lands with the field, because a stand-in that opened every uncontrolled region would also open the Indian Territory.

Rejected:
- **Values, regard or boldness as v2 person fields now.** Values for real people need cited research, and the Phase 1 people model decides their shape.
- **Leaving filters and chiefs dark on v2 packs until Phase 1.** Two small fields keep them live.
- **Letting the engine recognise filter kinds by name.** That breaks the pack boundary.
- **An objective field on `FactionStart`.** Goals replace objectives within a phase.
- **A region's government as World data.** Governments change in play: West Virginia, and the rival governments of Missouri and Kentucky.
- **Treating every uncontrolled region as neutral ground**, even as a stand-in. It opens regions no neutral government holds.

## Consequences

- **On v2 packs, relay objections and filter holds stay quiet** until values exist in Phase 1, and a delegate's choice by values (0057) falls back to its deterministic tie-break. Lane 0.8 sets its liveness bands to match.
- **Lane 0.5's done check** (Washington not in a pocket, and the Baltimore line open) depends on this format change merging first.
- **Research items:**
  - the Indian Territory nations' governments in 1861, and whether each declared neutrality;
  - the Union pack's filter posts and intelligence chiefs, from March 1861.
