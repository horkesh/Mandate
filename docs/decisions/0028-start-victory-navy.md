# 0028: The campaign starts at the inauguration; two national wills decide it; the navy works through people

Status: accepted (2026-11-14). The owner chose a March 1861 start (over the lead's recommendation of April 1861), "two wills" for victory, and the navy "through people". Amends 0018 and 0023.

## Context

- The Union pack started on 1 August 1861. Starting earlier means building the army from almost nothing, the state-building the owner enjoys, and the border states' choices from day one.
- Only the Union's defeat was defined (losing 1864, 0023, as amended by 0026).
- The navy had not been discussed beyond joint operations (0016).

## Decision

**Start.** The campaign starts at Lincoln's inauguration in March 1861.
- The secession winter and the Fort Sumter crisis are the first situations. Player choices shape *how* the war begins (who is seen to fire first, which border states secede, Northern opinion), not *whether*: the outbreak of war is the scenario's premise, and the one deliberate railroad, labelled as such in data and docs (AWWV's rule).
- Border states start with divided control and loyalty, which the permille control model (0019) represents directly.
- The facts of the secession winter and the Sumter crisis come from a research lane before any situation is authored.

**Victory: two wills.** Each side has a national will, derived from its blocs, losses, territory, armies and events.
- The Union wins when Confederate will breaks: armies destroyed or surrendered, key cities and rivers taken, the blockade's bite, emancipation's effects.
- The Confederacy wins by breaking Union will: losing the 1864 election (0023, 0026), or collapse before it.
- The verdict reports which will broke and why, with its contributing terms (hard rule 6).

**Navy through people.** The navy is posts and squadrons held by people (the Navy Department, flag officers). The player never moves ships.
- The blockade is a pressure clock on Confederate will and foreign patience, worked through those posts.
- River gunboats and fleets support joint operations along river and sea edges of the map graph (0019), as support requests with their own compliance (0016).

Rejected: April or August 1861 starts (the owner chose March); historical victory conditions or victory points by date (they reward map-painting over politics); detailed fleets (a second war to manage) or a fully abstract navy (no people to work through).

## Consequences

- The Union pack is rebuilt to start in March 1861; Phase 0's map lane uses the March 1861 situation.
- A research lane covers the secession winter, the Sumter crisis and the border states before situations are authored.
- National will is a Phase 1 politics mechanic; its terms are calibrated against named historical turning points as counts, not single cells.
