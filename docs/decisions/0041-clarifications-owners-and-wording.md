# 0041: Clarifications: owners for new state, business during a visit, and neutral option wording

Status: accepted (2026-11-14). Gaps found while folding 0040 into FOUNDATION, and by the combat-model, navy and first-month lanes. Amends 0025, 0029, 0030 and 0040.

## Decision

**Owners for new state** (hard rule 5; FOUNDATION's field-to-writer table):
- **Stances on record** (from Consult, 0040): State, written by `people`, proposed by Consult answers.
- **Adviser credibility** as the president sees it: Derived from his own knowledge (the advice reports he received and what followed), never stored.
- **Detachments** below the force block: written by `forces`, proposed by `command` and `operations`.
- **Stragglers** (men absent from a force block who rejoin over days, from the combat model): written by `forces`, proposed by `combat` and attrition.
- **Battle records**: written by `combat`.
- **Fortification and entrenchment** (0029): written by `regions`, proposed by operations and combat.

**Business during a visit** (amends 0025). While the president is away from Washington, business that reaches him goes to the holder he named as his delegate before leaving; with no delegate, it resolves to its `inaction` outcome (0040). The historical default is never used for the player.

**Neutral option wording** (amends 0040; from the first-month lane). Situation options are worded in the advisers' own terms and listed in a fixed order unrelated to the historical default, so labels cannot leak hindsight. This is part of lane 0.7's string-table rules and the gate checklist.

**0040 also amends 0030**: lane 0.9's planner spike adds a small joint naval relief case.

**Separate random streams** (from the combat model, under 0007 and 0020): generals killed or wounded in battle draw from their own named stream with a fixed number of draws per day; generated officers draw from a stream keyed by the report that names them, so reading a report cannot shift later draws.

## Consequences

- FOUNDATION's field-to-writer table gains these rows.
- Lane 0.7's brief includes the wording rule.
