# 0042: The inaugural address is the premise, not a choice

Status: accepted (2026-11-14). Raised by the first-month walkthrough (`docs/design/FIRST-MONTH.md`, Findings), which started after the inaugural and left its tone to the lead. The lead asked the owner twice without a recommendation; under the working style in `CLAUDE.md` the lead decides instead. The owner can overturn it. Clarifies 0028.

## Context

- The campaign starts at the inauguration on 4 March 1861 (0028). The address committed the government to "hold, occupy, and possess" federal property, ruled out invasion, and disclaimed any purpose to interfere with slavery in the states (`docs/research/2026-11-14-secession-winter-1861.md`).
- A choice of tone would shape the first weeks: the South's reading, the border states and Northern opinion.

## Decision

**The inaugural is fixed, already given when play begins.** Its text and its mixed Northern reception (`docs/research/2026-11-14-march-1861-facts.md`, row 12) set the scenario's starting values for the war's framing (owned by `politics`, 0040), border-state loyalty and Northern opinion. The player's own tone shows in what follows: the Sumter and Pickens choices, Consult and stances on record, and seat initiatives such as writing to the press (0040).

Reasons:
- **No one to push back.** A speech tone picked from a menu is a verb with no person in it, which goes against the premise of commanding through people (0016). The first choice the player meets should show the core loop (advice, an authored staff recommendation, a live clock), and Sumter already does.
- **The gate.** Varying the address means varying its slavery passage. Any wording of that option either scores the player morally or leaks hindsight, which the gate (0021) and neutral option wording (0041) exist to prevent.
- **Little game weight.** The war opens whatever the player does (0040's railroad), and the framing term is moved by the player's later choices of timing, fort and notice. A first-minute speech choice would add a scripted decision before the player knows any system, for an effect the first month already provides.

Rejected:
- **A tone option on the setup screen.** It is a setup slider, not a person, and it carries the same gate problem.
- **A live situation on 4 March** (draft the address with advisers). It is interesting, but it puts an authored set piece before any system is learned. It can come back as an optional prologue once the situations schema (lane 0.7) and the gate checklist exist.

## Consequences

- Lane 0.7's March 1861 situations start the day after the inauguration. The pack sets the starting framing, opinion and loyalty values from the address and its reception, with citations.
- The prologue idea is noted for lane 0.7, to be considered after the March situations are authored.
