# 0122: The Confederate cause is a value, and a signed undertaking shows who held it

Status: accepted (2026-11-23). From S113's research (`docs/research/2026-11-23-S113-confederate-cause.md` and `docs/research/S113/` on `sprint/research-cast` at `5810e450`). Decided by the lead under the working style in `CLAUDE.md`; the owner can overturn it. Clarifies 0074 (a signed undertaking is the signer's own motive) and 0120 (one rule for both sides' calls).

## Context

- **What 0120 asked.** Item 1 sent D1 to research: did Confederate actors hold a commitment to the Confederacy as a cause, distinct from `state_autonomy` and `slavery_defence`, as `union` is on the Union side?
- **What S113 read.** Contemporary evidence dated before 5 March 1861, under 0085 and 0087, for 48 people: the 38 Confederate rows of the cast and the ten governors with no faction at the start.
  - **The findings for all 48:** 12 held, 2 not held and 34 unknown. For the 38 Confederate rows: 12 held, 1 not held and 25 unknown.
  - **The positive cells rest on two kinds of evidence:**
    - the person's own words: Brown's of 5 January, Perry's of 2 February, Pickens's of 27 February, A. B. Moore's and Wigfall's of 4 March, and Davis's inaugural;
    - a charter or oath the person subscribed himself: Bartow, Howell Cobb, Memminger, Stephens and Toombs signed the provisional charter of 8 February, and Reagan took its oath on 2 March.
  - **The governors who answer the reserve call:** Thomas O. Moore, Pettus, Harris and Letcher remain unknown.
  - **Letcher's existing values:** his message of 7 January 1861 gives 16 cells (9 held, 3 not held, 4 unknown). `state_autonomy` is among the held.
- **The calls.** S113 read each of the 50 Confederate call profiles, ten forms in all. No call's own text rejects the common cause.
- **0120 described the Union side inexactly.** It said the Union profiles already follow a by-kind rule. They don't quite:
  - 41 of the 50 Union profiles honour `union` because their text names it;
  - the nine whose text is silent on purpose honour nothing (S101's readings). They are New York's acceptances of 3 May and 25 and 30 July, the three regular-army orders of 4 May, Rhode Island's letter of 15 May and New York's orders of 16 May.

## Decision

1. **The vocabulary gains `confederate_independence`.**
   - **Definition:** an expressed commitment to establishing or sustaining the common Confederate polity and its collective independence.
   - **How it stands beside other values.**
     - It can coexist with an objection to a Confederate appointment, or with limits on central power.
     - It is distinct from `state_autonomy` and `slavery_defence`.
     - It doesn't detach the Confederacy from slavery: Brown's letter and the provisional charter tie them together, and the retained context shows it.
     - 0074's rules for the values that touch slavery are unchanged.
2. **A personally subscribed undertaking is the subscriber's own motive.** This clarifies 0074's rule that a value is coded only on the holder's own motive.
   - The text of a charter a person signs, or an oath he takes himself, states his own commitment.
   - **What doesn't count:**
     - an office, a nomination or an anonymous state vote;
     - a resignation, a birthplace or later service;
     - a policy carried out for another man.
3. **S113's cells are accepted as proposed.**
   - Unknown stays unknown (0113). No cell is defaulted from faction.
   - The sixteen people with no personal page recovered remain research residuals, not absences.
4. **Letcher's sixteen cells from his message of 7 January are accepted:** 9 held, 3 not held and 4 unknown.
   - His July protest and April refusal supply none of them.
   - The cells that touch slavery enter under 0074's rules. Like the rest of the sprint's sensitive material, they wait for the deferred gate review (0106, 0107).
5. **One rule for both sides' calls.** This clarifies 0120.
   - **What a call honours.** A call for troops honours its own side's war purpose (`union` or `confederate_independence`) unless its own text says otherwise. That covers all 50 Confederate profiles and the nine silent Union profiles.
   - **What a call offends.** Offences stay as each call's text gives them.
     - Where a Union call's text names the suppression of the combinations or insurrection it raises troops against, it offends `confederate_independence`.
     - This is the same text rule under which eighteen Confederate profiles offend `union`.
6. **The pack's call errors S113 found are corrected** from OR IV-I pp.231–232:
   - **Four destinations:** Georgia's and Louisiana's absolute calls of 22 April name Richmond, Kentucky's names Harpers Ferry and North Carolina's names Richmond, where the pack says Lynchburg;
   - **Tennessee's count:** the shared clause must keep the distribution note's three regiments.

Rejected:
- **A cause value for every Confederate by faction.**
- **Coding the value from office, nomination, vote, resignation or service,** or from a policy executed for another.
- **Reading July words back into starting cells.**
- **Exceptions by register row, and any change of weight, threshold or trait.**
- **A call offending the other side's purpose by its kind alone.** Offence stays a matter of the call's text, as S101 read it.
- **Keeping the nine silent Union profiles empty while Confederate calls honour their cause.** That would be a different rule on each side.

## Consequences

- **The build.** S116 builds this on `sprint/july`: the value, the cells, the recoding by kind on both sides, the text offences and the corrections. Then it measures.
- **What the measurement may show, without any promise:**
  - Brown holds the value, so his reserve answer (C10) may become an undertaking with a protest.
  - Thomas O. Moore is unknown, so C11 may stay a mismatch.
  - Letcher's `state_autonomy` may ground his appointment protest (C07).
  - Every other answer by an actor who holds either war-purpose value may move too, and each move is reported with its cause.
- **Further evidence stays open.** Evidence for the unknowns is welcome, from a later research lane, under the same rules.
