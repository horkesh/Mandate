## Mechanisms and proposed rulings

**Entrusted carriers [I].** Optional sourced `latency.dispatches` rows name
the target post, ordered carrier posts, each outbound courier/ship route,
and the delivery's boldness and value terms. They supplement the command
chain; bypassing that chain does not erase the carriers. Each carrier
answers through ordinary compliance. Positive extra holds on orders count
as relay holds; travel time does not. Reports stay sealed during carriage,
and report carriage does not fire an incoming-wire filter counter.

Ship transit uses the movement model's directional water network, seasonal
capacity, damage and obstruction checks. It does not substitute roads when
water passage is unavailable. The lookup belongs to delivery, outside the
principal's menu projection. Missing or departed carriers are not silently
skipped. A present carrier may decline; an unavailable carrier's report
failure is filed for its sender without creating fresh authorship under
the former holder's post.

No historical carrier, appointment, tenure or deliberate staff resistance
was invented. S37's source qualifications still hold. The new path is proved
with fictional fixtures, including a logged order through an ordinary relay,
a staff carrier and a separate ship carrier. Historical admission of the
Pickens route remains with the lead and command-data owner; a synthetic
journey is not a new verified floor case.

**Originator disclosure [I].** `sendReport(..., relayOf)` requires a source
paper the reauthor actually received. An optional report-template rule
permits an uncandid holder of a listed value to omit its known originator.
The ordinary typed-claim channel carries either a named originator or an
unnamed one. Private source/origin audit fields survive subsequent filters
but never supply a name to the next reauthor or enter the seat view. A
submitted plan's sealed enclosure cannot be reauthored this way.

Reject generating a fictitious complete wire to trim, recovering hidden
provenance from truth, or refreshing the projection ratchet. The draft
top-level projection field was replaced by a typed claim; protected
projection functions and the rule baseline remain unchanged. Existing
papers retain their old representation. Two appended console strings show
only the received attribution; every old string value is preserved.

**Calibration [I].** The [single proposed change](../research/2026-11-22-S53-deterrence-proposal.md)
raises deterrenceWeight 250 → 1000 in a measurement-only World override,
with temperamentWeight 400 and complyAt 10 fixed. Sixteen paired histories
over eight seeds completed with zero rejections. The baseline has 124
reservations / 216 slow walks; the candidate has 18 / 46. In the reference
seed, 17/18 becomes 3/1, while plan objections fall 38 → 6 and relay holds
1 → 0. Across the seeds, the candidate breaches the reservations floor
three times, plan-objection floor five times, and slow-walk band twice;
relay holds are zero throughout. Recommend rejecting global application.
All 1,176 changed daily hashes, every changed daily counter/liveness row,
and response terms have direct or explicitly inferred downstream causes
in the [portable trace](../research/_pushback/s53-calibration.json).

**Reclassification [I].** The [unsigned proposed record](../research/2026-11-22-S53-proposed-0099-amendment.md)
would classify N3-27 as authorized conditional delivery and N3-06 as outward
diplomatic handling. It amends no accepted record or source catalogue here.
Every numerical bound remains binding pending the lead's explicit ruling.
