# S119 Tennessee April requisition timing

**Verdict.** The handoff conflates dispatch with receipt. S116 sends the April 22 requisition on July 3, Harris receives it and answers on July 5, and the ordinary levy musters on July 10. The model's failure to issue the paper in April is an authored-path mismatch. This call was put in the doctrine that requires a known holder, even though the model already supports addressing a requisition to a public office before its holder is known. The historical discrepancy is established. Which authored path should carry the call and what authority its resulting levy has are design decisions. Route those to the lead and keep the timing change out of S119.

## Read evidence and chronology

| Evidence | What it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| OR, ser. IV, vol. I, printed p. 272 (`S118/read/IV1-p272.txt`) | Harris writes Walker from Nashville on April 30. After Walker's April 22 telegram and a same-day telegram from Letcher, Harris proceeded to organise three regiments for Lynchburg. Letcher now asks him to wait. Harris reserves recall to Tennessee and asks for arms and provisioning at Lynchburg. His reply proves receipt by April 30 and identifies the route as telegram. It does not give the exact receipt day. |
| OR, ser. IV, vol. I, printed p. 275 (`S118/read/IV1-p275.txt`) | Harris's May 2 telegram reports that Turney's regiment left Winchester, Tennessee, for Lynchburg **the preceding night**. The source supports the night of May 1, reported May 2. Bate and Maney are expected to be ready in a day or two, and Harris asks whether to send them. The telegram neither dates their departures nor attests the muster, headcount or Confederate receipt of any of the three. |
| OR, ser. I, vol. II, printed pp. 897–898 (`S118/read/II-p897.txt`, `II-p898.txt`) | Lee's June 1 letter to Johnston names Turney's regiment and says it has been directed to report to Johnston as soon as practicable. It supplies a later command step distinct from the April requisition and the departure for Lynchburg. |

## What held the order

- The pack authored April 22, three regiments, 365 days, with no `requiresPapers` or department predicate on the call ([pack.json](../../../packs/union-1861/pack.json#L990)). The doctrine projects `fromDate` into a minimum day and uses the call ID for deduplication ([requests.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/seats/requests.ts#L176)). `sendCalls` considers a due row only if the post is present in `menu.raising` with an available route and the call remains unissued. The seat then applies normal attention pricing ([rule seats](../../../packages/rule-seats/src/index.ts#L162)). The contemporaneous April calls and the other overdue Tennessee calls sent together distinguish this from a Tennessee-only authored date or quarter-long transit.
- `menu.raising` requires a received **held** post with a named holder. Its side may be the issuer's or neutral ([requests.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/seats/requests.ts#L288)). At opening Tennessee is neutral, with Harris at Nashville in the scenario's post table. Davis's people projection starts from his own establishment and derives a foreign holder from evidence, such as a report signed from that office. Government-side notices change the known side without filling an unobserved office ([people.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/view/people.ts#L43), [received.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/seats/received.ts#L38)). The day-60 save still has Harris in the actual post and no retained Harris/Tennessee report in Davis's knowledge. `issueCall` also requires a known holder on the ordinary command path ([recruitment.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/tick/recruitment.ts#L58)). Neutrality itself is admissible. The rule-seat call could issue once Davis had a held governor in his received establishment and sufficient attention.
- The day-120 named dispatch proves availability by that morning. The exact first holder witness and the exact day the menu first offered Harris cannot be recovered from these retained artifacts. S116's only full intermediate save is day 60. `pruneKnowledge` can remove both superseded reports and their truth records ([knowledge.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/knowledge/knowledge.ts#L419)). The earliest surviving Harris-authored Nashville control report to Davis is stamped day 120 and received day 122, too late to trigger the day-120 dispatch. The July 2 tender notice names Walker as author, and its `state_forces` effects do not write a known post holder. Its proximity to the call is therefore insufficient to identify the transient witness or prove that attention played no part after the office was first learned. No fresh run was taken against the changing S119 tree.
- `regimentsRaised` executes `comply_with_reservations` at the `comply` rung, which has zero days of delay. `arriveCall` schedules the levy at receipt plus `musterDays` plus the rung delay ([recruitment.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/tick/recruitment.ts#L97), [pack rules](../../../packs/union-1861/pack.json#L1686)). Here it is `122 + 5 + 0 = 127`. That muster rule worked as authored. The source dates concern receipt, organisation and departure. They give no reason to change the global muster delay.

## Smallest follow-up for the lead

1. Prefer one explicit office-addressed Confederate requisition for the April instrument, preserving its existing identity, terms and ordinary recipient compliance. The accepted S65 path already handles a chosen situation's office-addressed call ([sender.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/seats/sender.ts#L22), [situations.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/tick/situations.ts#L164), [orders.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/tick/orders.ts#L215), [deliver.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/tick/deliver.ts#L91)). Under 0098, replace its matching doctrine dispatch rather than creating a duplicate. The current situation call forwards `termsId` but does not carry the stable `callId`. Keep that ID through dispatch and response so C05 and lot lineage still refer to the same request. If a dated principal-doctrine row is the right owner, an explicitly authorised per-row office address reusing this validation/delivery path is the narrow code alternative. Either approach should check the evidenced telegram against the existing wire policy, which has no Montgomery–Nashville public-office row ([pack latency](../../../packs/union-1861/pack.json#L1353)). Page 272 supports that historical route and reply bound, without a guaranteed same-day receipt.
2. Keep delivery, Harris's organisation and conditions, onward movement and Confederate receipt separate. Current neutral levies muster under their governor and serve no side while the government is neutral ([recruitment.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/tick/recruitment.ts#L227)). The rule seat's ordinary concentration uses its own-side `commandable` posts. `senderFor.promisedTroops` can recognise a neutral governor's received positive reply when an explicit transfer is requested, but it does not add that automatic concentration entry ([sender.ts](../../../packages/kernel/src/seats/sender.ts#L60)). Correcting issue alone therefore promises no May 1 departure. Under 0108 and 0124, let the resulting lots and any eventual transfer follow their owners and receiving papers. Leave C05's authority question and qualification pending the proper mechanism under 0112; the retained `muster_receiver` request supplies no authority reservation. 0113 supplies no trait-based timing fix, and 0110 supplies no reason to turn unknown men into a numeric credit.
3. A general public address book for neutral governor offices would change every eligible correspondence and needs a separate ruling. Advancing the Tennessee side flag, inserting the hidden holder into Davis's menu, reducing the shared delay, or scripting departures and named musters would conflate the separate decisions above. Keep this diagnosis and route the narrower office-addressed call for a ruling.
