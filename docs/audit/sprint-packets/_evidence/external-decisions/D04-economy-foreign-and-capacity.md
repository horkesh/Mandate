# NNNN: Economic stocks and foreign pressure work through holders with declared units and owners

Status: proposed (2026-11-20), S10 design recommendation [I]. No new economic quantity, rate or foreign-policy fact is established.

## Context

Read sources: decisions 0023 (light economy), 0028 (blockade/two wills), 0029 (weather and symmetric opponent), 0078 (raising men and protected agency), 0080 (stores norms); FOUNDATION sections 4, 7, 9, 10 and 12; `docs/OPEN_QUESTIONS.md`, rail-gauge ruling and Phase 1 economy/foreign findings. The gate's sign rule needs measure direction declarations before new fields are used. `notes/lane-1.14-done-check.md`, “Since the standing review” and R85, leaves capacity and foreign channels unmeasured.

S5 and proposed lanes 1.18/1.20 must establish amounts, periods, delays, holder authority and compatible comparisons from pages read. This draft proposes the interfaces for 1.27–1.30. It does not treat the underlying research note's legal or numerical claims as freshly verified.

## Decision

1. **Retain `economy` as the sole writer of money, obligations, munitions and named-line capacity allocations.** Proposed [I]: use declared integer units, dated instruments and typed transactions. Congress authorises; Treasury and procurement holders execute through their own answers. A priority is an instruction to a person, not a purchase that bypasses him.
2. **Proposed [I]: keep recruitment flows distinct.** Governors answer calls for new blocks; the War Department supplies refills to existing blocks, through `forces` for strength, `movement` for location and `command` for assignment. No duplicated free-men stock is introduced merely to make a dashboard total. Terms, service expiry and casualty/refill accounting remain explicit.
3. **Proposed [I]: use `politics` for separately identified foreign/blockade clock State.** Blockade effectiveness is derived from supplied active stations; it is an input to pressure, not another saved copy of station strength. Britain and France remain non-playable powers worked through the Secretary of State. Clock units, directions, lags and limits are pack data with typed explanations.
4. **Proposed [I]: foreign actions are authored pack acts over conditions.** Demands, deadlines and restrictions have cause, transmission and receipt. A clock threshold does not make a holder instantly know a demand or automatically agree to it. The 1.27a contract is usable on a fictional station fixture; real-pack blockade completion waits for 1.32's actual station service.
5. **Proposed [I]: named rail allocations are shared inputs to actual movement and supply.** The railroad holder can stall. The already-ruled gauge-break transfer carries a pack cost; parallel named lines remain distinct. Demand and available allocation cannot both spend the same capacity. The planner uses the reported allocation/condition contract, not hidden damage.
6. **Retain weather's existing owner and randomness boundary.** Proposed [I]: a named weather-zone/day stream supplies exogenous conditions. They affect actual passage/capacity and enter commanders' stated reasons through known conditions. Commanders' reactions never roll dice. Any new combat weather/supply term is checked against named fixtures; no blanket assault-rate fitting.
7. **Retain 0078's protected-agency restrictions.** Only the allowed capacity reader and `tick/regions.ts` consume self-emancipation/arrival inputs. Proposed [I]: its effect on enemy capacity has named typed terms and calibration evidence, without a labour stock, recruitment pool or direct benefit assigned by a policy option. The census is never depleted. No valuation of enslaved people enters Treasury finance.
8. **Proposed [I]: every new side measure declares its favourable direction before effects load.** Sign and dependency checks follow it through stocks, flags, clocks and derived measures. Missing directions, unsupported units or unmeasured consequences remain errors/holds rather than free zero-cost effects.

Rejected:
- A detailed second economy game, resource purchases by the principal, or railroad capacity as a region bonus nobody owns.
- Treating every unknown stores entry as an invented norm or equating incompatible annual, monthly, paper and present quantities.
- Autonomous foreign factions, personality dice, or a scalar pressure that silently performs all diplomatic acts.
- Emancipation directly filling Union men/money/labour, or monetising protected populations.

## Consequences

1.27a supplies the foreign-act interface before 1.28; 1.28 supplies authority/transactions before 1.29; 1.29/1.30 supply stores/capacity interfaces before 1.32. 1.27b completes real blockade wiring after 1.32. These are staged contracts, not a cyclic dependency between completed lanes. New fields require sole-writer entries, migrations and source-backed pack rules. Conservation, refusal, gauge transfers, weather replay and identical-reports planning probes precede a long campaign. R2/R7/camps and their reviewer still govern affected facets.
