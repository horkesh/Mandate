## Decision

1. **Recommended [I]: split ordinary guerrilla pressure/policy from atrocity-class executions and reprisals**, as C.1 recommends. The exact topic ids/classes receive gate review. The split must preserve every permanent refused signature and every affected existing signed item's identity or explicitly send it for re-review.
2. **Retain 0046/0029 and 0074:** guerrilla war is regional pressure, and military raids target infrastructure/depots. No player or non-player goal, option or initiative targets civilians, burns civilian homes or orders collective reprisals. **0074 explicitly includes assessments laid on a community, banishment and hostages in the atrocity-class collective-reprisal boundary**, carrying both topics. The old C.1 draft cannot place those as ordinary non-atrocity policy. No appointment, delegated order or change of topic class circumvents that refusal.
3. **Recommended [I]: conduct is source-backed seat behaviour with locked consequences through owners.** Atrocity-class conduct is event/text under the sign rule; it never improves the acting side's control, supply, loyalty, will or favourable foreign pressure. Under 0057/0060, a notice's effects may only worsen the acting side's own measures; other sides stay unchanged. A tainted response decision has its separate deciding-side locked-cost rule and no favourable gain. The fact that a commander was appointed or had formal authority is no exception.
4. **Retain 0074's response boundary:** every option restricts, suspends, refers or revokes the atrocity-class order/sentence; letting it stand is only inaction, costed under E7, narrated as non-intervention in a matter put before the president and watched by D6. Recommended [I]: use the ordinary relief/pressure/order pathways and sourced restriction/review situations for the concrete cases. Restricting or revoking a shooting clause is distinct from deciding an emancipation clause. No confirm-execution or “let stand” option is offered. Dakota's review remains R3's separate ruling, as 0074 expressly requires.
5. **Recommended [I]: separate periods and evidence holds.** In-window occupation and guerrilla cases are included only after their facet is sourced and placed. Later examples remain labelled later. Do not use one verified commander's case to invent motives or conduct for another side or theatre.
6. **Retain the ship hold until the record and required facets are accepted.** Remove only a matching temporary hold rule; keep permanent refusals. If one wording rule is narrowed for ordinary military narration, prove its atrocity cases still fail with independent probes.

Rejected:
- One non-atrocity topic that loses the sign rule, or a split used to reopen permanently refused killings.
- Confirm-execution, occupation-severity or reprisal-benefit controls; claiming formal legality makes them exempt.
- Treating the old C.1 draft or its cited historical summary as a new signature/source verification.
