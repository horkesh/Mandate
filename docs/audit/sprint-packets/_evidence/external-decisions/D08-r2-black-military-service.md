## Decision

3. **Recommended [I]: represent unequal treatment/pay as cited conditions and derived grievances/enlistment responses**, with a sourced policy route for pressing Congress only when evidenced. The pay difference cannot create savings available to the player. Duty/officer policy remains needs-source until placed; Navy service is separately represented at the existing naval scale, without a crew pool.
4. **Recommended [I]: route any resulting ordinary force creation through existing owners** only after its agency-safe formation contract is accepted. `forces` creates, `movement` places and `command` assigns. No situation directly proposes a protected-person count or consumes a census stock. The exact admissible metadata and derivation receive separate gate review.
5. **Retain locked consequences and capture boundaries.** Conditions feed attributed responses by actors; no virtue score, racist narrator voice or captured-people fate option. Under 0074, **R2 and R4 must re-rule capture before the first Black unit exists in force data**, including any newly researched captured Black teamsters/servants facet. R4 handles exchange, and Confederate capture-policy acts retain 0046's event restriction. Later topics remain registered for their actual window.
6. **Recommended [I]: keep R2's release hold until the evidence, placement, refused signatures, agency reasons, working readers and content-hash sign-offs are complete.** Source availability or a proposed decision is not clearance.
