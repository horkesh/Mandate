# NNNN: The second operational pass keeps planning on reports and execution with the existing owners

Status: proposed (2026-11-20), S10 design draft [I]. Not implemented; no accepted detachment or gate rule is superseded.

## Context

Read sources: decisions 0026, 0029, 0041, 0062, 0075, 0080, 0081 and 0083; FOUNDATION sections 4–5 and 10–12; `notes/lane-1.2-planner.md`, “The boundary” and findings; `notes/lane-1.4-operations.md`, findings; the complete 1.4b and 1.4c hand-overs. OPEN_QUESTIONS carries the same two-carrier, garrison, unseen-damage, naval stores and detachment regression items. The source limits and R93 contact probe belong to S1's integration; this draft does not endorse a global assault-rate change.

1A's planner may read true edge condition and derived supply because nothing damages an edge there (0075). That permission must not become knowledge of an unseen 1B raid. Water axes currently require troops already aboard and move only the first carrier. A declined assault is a stand-off, not the planned siege method. These are the concrete triggers for proposed lanes 1.23/1.30–1.33.

## Decision

1. **Proposed [I]: report the ground before it affects planning.** Derive known edge condition, capacity and the supply reach dependent on them from the commander's received information. Identical views must draft identical candidates when only hidden damage changes. Actual movement still reads real passage and may discover a failure. Keep the existing shared passage rule and one-level planner; no second simulation inside planning.
2. **Proposed [I]: save an unsolicited proposal as a kernel-drafted candidate from its author's view.** Adoption attaches its identity to an ordinary goal addressed to the executing holder. That holder can object or reinterpret an outsider's plan. The proposing person's seat does not assign units, teleport troops or bypass review. Proposal generation respects per-side drafting limits and deterministic content ids.
3. **Proposed [I]: a siege is an operation stage/method**, with elapsed time, supply, works, progress and abandonment carried by existing owners and reports. The commander chooses; the player supplies, questions or replaces him. No tactical approaches/mines or automatic dated capitulation. Named test cases distinguish a continuing stand-off, a real siege and an assault.
4. **Proposed [I]: add river and rail-capacity-aware candidates and a commander-authored garrison step.** Route permissions, support requests and recipient compliance remain in force. The commander assigns men; a player edits goals/arrows within the accepted grammar. Freelancing is a validated seat initiative executed as an operation, not a new direct unit verb.
5. **Proposed [I]: carriage uses one checked proposal across existing owners.** `movement` alone places a block on a carrier or ashore; `forces` alone splits/joins strength and stores; `command` alone assigns; `operations` alone changes stage. Validate capacity, adjacency, carrier support and all affected blocks before applying anything. An axis names and moves every carrier it needs, not just the first.
6. **Retain 0081/0083 identity and conservation.** Empty parent shells remain until the accepted rejoin/disband path removes them; they cannot be traded as units. A landing's detachment identity is not replaced with a new parent path. Same-day rejoin remains refused, including counter-suffixed ids. Stores and stragglers are conserved apart from separately explained consumption/loss.
7. **Proposed [I]: afloat units consume and obtain stores under the same supply contract the planner scores.** Supplied blockade stations contribute service; unsupported ones can run low or leave. Navy encounter/battle news uses the existing abstract squadron/post scale, with typed loss records and ordinary reports. No ship movement commands are exposed to the principal.
8. **Retain the raid/atrocity boundary.** Military edges and depots may be raid targets; civilians, protected people, homes and collective reprisals are not goal targets. `infrastructure` applies damage/repair proposals and reports carry the new information. R3/R6 must place conduct before it loads.
9. **Proposed [I]: 1.30c owns R93's confined-assault contact/exposure question**, explicitly parked to 1B in OPEN_QUESTIONS. Separate the men abreast from cumulative exposure only under a lead-approved bounded geometry definition, with defenders' fire as the limiting evidence. This would amend 0076 item 1; it is not an accepted change now. Preserve the other named assault fixtures and stop at the agreed probe/implementation budget, rather than raising all loss rates to fit Wagner.

Rejected:
- True hidden infrastructure supplied to the planner, perpetual ship supply, or a real-pack blockade calibrated before station service exists.
- A hard-coded historical operation catalogue, tactical siege controls, or scripted battle results.
- Applying only the forces half of a refused embarkation, moving only one of two carriers, or silently changing 0083 to simplify implementation.
- A garrison or raid verb that puts the president back in the general's seat.

## Consequences

1.23 supplies beliefs; 1.30 supplies real passage/capacity/weather contracts; 1.31a/b supplies proposal/siege/garrison contracts; 1.32a/b supplies carriage/stores; 1.31c/1.32c finish river execution/news. 1.27b then completes the real blockade. These named substeps avoid waiting for two mutually dependent complete lanes. Tests must break on two-carrier omission, unknown-damage planning, partial mutation, duplicate ids, lost stores and support refusal. New fields get exact ownership and migrations before use. Any departure from 0081/0083 requires a separate superseding record, not acceptance of this draft alone.
