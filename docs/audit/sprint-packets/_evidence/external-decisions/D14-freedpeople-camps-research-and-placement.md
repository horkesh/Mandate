# NNNN: Freedpeople's camps require their own evidence and placement before the slice can ship

Status: proposed (2026-11-20), S10 candidate [I]. The whole-topic needs-source hold remains; no camp text or mechanics is accepted.

## Context

Read ruling sources: 0021/0026 as incorporated by 0046; SENSITIVE-HISTORY sections 1, 3.2 `union-freedpeople-camps`, 3.8 and 4; `notes/lane-1.8-gate.md`, C.2's held topics and needs-source; OPEN_QUESTIONS, the unresearched camp conditions, plantation policy and whole-topic ship-blocker. 0078 places self-emancipation and arrivals; it does not source camp conditions or permit assigning the people's labour.

S7's general civilians/policy remit cannot be treated as a completed camps source dossier. Proposed lane 1.21's camps/prison brief supplies one or records the gaps. This draft claims no camp number, population, mortality rate, wage, contract or statement by a historical person.

## Decision

1. **Recommended [I]: commission the smallest complete evidence dossier** for the camps and conditions required in the actual release window. Read records of the people themselves alongside administrative and medical accounts where available; preserve their different interests and incompatible measures. Distinguish the source's observation date from its publication date and identify unread/blocked leads.
2. **Retain the whole-topic hold until the dossier and placement record are accepted.** A later-start scenario, a full military campaign or a signed policy situation cannot silently clear it. The release lane must inspect the actual scenario/topic overlap and required text/derived facets.
3. **Recommended [I]: begin the placement proposal with attributed events and text**, plus only the already-permitted agency-derived terms. Whether a further policy reply is needed goes to the gate reviewer after the evidence identifies the actors and authority. A narrated-only choice is not automatically a complete or sufficient placement.
4. **Retain permanent resource refusals.** No Union labour stock, plantation labour assignment, price per person, transport allocation or policy effect that spends the people counted at the lines. Arrivals and protected census data are not an economy's input pool. Any fixed institutional appropriation requires its own source and accepted policy contract.
5. **Recommended [I]: show conditions and people's responses with explicit agency and uncertainty**, without a productivity/efficiency reward or an option improving measures through deprivation. Both omission and an invented benign condition misstate the topic; unresolved evidence must remain visible in the hand-over.
6. **Recommended [I]: if the dossier reveals a genuinely new mechanism**, stop that item at a proposed bounded brief with its fields, sole writers, gate placement and validation cost. Do not enlarge the research lane into implementing it or call report-only staging done.

Rejected:
- Calling self-emancipation research a camp-conditions dossier, or clearing the topic on the strength of one administrative order.
- Managing protected people as workforce, supplies, transport cargo or a benefit to either side.
- Removing a release blocker by ending the checkpoint before its material arrives, or inventing rates to make the economy run.

## Consequences

1.21 returns cited facets and HOLDs; 1.16b obtains the precise placement and reviewer decision. Authoring waits for that record and receives independent hashes/signatures. 1.29 respects the restricted capacity reader; it cannot import arrivals as a stock. If evidence remains unavailable, work on unrelated lanes continues while the release claim remains held. S10 writes this decision candidate only, without any source-clearance or signature claim.
