# NNNN: R3 preserves Native nations' agency and places each war facet before authoring

Status: proposed (2026-11-20), S10 candidate [I] for the lead and separate gate reviewer. R3 remains held; no historical count, speech or placement is certified.

## Context

Read ruling sources: 0026; 0046; 0069–0071's nation-agency requirements as referenced by FOUNDATION and OPEN_QUESTIONS; SENSITIVE-HISTORY sections 3.7 and 4, R3; OPEN_QUESTIONS, territorial-nation research, the regional-move guard and the Dakota-voiced leads. The register requires a record even when a later-start scenario omits an earlier situation. Its R3 recommendation remains a proposal, not authority to offer execution choices.

S7 names policy, prisoners, guerrilla war and civilians; S10 must not assume that this exhausts Dakota, Native-voiced or other territorial-nation research. Proposed 1.21's nations brief owns those gaps. December's review and later events remain release obligations even if the integration checkpoint is November. Sources' contested numbers stay attributed ranges, and no words are assigned to a historical person without a primary copy read.

## Decision

1. **Retain nations as their own governments/seats or sourced data**, never subdivisions whose allegiance follows a neighbouring state. A treaty or response is the nation's own act, with its own people and internal conflicts represented as the read evidence permits. No Union or Confederate recruitment verb draws on a national manpower pool.
2. **Recommended [I]: define protection at the interface.** It forbids resource/population targeting and coerced manpower flows, while diplomacy can reach a nation's authorised holder. The lead/gate reviewer settles which specific diplomatic facets are legal after the contemporary evidence is read. Neither administrative convenience nor present military control erases a polity.
3. **Retain 0069's runtime region-government guard** for any first in-play region move. Validate same-kind/polity requirements and return the reserved refusal. Geographic gaps, mixed-government regions, Osage and other territorial nations receive sourced mapping or explicit unknowns; do not infer the boundaries from the game engine's existing departments.
4. **Recommended [I]: keep the Dakota war and sentence-review facets held until Native-voiced evidence and the competing institutional accounts are read.** Prefer a review/suspension/information process through holders, with executions narrated as conduct rather than a player selecting people or an execution count. The final permitted choices and inaction still need the lead/gate ruling; this recommendation does not author or auto-schedule the historical act.
5. **Retain atrocity and sign rules.** No option orders or profits from killing protected people; appointment, delegation, waiting or a formally legal sentence cannot bypass the rule. Facts, actors' positions and disputes appear through attributed text and reports, not a game's moral or military reward for a chosen fate.
6. **Recommended [I]: review the whole release window**, including December tails and later-facet labels. R3's ship hold remains while its required facets or whole-topic evidence are missing; ending 1B earlier cannot waive it.

Rejected:
- Nations as manpower pools, territories silently assigned to either side, or invented primary voices.
- Selecting condemned people, a target number of executions, or a supposedly “best” sentencing option.
- Omitting the topic because the last simulation checkpoint precedes its later stages.

Existing nation treaties already accepted are not reopened without cause. Nation responses to force entry and 1862 cases are separately sourced. Signature and runtime guard probes precede acceptance. The recommendation on review-only choices is a design choice still to rule on, not a claim that history offered only those alternatives.
