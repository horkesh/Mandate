# NNNN: Phase 1 acceptance binds every criterion and calibrates only measured consequences

Status: proposed (2026-11-20), S10 draft [I]. No acceptance criterion, band, CI flag or D6 hold changes now.

## Context

Read sources: FOUNDATION sections 11–12; decisions 0068, 0072, 0073, 0086, 0099 and 0105; `notes/lane-1.14-done-check.md`, Part A, G-1.14a–d, R85/R94, “The stand-ins” and findings; CODEX.md's S1/S2/S10. 0099 makes the default runner exit bind Phase 0 while separately reporting 1A; `--milestone 1a` exists. It does not implement `--milestone 1b`.

0105 parks D6 clearance until the missing consequence channel exists and keeps the post-policy new-levy requirement. It rejects global fixes for missing mechanisms and grants a separate second 1A budget. 1B must not spend or relabel that budget. The longer endpoint and new political/foreign/capacity inputs need an explicit acceptance and scoring contract.

## Decision

1. **Proposed [I]: freeze the Phase 1 acceptance specification before final calibration.** Lane 1.15 names the endpoint, reference cases, seeds and checkpoints. Research supplies compatible measures, units, dated observations, source pages, inferred limits and binding status. No run supplies its own historical target. Missing evidence remains an unmet criterion.
2. **Proposed [I]: make all retained Phase 0 and 1A criteria binding alongside the new Phase 1 criteria** in the actual implemented milestone path. Verify the flag and exit behaviour with a deliberately failing fixture before CI uses it. A default exit 0, expected-failure annotation, parked criterion or absent observation is not a phase pass.
3. **Retain 0099's D6 coverage rule.** Follow flags, policy pushback, grievance/ladder, capacity, foreign and national-will terms to the eventual measured outcomes. Exercise the new border-government call/answer/levy after each affected policy fork. A transfer, old levy or an unexercised route cannot satisfy it. Inaction/delegation/grouped choices retain their watched status and cost.
4. **Proposed [I]: extend the victory measure only after the real consequence owners exist.** Return each count and contribution, explain directions and double-counting, and obtain an explicit lead/gate ruling. Do not add every stock positively merely to claim channel coverage. A partial consequence remains “measured in part,” invalid as D6 clearance. Ties count as best; watched best in more than a quarter of seeds and any unpaid floor remain breaches.
5. **Proposed [I]: one final budget of eight measured calibration changes** opens on the frozen integrated 1B head. Make one change per measured run, source corrections before engine parameters. The eight-seed D6 comparison inside a run is not eight calibration changes. Keep baseline, rejected candidates, complete failures, commands, exits and source/pack hashes.
6. **Retain deterministic and cost checks.** Daily hashes, repeat/replay, saved-log replay and resume must agree, including around bills, elections, embarking and recurrent/grouped decisions. Linux/Windows evidence remains required. The existing median 40 ms/p95 160 ms limits stand unless a separate record supersedes them; local loaded or unloaded readings cannot certify CI.
7. **Proposed [I]: gate expensive runs with cheap prerequisites**, including local workspace resolution, compiler, selected tests, pack/schema freshness, signed-item validity, a one-day start and a short complete reference before multiplying the longer horizon. Record the question, commands, cost and stop rule before starting. After failure inspect all categories, reproduce and verify cheaply, then rerun only affected evidence. Never repeat a full unchanged suite for reassurance.
8. **Proposed [I]: after the eight changes, give a continue-or-park recommendation per unmet criterion.** The lead owns any new budget or retirement of a criterion. The fresh exit review remains independent and the separate gate reviewer signs content. A calibration ceiling reached is not completion.

Rejected:
- Widening historical bands to match a run, erasing quiet mechanics or substituting telemetry for a fought battle record.
- A single good headline score, a D6 win count with missing costs, or `--milestone 1b` printed in docs before it exists.
- Silent extra campaigns, a parameter search beyond eight changes, or fitting a questionable proxy before resolving the intended outcome.

## Consequences

1.39 implements the harness specification early enough to reject incomplete effects, and spends its final budget only after 1.22–1.38 integrate. It reports every failure and its owner even when another phase exits 0. Diagnostic inventories and preservation checks precede final reports. The lead's continue-or-park ruling and 1.40's exit review decide the next bounded work; neither releases held gate topics. S10's own unchanged-base check is not evidence that any proposed 1B feature exists.
