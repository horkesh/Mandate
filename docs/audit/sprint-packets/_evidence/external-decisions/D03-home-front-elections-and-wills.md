## Decision

1. **Proposed [I]: implement institutions before elections.** `politics` owns each chamber's state, legislative actions, the accountability ledger and election results. A bill passed by Congress, presidential signature/veto and execution by a department are distinct acts. Session/lapse and conventions' acts use sourced pack calendars and causes; receipt delay is not a universal legislature simulation.
2. **Proposed [I]: model the Joint Committee through investigating seats and reports.** Requests reach holders, whose answers and leaks use ordinary routes. A committee report is an interested account, not access to combat truth. Historical case evidence, authority and the lawful range of actions precede content authoring.
3. **Retain the received-story press boundary.** Editors and political actors can send sourced leaks or records already known to them; alignment changes selection and framing, not the facts they secretly know. Proposed [I]: an enemy readership route preserves origin and latency; no immediate cross-side truth copy. Effects go only to the existing `politics` and `people` writers.
4. **Proposed [I]: political generals and stance/relief costs flow through regard, grievance, standing and ladder behaviour.** A popularity term cannot simply appoint the opposition's future candidate or guarantee a later election. The 1864 rival path remains possible under 0026, while 1B tests the political action visible in its own window.
5. **Proposed [I]: state laws determine election eligibility and procedure.** Pack records carry actual voting dates, lawful soldier-voting arrangements and their effective period, party crosswalks and source uncertainty. National results aggregate state observations. Keep ballots, elected seats, confirmed returns and seating/composition distinct. Future contests remain pending at the November checkpoint.
6. **Retain 0028's two derived wills, with proposed implementation [I].** `politics` derives each from explicit typed terms including blocs, losses, territory, armies, blockade and policy consequences. Do not save a second mandate or will stock. Each source contribution is explainable and counted once; values and thresholds remain pack rules to calibrate, not historical facts.
7. **Proposed [I]: causal channels and gate directions are required before political scoring.** Racist opposition is named as such where the gate requires it, not disguised as neutral economic concern. A border bloc policy value needs sourced/gate-reviewed data; no silent rating fills the gap. R5 governs verdict clauses and R7 policy effects.

Rejected:
- One national swing that overrides state law, or a fixed November date that makes every future House seat already known.
- A permanent political penalty with no meaningful election threat, or an election result forced to history independently of conditions.
- A press module with its own support/standing truth, or leaks that reveal data the sender never received.
- A saved will duplicated across economy, politics and the sweep, or a proxy score presented as the complete political model.

Lane 1.39 separately proposes the victory-score extension. Neither a plausible midterm count nor a passing proxy waives missing gate consequences.
