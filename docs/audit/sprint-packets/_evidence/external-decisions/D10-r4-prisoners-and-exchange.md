## Decision

1. **Retain 0074's present battle rule** until an accepted R4 expressly supersedes the part it changes. Captures are typed battle losses; no camp stock, parole, exchange or individual fate action is introduced by the plan alone. Cited surrender terms remain restricted to the actual historical case and its commander/date guards.
2. **Recommended [I]: make exchange policy a situation through the War Department and authorised agents**, with the sourced choices, counterpart responses and locked political/institutional consequences. The other side's restrictions are attributable acts under the interim Confederate rule where protected people are touched. No officer chooses a captive's death or enslavement from a menu.
3. **Recommended [I]: begin with source-backed events/text for camps and conditions**, naming the people and administrative actors without a capacity-efficiency game. No prisoner labour or savings stock, no “cheaper” maltreatment and no prisoner-count target for a player. A precise simulation of exchange returns needs its own reviewed conservation/eligibility contract before this boundary can expand.
4. **Recommended [I]: if accepted exchange/parole later returns people to service**, identify which existing owner changes person status, force strength, location and eligibility, and name the exact superseded 0074 clause. Prove no double creation, double count or automatic clearing of `captured` before releasing that path. This draft neither authorises that path nor claims an event/text model completes the historical exchange policy.
5. **Retain permanent refusals.** Killing hostages/prisoners, lot selection, enslaving captives and retaliation executions cannot become options, goals or initiatives. An indirectly beneficial atrocity remains refused. All relevant costs and responses, including inaction, remain in D5/D6 coverage.
6. **Recommended [I]: remove only holds justified by the accepted facet record.** Pattern probes distinguish narrated sources from allowed policy. Keep needs-source conditions and the release blocker until required evidence and placements are complete; never delete all R4 narrator rules as one batch.

Rejected:
- Reinterpreting “taken prisoner” as killed, instantly returning every captured soldier, or treating a threatened reprisal as a choice.
- Camp efficiency, prisoner labour, tradeable captive pools or hidden low-cost mistreatment.
- An exchange policy claimed complete while its accepted return/eligibility consequences are absent.
