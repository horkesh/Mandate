# NNNN: New people choices preserve the difference between reports, declarations and private truth

Status: proposed (2026-11-20), S10 design draft [I]. No implementation or gate approval.

## Context

Read sources: decisions 0020 (hidden stats, assessment and officer pool), 0026 (promotion lists and visits), 0041 (business during a visit and report-keyed streams), 0079 (verbs), 0087 (evidence), 0097 (starting blocs) and 0101 (service choice). FOUNDATION sections 5–6 and 8 reserve these behaviours. `notes/lane-seat-view-people.md`, “Audience requirement not met,” explains that earlier papers cannot supply a live case or fresh counter reply. `notes/lane-1.13-console.md`, R88, adds a received answer, not that missing prospective case. `notes/lane-1.1-people.md`, findings, leaves promotion-rank validation open. The later decision 0080 already provides pruning; the old combat note's unbounded-record warning is not authority to build another archive.

This draft proposes contracts for lanes 1.23–1.25/1.38. It makes no historical rating, tenure, name-list or commission claim. S6/S9 and lane 1.17 must supply reviewed evidence. **0087's current March start-class and July innate boundaries remain:** later conduct cannot be back-projected to fill them. A later entrant or changing assessment needs its own dated evidence semantics, and any change of source-policy boundary requires an explicit record before data application.

## Decision

1. **Retain accepted truth boundaries.** `people` alone writes stats, traits, standing, grievances, ranks, stances and service; `command` alone writes occupancy/assignment; `proxy` keeps actual replies private. Assessment and adviser credibility are derived from that principal's received evidence, not saved true-stat estimates.
2. **Proposed [I]: retain origin identity through reports and reprints.** Independent originating sources can strengthen assessment; repeated printing of one account cannot masquerade as several witnesses. Explanations show source, date and uncertainty without revealing true bands. Battle updates and earned traits use explicit pack rules; winning never automatically makes a man braver.
3. **Proposed [I]: add an explicit declaration path for a live audience case.** The menu reads a received or in-person declared account with speaker, recipient, subject and as-of day. It does not execute `previewAudience` over hidden inputs on a UI read. Actual replies occur through a validated command; their declared reasons travel as attributable reports. Consult preserves its actual situation subject. A prior answer remains prior evidence.
4. **Proposed [I]: officer discovery and nomination records belong to `people`.** Real juniors and invented officers surface through reports. Generated identity uses a report-keyed stream under 0041, so reading order changes nothing; names must pass the real-person/roster check. A list's nomination, seniority, consent/lapse and rank validity are distinct from a post appointment. The rank validator checks every proposed rank against the vocabulary before mutation.
5. **Proposed [I]: a later commission changes a civilian's service/bloc only by a dated, explicit pack rule.** Preserve 0101's existing service-choice writer and the fifteen protected rows. Do not equate every office or appointment with army membership or infer allegiance from birthplace. Unsupported membership stays unknown.
6. **Retain the visit boundary in 0041.** The principal names a delegate before leaving; business otherwise takes inaction, never the hidden historical default. Reports from a visit remain fallible. Proposed [I]: visit/return/delegation are logged and saved; honour and list amendments use the accepted attention rules. A retrospective reckoning uses a separate authorised surface.
7. **Proposed [I]: price stance reversal, relief resentment and political escalation with typed terms through existing owners.** Practice changes require the relevant gate record and cited conduct; they are not invented value changes. Calibrate these effects only after independent evidence and consequence coverage exist.
8. **Proposed [I]: source personal eligibility after office and wounded-to-duty recovery separately.** Houston's routable office-service tag under 0100 is not itself a fact of personal allegiance or a nomination entitlement after succession. Lane 1.17 must source the later status before 1.25c changes eligibility. A recovery path would extend 0076 item 11's “not yet” wounded return limitation; `people` alone applies it after acceptance. It must not clear killed/captured status or supply an unsourced healing timetable.
9. **Proposed [I]: `command` owns source-backed permanent post creation, merger, abolition and chain changes**, with atomic validation of occupants and assignments and ordinary bounce rules for old dispatches. Corps remain posts; the deferred inside-army relay mechanic is not introduced by administrative restructuring. 1.25c lands this contract before 1.37's doctrine consumes it.

Rejected:
- Recomputing private compliance to manufacture a newly spoken case, or showing a hidden value merely because it helps build a menu.
- Treating newspaper copies as independent evidence, success as automatic nerve, or a public reputation as competence.
- New rank/bloc/observer writers inside the UI or rule-seat package.
- A promotion list that directly moves divisions, names generic invented real people, or guesses a source's exact date.

## Consequences

1.23 lands declaration/report contracts before 1.24/1.25 or console consumers. Each new field receives ownership, immutable-record and save/log treatment. Paired hidden-world cases must leave an unchanged received menu unchanged; declared answers, visit delegation, list lapse and report-keyed generation require replay/save probes. If attribution cannot be sourced without a new declaration, keep the live-case item unmet rather than substituting old papers. The lead records any superseding change to the accepted audience or service contract before implementation.
