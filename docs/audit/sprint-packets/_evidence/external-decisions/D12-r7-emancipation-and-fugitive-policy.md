## Decision

1. **Retain 0046's policy-versus-resource boundary.** The already-accepted `return` policy ruling is not redrafted: its costs are locked, it is watched, it says return to slavery plainly, and its expiry follows the accepted supersession rule. The lead/gate reviewer checks precise statute predicates against read sources before any later case is authored.
2. **Recommended [I]: place each later policy stage separately**, using the prior R7 draft as an inventory: commanders' policy orders and presidential replies, congressional acts and signing choices, compensated emancipation, the proclamation and colonization policy. The lead/gate reviewer accepts only the sourced advisers' options, lawful authority, inaction and hidden default. A catalogue menu is not a verified set of choices.
3. **Retain 0074's restrictions when reconciling the old draft.** Drop `fund-a-scheme`. Separate any shooting/reprisal clause from emancipation; no “uphold” confirms a killing, burning or collective reprisal. Slavery-defence/noninterference values stay cost-only, and attributed quotations never become narrator reasons or option labels.
4. **Recommended [I]: all effects run through named actor and agency channels.** Blocs, border loyalty, commanders, foreign pressure and institutional accountability have typed, locked consequences; people's resistance/flight and enlistment remain their own responses. No policy directly fills Union men, labour, wealth or virtue. A compensation obligation is an accepted fixed institutional amount, never a per-person player price or movable protected quantity.
5. **Retain 0078's distinctions.** Protecting people at Union lines is not ending slavery in a whole region. The derivation retains internal resistance and flight to lines, including Union-held slave states. World census inputs are read-only. Only the restricted reader derives enemy capacity; arrivals cannot be recruited or allocated by an economy function.
6. **Recommended [I]: a new legal/policy cause can reopen a case under D06's occurrence contract**, preserving its earlier decisions. Practice changes are cited acts through the accepted owner, never edits to immutable World data. New inaction or group combinations carry their policy costs and hashes; floors remain paid and unpaid floors remain breaches.
7. **Recommended [I]: colonization is only source-backed policy toward willing actors**, with no option sending, counting, transporting or pricing people as a managed quantity. The exact permitted in-window choices need gate review; all watched/cost requirements remain. Later schemes and the final proclamation remain in their own period and do not fire early to satisfy November coverage.
8. **Retain D6's hold while any channel is unmeasured**, including policy grievances, enemy capacity, foreign and election effects. R2 governs service; R6 governs civilian conduct; the camps topic remains needs-source until its own record. Acceptance of R7 cannot clear those topics by implication.

Rejected:
- A virtue score, a workforce/military unlock or a law instantly producing troops.
- Reprinting C.2's later-rejected funding option or using a private staff line without a sourced author.
- Collapsing all policy into one proclamation event, conflating protected arrivals with freedom in law, or treating silence as consent without costs.
- A passing sweep over a partial score as permission to lift the gate.
