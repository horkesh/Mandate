# NNNN: Situations preserve earlier decisions when a new cause reopens a case

Status: proposed (2026-11-20), S10 recommendation [I]. No schema, signed content or historical default is changed.

## Context

Read sources: FOUNDATION sections 7–10; decisions 0040, 0057, 0082, 0100 and 0104; `docs/design/SITUATIONS-1861-62.md`, section 3's P2/P3/P4/P8 and section 4's collective-action gap; `notes/lane-1.5b-gate-wiring.md`, “Not done”; OPEN_QUESTIONS, the 1B items on grouped options, changing practices and S17 reopening. The later records already settle notices, typed absence and received causes; the older catalogue is a pressure inventory, not a command to rebuild settled parts.

The existing `resurfaceOn` brings back an open decision; it is not itself a new occurrence of a decided case. Sequentially choosing compatible options can also change cost, expiry and gate effects. A causally later policy review needs history rather than mutation of the old signed card. The exact legal causes and advisers remain research/authoring work.

## Decision

1. **Proposed [I]: distinguish resurfacing, deferral and a new occurrence.** Resurfacing shows an already-open case again. A deferral records what the principal chose to await and the receiving condition. Reopening a decided case requires a newly satisfied authored cause or stage and gets a distinct occurrence id. None overwrites the previous resolution.
2. **Proposed [I]: `situations` is the single writer of occurrence, stage, wait and group state.** The command log names the occurrence being decided; stale ids are refused. Saved decisions and pending waits replay exactly, including across changes of holder and policy authority. People stances remain with `people`, actual replies with `proxy` and reports with `knowledge`.
3. **Proposed [I]: compatible groups are decided as one validated bundle.** The schema declares compatibility and mutually exclusive choices. Validate the entire bundle against the same offered morning before charging attention or proposing effects. Charge the accepted decision once under 0040; no partial effects from an invalid bundle. Locked costs are applied to every actual selected outcome and inaction as the gate requires; shared costs cannot be dropped or multiplied accidentally.
4. **Proposed [I]: wait conditions and new options surface on received/public knowledge.** They must not leak a hidden battle, a future statute or a private stance. Institutional authority may change legal options, but expiration is explicit and author-tested. Historical defaults remain hidden and subject to the same legal validation.
5. **Proposed [I]: collective petitions retain each participant and origin.** Prefer a single authored act/chain using the existing initiative/report interface to inventing a new group-decision engine. No participant is given knowledge, a signature or a judgment not supported by evidence. A bargaining resignation is distinct from 0101's service-side resignation.
6. **Retain gate dependence and signature rules.** Groups, waits, flags and later occurrences remain forward routes for D5/D6. A new policy or wording changes the hash and returns to review. Changing practice in play requires the accepted R7/R6 contract, not a situation writing a static World row. Removing a “held until” rule does not remove a permanent refusal beside it.

Rejected:
- Reusing a decided id and deleting its old answer, or letting a clock reopen every decision indefinitely.
- Several sequential single choices as a substitute for a compatible bundle when the first can affect the others.
- Historical defaults for an absent human principal, or truth-only predicates used to reveal news early.
- A general collective-intelligence subsystem without a sourced case that needs it.

## Consequences

Lane 1.34 first lands the generic schema/occurrence/group contract and a fictional full-path case, then authors its first real case after the relevant gate review. Later waves consume it rather than each inventing recurrence. Tests cover save/resume during a wait, stale commands, a later cause firing once, policy expiry, conflicting groups, atomic refusal and paid inaction. Existing signed cases and their hashes stay untouched unless the lead explicitly routes a new review. December stages remain pending rather than firing before the November endpoint.
