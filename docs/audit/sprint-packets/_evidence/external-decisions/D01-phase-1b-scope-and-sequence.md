## Decision

1. **Proposed [I]: accept the proposal's lane structure as the candidate for 1A's exit review.** Opening simulation work waits for that review, the owner's playtest findings and an explicit disposition of every inherited failed criterion. Preserve 0105's separate S1 calibration and S2's unchanged-behaviour budget work.
2. **Proposed [I]: use 30 November 1862 as 1B's integration endpoint**, keeping the original 5 March 1861 start. The final election observation distinguishes results already known, pending elections and future seating. Lane 1.19 must source state timetables before the lead freezes that contract. This date is a design boundary, not an assertion that the next House was complete.
3. **Proposed [I]: adopt lanes 1.15–1.40 and their bounded sub-briefs**, subject to revision from the exit review. Size counts coherent merges; it promises neither days nor throughput. After 0106's temporary sprint, use the ordinary four active lanes and at most two simulation lanes. For this proposed 1B schedule, behaviour-changing data and rule-seat work count as simulation. **This would amend 0073/FOUNDATION's lane-specific exemption for 1.11-style pack authoring when applied to 1B**, not retroactively change 1A's schedule; draft-only content and research still do not count.
4. **Proposed [I]: accept interfaces before their consumers**, with a small playable case before bulk content, and freeze bands before final calibration. Exact field ownership, file scope, seeds, cost and stopping rules belong in each brief. No lane grows to absorb a newly found system.
5. **Retain accepted boundaries:** principal-only commands, symmetric machinery with 0046's interim Confederate rule, single writers, pure integer kernel, explainable terms, reviewed sources, separate gate review and signatures. No playable Confederacy, Phase 2 build, publication or spending is included.
6. **Proposed [I]: retain the full release obligations separately.** December catalogue stages are a named backlog, and a held topic overlapping the shipped scenario still blocks release. Acceptance of this plan neither signs content nor changes the register.

Rejected:
- Starting implementation on S10's authority alone; 0073 reserves the exit-review step.
- A monolithic lane per major system with no bounded output, or all lanes dispatched at once; neither respects ownership and the work limit.
- Calling every election final on one national date, or using a November endpoint to erase December's sensitive content.
- Replacing a failed criterion with unrelated passing evidence or interpreting a default Phase 0 exit as Phase 1 acceptance.

## Consequences

If accepted after review, the lead gives this record its number and resolves the endpoint from lane 1.19's evidence. The steward synchronises FOUNDATION's accepted plan, ROADMAP, OPEN_QUESTIONS and phase pointers without turning proposal text into history. Lane 1.15 pins input commits and the acceptance specification. D02–D14 remain separate proposals; this record accepts none of their substantive recommendations automatically.
