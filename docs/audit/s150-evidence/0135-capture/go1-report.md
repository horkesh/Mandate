# McDowell’s May28 GO1: report only

GO1’s brigade provisions are **not declared**. The new hold mission uses its assumption of command; it neither instantiates these brigades nor reassigns their regiments. All63 printed-regiment/day groups on84–90 are published in [the capture observer](go1-holdings-84-90.json), including possible alternative numbered New York IDs. Every candidate has `underMcDowell:false`. Their positions and holders equal the independently verified0134 baseline replay. [Baseline verification](go1-baseline-verification.json).

GO1, OR I-LI.1 pp.389–390, names the nine bodies below without militia qualifiers. Sandford’s contemporary May28 report, OR I-II pp.37–39, supplies militia designations for the numbered New York bodies and identifies the Zouaves as Eleventh New York Volunteers. In particular the Twenty-fifth is N.Y.S.M.; U39’s earlier volunteer candidate is retained as an alternative observation, not used to rewrite identity. Heintzelman’s retrospective1863 report, pp.40–41, is corroboration only.

Only First Michigan is under Mansfield at Washington during this window (89–90). Its declared May14 Willcox sending instrument directs Washington (OR III-I p.200); none of the read sending papers transfers this still-Washington model body to McDowell. Historically Sandford attests the May24 crossing and dispositions, including First Michigan/Zouaves at Alexandria, under Scott/Mansfield instructions (OR I-II pp.37–39). The original crossing instruction is referred to there, not recovered here. GO26 establishes McDowell’s geographical department in Virginia with movable headquarters (GO1861 p.61; same text OR I-II p.653). It does not expressly order a named Washington regiment to cross. McDowell’s May29 Arlington report describes Michigan/Zouaves and69th already there (p.653); GO1 explicitly brigades them under his command (OR I-LI.1 pp.389–390). That is the read historical chain. The missing model declaration is the crossing/assignment chain before GO1, together with GO1’s brigade organisation; a territorial title alone does not move these model lots.

McDowell’s existing organisation template is for July orders, not May28 GO1. No crossing, territorial reassignment, brigade declaration, lot identity change or new rule was built for this finding.
