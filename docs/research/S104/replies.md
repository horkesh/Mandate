# S104 reply inventory

2026-11-23. Descriptive forms **[I]**, not new accepted register classes.
P = plain compliance; Q = compliance with named qualifications; O = objection
to a course/request, without undertaking it as requested; R = refusal of the
specific demand, including stated inability. R does not prove motive or the
model's numerical refuse threshold. X = no attributable reply/identified
request/completed response of these forms established. Split a letter by
demand where necessary. A Q need not be a blocking condition.

OR = *War of the Rebellion: Official Records of the Union and Confederate
Armies*, series–volume; ORN = *Official Records of the Union and Confederate
Navies*, series–volume. All locators below are **printed pages** unless the
LoC six-image document is explicitly named. [Receipts](source-pages.csv)
identify extracts and originals; descriptions paraphrase, without silently
correcting OCR. Links resolve to sources, not search results. Stated legal
arguments are attributed to the speaker and not adjudicated here.

## Limits and findings outside my scope

Every form describes the particular paper or bounded act, not all the
actor's conduct. No officer value, governor motive, legal holding, complete
inventory, original unread incoming letter, or eventual fulfilment is inferred.
No excluded or source-qualified row is promoted. Contemporary quotations and
third-party reports remain attributed. The external cases and proposed rung
rule are in [the proposal](../2026-11-23-S104-conditional-compliance.md).

Unrepresented advice/authority/organisation/consent, new source discovery,
code/pack implementation and measured coverage remain outside this inventory.
