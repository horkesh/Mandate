## Corrections and conflicts for later review

1. **March versus July:** 12 Stat. 354 already prohibits employing commanded forces to return fugitives from 13 March 1862. July's section 10 separately forbids military adjudication or surrender. The research note explains why changing a date without identifying the conduct could preserve an error. No expiry was changed.
2. **Prison count locator:** Hoffman's approximate 20,500 Union-held prisoners is in OR II.4, **p. 278**, not p. 277. It is holdings at one date, not a cumulative total.
3. **Agency versus an authority claim:** Hunter's assertion of authorized successful organization must be read with Stanton's denial and Pierce's report of unwilling men being held. Forecasts of future regiments are not actual enlistment returns.
4. **Numerals need images:** Pierce's 9,050 plantation total and the Camp Barker resident/registration figures were read on scans; their OCR is damaged. Camp Barker's cumulative death numeral is left for another-copy comparison.
5. **Dates can conflict inside an edition:** Osceola's heading has an editorial uncertainty note. Benjamin's letter printed as 24 February precedes a reply printed as 21 February. Carpenter and Welles differ over the final cabinet meeting's date. Those discrepancies are recorded, not repaired by guesswork.
6. **Pagination can conflict across hosts:** the retained Jacobs newspaper image is printed p. 144; UNC labels its source p. 3. This research cites the visible original and preserves UNC's attribution and locator discrepancy.
