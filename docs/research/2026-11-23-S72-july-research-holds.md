## Identity and militia findings

[Identity register](S72/commander-identities.csv): three supported joins [I], without expanding middle initials:

- **James H. Carson:** signed Winchester letter of 14 June, Sixteenth Brigade, Virginia Militia; the index explicitly links him to the S66 passages (OR I-II pp.926–927,1028).
- **Gilbert S. Meem:** signed 26 August letter and 28 August War Department address, Seventh Brigade at Winchester. The latter recites Johnston's 21 June two-regiment call and 2 July posting, bridging the July observations (OR I-V pp.808,810–811; OR I-II pp.772–773,948,956).
- **George C. Wynkoop:** signed `Geo. C. Wynkoop` telegram plus explicit first name in the index, linked to the June10/July8 Second Brigade orders. John E. is separate (OR I-LI.1 pp.366,397–398,415,1469; OR I-II pp.699,1101).

**Williams July stays held.** April correspondence names **E. C. Williams** at Chambersburg; the supplement's index names **Edward C. Williams**. Bates names E. C. over Seventh, Eighth, Tenth and Twentieth Pennsylvania in June (OR I-LI.1 pp.366,1466; Bates I p.68). PA survey p.113, MG318 corroborates the E. C. identity. But the supplement's same index attributes other June/July brigade pages to **Alpheus S. Williams**, as does OR I-II p.1099; Dyer p.270 prints **A. S. Williams** through 25 July. Surname-only July orders do not resolve an error or handover. Obtain a signed July Third Brigade return or dated command handover/commission/muster-out record naming the officer and interval. No Edward/Alpheus merger, unseen succession or middle-initial expansion.

[Militia register](S72/winchester-militia.csv): Meem describes condensation into **four field regiments** after the additional 19 July call. The August23 petition identifies field First–Fourth through its officers' signatures: E. Sipe; Mann Spitler, Thomas Buswell and Cullin W. Finter; Jas. H. Sibert, J. A. Hottel and John H. Newell; and **C. P. Horn**, respectively (OR I-V pp.808,810,820–821). These are later field identities, not permanent-number aliases or individual July arrivals, and not First–Fourth Virginia volunteer infantry.

Carson's **October** return names **31st, 51st, 55th, 67th, 89th and 122nd Virginia Militia** (OR I-V p.933). This proves later membership only. The July retention and June letter supply no numbered July list (OR I-II pp.472–473,926–927; p.982 is damaged). July numbered membership and Meem's field-to-territorial crosswalk remain unmet. They need July brigade returns, the condensation order or signed constituent rolls; a later roster cannot be projected backward.
