useful,  while  my  attention  was  particularly  attracted  to  the  adjutant,

Lieutenant  (Jammings,  whose  good  conduct  OIL  this  as  on  every  other

high  praise  for  the  success  of  his  battery,  as  he  acted  as  gunner  to  each
