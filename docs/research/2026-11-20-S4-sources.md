## How the evidence was used

**Selected pages were read, not entire volumes.** A scan being downloaded is not a claim that every fact in it has been checked. The notes cite printed page numbers. PDF numbers below are one-based image positions, supplied to help the reviewer reach the same page. OCR is a finding aid; where it omits a table, loses column alignment or gives an implausible name, the image controls or the cell stays held.

The OR is a post-war edited compilation containing documents of very different kinds: contemporary dispatches, later reports, revised casualty tables, retrospective letters, editor's notes and compiled rosters. A compilation's publication year is not a report's date. The notes distinguish those layers, including Lee's March 1863 report about June 1862 and Woodbury's December 1863 letter about November 1862. Neither later source becomes information already possessed by a principal in 1862.

Livermore, Dyer, Mahan and Ammen are later analytical/narrative works. Their arithmetic, force selection and judgments remain attributed. They do not independently verify all statements by the commanders they quote. **[I]** Choosing a later reconstruction for a reference comparison is a source-choice proposal, not a finding that an opposing contemporary report was a deliberate lie.

Inferences, arithmetic, model suggestions and proposed rulings carry **[I]**. A blank/held figure is not zero. No transcription here supplies a gate signature, acceptance of a checkpoint band, personality score or implementation instruction outside research scope.

### Page mapping cautions

- **Mahan:** the retained PDF is image-only for these pages. The Archive DjVu XML supplies reading text; physical-page requests were checked against the printed headers. Maps/unnumbered leaves interrupt the offset. Printed 9 is PDF 30, while printed 53 is PDF 76. The OCR misreads the header of printed 108 as 103; the rendered header was checked. `mahan-header-map.png` retains the comparison.
- **Ammen:** maps interrupt the numbering. For example, printed 172 is PDF 198, but printed 173 is PDF 203. The XML mapping must account for two scan covers excluded from the access PDF. Earlier physical-position assumptions were corrected before drafting the final citations.
- **OR XXI:** this physical copy lacks printed 47–62 and 537–546 according to the retained item metadata. The actual cited headers at printed 83–86 and 171–172 were checked. Do not extend their offset across the missing leaves.
- **OR V:** printed 290 is PDF 300, not PDF 302. Early extracts made with the wrong offset remain in the working archive for provenance but are not the source for the final Ball's Bluff claims.
- **OR XII.2:** the correct item has the `_0` suffix. The similarly named item without that suffix is not this battle-report volume.

## Research residuals

The notes deliberately do not assert complete division-by-division strength returns for every engagement, final paired losses for every smaller action, a consensus Antietam Confederate strength, a statistical blockade interception rate, or the exact calendar key of the Pocotaligo attempt from Ammen's undated pages. The bridge sequence is now supported by dated messages printed in Burnside's later report; independent quartermaster/engineer reconciliation remains a follow-up. These gaps do not invalidate the qualified evidence already read, and they are not filled from memory.
