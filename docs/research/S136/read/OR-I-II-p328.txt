HDQRS.  DEPARTMENT  NORTHEASTERN  VIRGINIA,
Arlington,  August  12,  1861.
COLONEL  :  My  attention  lias  been  called  by  those  interested  to  two
omissions  in  my  report  of  the  battle  of  the  21st  ultimo  near  Manassas,
and  I  ask  leave  to  make  the  following  corrections,  wishing  that  they  bo
made  part  of  my  original  report :
1.  In  speaking  of  the  retreat,  I  mentioned  that  it  was  covered  by
Colonel  Blenker's  brigade.    I  should  have  said  Colonel  Eichardson's
and  Colonel  Blenker's  brigades.    The   former  was  on  the  left  of  the
Centreville  ridge,  and  the  latter  in  front  of  it,  on  the  Warrenton  road.
Each  covered  the  retreat  of  those  on  the  respective  roads  to  the  common
point — Centreville ;  from  there  to  the  rear  Colonel  Eichardson  was  be
hind,  and  covered  the  main  body.
2.  In  speaking  of  the  action  on  the  left  at  Blackburn's  Ford,  I  men
tioned  Colonel  Eichardson's  and  Major  Hunt's  reports  as  giving  the
account  of  what  transpired  in  that  direction.     In  this  connection  I
omitted  to  mention  Col.  T.  A.  Davies7  report,  and  now -.-beg  to  refer  to
it  as  necessary  to  a  full  account  of  this  part  of  the  battle.    He  was  en
gaged  to  the  left  of  Eichardson,  and  repulsed  an  attempt  of  the  enemy
to  turn  our  left.
I  have  the  honor  to  be,  very  respectfully,  your  most  obedient  servant,
IEVIN  MCDOWELL,
Brigadier- General,  Commanding.
Lieut.  Col.  E.  D.  TOWNSEND,
Assistant  Adjutant- General,  Headquarters  of  the  Army.
