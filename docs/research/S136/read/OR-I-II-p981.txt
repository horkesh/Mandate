EICHMOND,  July  17,  1861.
General  J.  B.  FLOYD,  Wytheville,  Va.  :
Information  of  the  disaster  to  General  Garnett's  command  has  been
received,  and  McClellan  is  at  Huttonsville.  To  oppose  his  seizure  of
the  Yirginia  Central  Eailroad,  a  junction  of  >our  command  and  that
of  General  Garnett,  now  probably  at  Monterey,  is  desired.  You  will
advance  with  your  brigade  by  railroad  to  Salem,  thence  to  Jackson's
Eiver,  if  your  transportation  will  permit.  If  not,  to  Staunton.
S.  COOPER,
Adjutant  and  Inspector  General.
EICHMOND,  July  17,  1861.
Brig.  Gen.  H.  A.  WISE,  Charleston,  Kanawha  County,  Va.  :
GENERAL  :  Information  has  been  received  of  the  disastrous  retreat  of
Garnett's  command  to  Monterey  and  the  advance  of  McClellan's  to
Huttonsville.  The  aid  of  your  force  and  that  of  General  Floyd  becomes
important.  If  your  command  is  not  now  essential  in  Kanawha,  move  up
toward  Covingtou.  Communicate  with  General  Floyd,  who  is  ordered
to  proceed  in  that  direction.
S.  COOPEE,
Adjutant  and  Inspector  General.

McEae's  North  Carolina  regiment  goes  to  you  this  evening ;  Barks-
