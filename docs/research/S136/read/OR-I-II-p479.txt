HDQRS.  DEPARTMENT  OF  NORTHERN  VIRGINIA,
Centreville,  February  3,  18G2.
SIR  :  My  attention  has  been  called  to  the  fact  that  in  the  enumeration
of  the  officers  who  distinguished  themselves  in  the  battle  of  Mauassas
the  name  of  Col.  S.  B.  Gibbons,  commanding  the  Tenth  Virginia  Begi-
ment,  was  omitted.  This  omission  was  due  to  unaccountable  careless
ness,  and  is  a  matter  of  regret  and  mortification  to  me.  I  beg  that  it
may  be  corrected  in  my  report  on  file  in  your  office,  and  the  correction
published.  Colonel  Gibbons  and  his  gallant  regiment  played  an  impor
tant  part  at  a  critical  time,  and  injustice  to  them,  even  accidentally,
is  unpardonable.  Colonel  Elzey,  to  Avhose  brigade  Colonel  Gibbons
belongs,  made  honorable  mention  of  him  in  his  report.
Most  respectfully,  your  obedient  servant,
J.  E.  JOHNSTON,  General.
General  S.  COOPER,  Adjutant  and  Inspector  General.
