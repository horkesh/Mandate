## Proposed name, definition and evidence rule

**`confederate_independence` [I]: an expressed commitment to establishing or
sustaining the common Confederate polity and its collective independence.**
It can coexist with an objection to a Confederate appointment or with limits
on central power. It is distinct from a State's right to determine its own
course (`state_autonomy`) and from preserving or extending slavery
(`slavery_defence`). A preference for a State's separate independence, an
unspecified future confederacy, or Southern cooperation conditional on failure
of Union remedies does not alone establish this cell.

There are clear positive legs. Brown calls for the seceding States to unite on
a common platform. Perry asks Florida to prepare its share of troops and money
for the forthcoming common government's defense. Pickens expressly identifies a
common cause and government and gives that as his reason for awaiting its
decision about war. A. B. Moore expressly welcomes Confederate control and
promises aid in organizing its defense. Davis's period inaugural commits to
maintaining the Confederacy's separate existence and independence.
[Brown, January 5, OR IV-I pp.18–19](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079579524/page/n33/mode/1up);
[Perry, February 2, pp.85–88](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079579524/page/n100/mode/1up);
[Pickens, February 27, OR I-I pp.258–259](https://archive.org/details/cu31924077725913/page/n277/mode/1up);
[A. B. Moore, March 4, OR IV-I pp.120–122](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079579524/page/n135/mode/1up);
[Davis, February 18 address entered February 19, p.104](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079579524/page/n119/mode/1up).

The charter-based findings are a specific inference for review: Bartow, Howell
Cobb, Memminger, Stephens and Toombs personally subscribe the February 8
undertaking to establish the common provisional government. Their own adopted
text supplies the commitment; their office, an anonymous State vote or later
military service does not. Reagan's leg is the March 2 record of his arrival
and the Texas delegates' actual oath to support the charter and signing of the
roll, rather than an authorization to sign. Wigfall's own March 4 telegram
makes Confederate authorization of any Sumter attack essential.
[Charter preamble and subscriptions, OR IV-I pp.92,99](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079579524/page/n107/mode/1up);
[Reagan, JCCS I p.97](https://archive.org/details/journalofcongres00conf/page/n106/mode/1up);
[Wigfall, OR I-I p.261](https://archive.org/details/cu31924077725913/page/n280/mode/1up).

**This distinction does not detach the common polity from slavery.** Brown's
same letter explicitly identifies the pro-slavery States' common institutions
and destiny. The provisional charter regulates the slave trade and requires
the return of people escaping enslavement, including State compensation in
the stated cases. Those provisions remain visible in the retained context.
The proposed value is not an alternative account of secession's causes, a
moral endorsement, or permission to drop slavery content. 0074's `costOnly`,
attribution and sensitive-history requirements remain unchanged.
[OR IV-I pp.18–19,94,98](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079579524/page/n109/mode/1up).

Every positive or contrary finding is based on a document **before 5 March
1861**, under 0085/0087. A verbatim period document printed later counts by its
document date; later editorial characterizations do not. 0074's own-motive
rule also applies. Thus Semmes's procurement work, Walker's transmission of
the President's instructions, a nomination of Ellett, or Twiggs's account of
surrender to Texas does not automatically yield the new value. A subscribed
political undertaking is treated differently from carrying out a policy or
accepting an assigned task. The CSV exposes this interpretive boundary for
the lead, rather than quietly substituting faction membership.
[Semmes, February 28, OR IV-I pp.118–119](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079579524/page/n133/mode/1up);
[Walker, March 1, OR I-I pp.259–260](https://archive.org/details/cu31924077725913/page/n278/mode/1up);
[Ellett nomination, February 25, JCCS I p.85](https://archive.org/details/journalofcongres00conf/page/n94/mode/1up);
[Twiggs, February 19, OR I-I pp.503–504](https://archive.org/details/cu31924077725913/page/n522/mode/1up).

boldness, candour, regard or personal-reputation rating is proposed. His July
protest and April refusal supply none of these starting cells.

Document No.1. The [retained primary text](S113/source-pages.json) and
