## Evidence boundaries

Legal authority, requested men, enrollment, muster, men credited and field effectives are distinct. An exemption class is not a count of exempt men. Later editorial totals have no assumed contemporaneous receipt date. Report dates and accounting windows remain separate in the economic files. All computed quantities and modelling choices are marked **[I]**. No undocumented parameter, daily probability, foreign pressure increment, army stock or population-management mechanism is supplied.

Sensitive laws remain attributed historical evidence. Source racial categories and legal claims over enslaved people do not become neutral narrator categories or resource values. The separate gate reviewer owns wording, placement and signatures after the sprint.
