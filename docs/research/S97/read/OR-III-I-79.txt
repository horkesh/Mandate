HEADQUARTERS  OF  THE  ARMY,
Washington,  April  30,  1S61.
Lieutenant-General  Scott  does  not  think  it  necessary  to  occupy  the
forts  in  Massachusetts  by  volunteers,  nor  does  he  think  favorably  of
the  plan  proposed  within.
Respectfully  submitted,  by  command :
E.  I).  TOWNSEND,
Assistant  Adjutant-  General.
BOSTON,  MASS.,  April  17,  1861.
Hon.  SIMON  CAMERON,
Secretary  of  War :
One  regiment  starts  at  0  by  rail  for  Washington;  another  to-night,
half  by  propeller  Spaulding,  half  by  steamer  State  of  Maine,  from  Fall
River,  both  direct  for  Fort  Monroe.  Steamer  State  of  Maine  can  take
the  whole  up  river  to  Washington  if  notified  at  Fort  Monroe,  if  you
[desire]  that  regiment  in  Washington.  Another  regiment  starts  to
morrow  by  rail  for  Washington,  another  within  three  days.  Butler  is
brigadier.  We  work  as  fast  as  men  can  be  properly  equipped  and
moved.
JOHN  A.  ANDREW,
Governor  of  Massachusetts.
