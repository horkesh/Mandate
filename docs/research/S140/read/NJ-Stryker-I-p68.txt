OFFICERS  AND  MEN  OF  NEW  JERSEY
First  Regiment  -  Infantry— Volunteers.
The  First  Regiment  was  organized  under  authority  of  General  Orders,  No.  15.  War  Department,  Adjutant  General's  Office,  Washington,  IX
C.,  May  4th,  1801.  The  Regiment  was  fully  organized,  equipped  and  officered  by  the  18th  day  of  May,  and  on  the  21st  day  of  the  same  month
was  duly  mustered  into  the  service  of  the  United  States,  lor  three  years,  at  Camp  Olden.  Trenton.  N'.  J.,  by  MAJOR  THEODORE  T.  S.  I.AIHI.KY,
U.  S.  Army.  It  left  the  State,  June  28th,  1801,  with  a  full  complement  of  men.  Officers,  38  ;  Noncommissioned  Officers  and  Privates,  990.
Total,  1.031.  In  the  latter  part  of  18(13,  Company  K  failing  to  muster  a  sufficient  number  of  men.  and  being  without  officers,  was  disbanded,
the  enlisted  men  having  been  assigned  by  transfer  to  Battery  A,  First  Regiment,  New  Jersey  Artillery,  and  to  the  several  companies  in  the
Regiment.  In  compliance  with  instructions  from  War  Department,  Adjutant  General's  Office,  Washington,  D.  C.,  September  1 1th,  1803,  authority
was  issued  for  the  raising  of  a  new  company  to  fill  this  vacancy,  and  on  the  3dth  day  of  January,  1M>4,  a  full  company  of  three  Commissioned
Officers,  and  ninety-two  Non-Commissioned  Officers  and  Privates  was  mustered  into  the  service  of  the  United  States,  for  three  years,  at  Trent'  n,
N.  J.,  by  SAMUEI,  CAXRY,  1st  Lieut.  Fourth  Artillery,  L".  S.  Army,  and  were  immediately  forwarded  to  join  the  Regiment,  in  tie  field.

consolidated  into  what  was  known  as  Companies  A,  B  and  C,  First  Battalion,  and  were  mustered  out  with  that  organization,  at  Hall's  Hill.  Ya.,
June  29th,  1805,  by  MAJOR  A.  M.  TYLER,  Assistant  Commissary  of  Musters,  First  Division,  Sixth  Army  Corps,  under  the  provisions  of
