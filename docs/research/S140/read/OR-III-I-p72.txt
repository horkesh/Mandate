Two  of  my  staff  spent  last  Saturday  in  new  experiments  of  the  most satisfactory  character  with  Schenkl's  new  invention  in  projectiles,  and so  extraordinary  was  the  tiring;  that  I  have  directed  eighteen  guns  to  be rifled  and  the  projectiles  to  be  made.  May  I  commend  this  invention to  the  examination  of  the  United  States  Government! I  am  happy  to  add  that  I  find  the  amplest  proof  of  warm  devotion  to the  country's  cause  on  every  hand  to-day. I  have  the  honor  to  be,  your  obedient  servant, JOHN  A.
