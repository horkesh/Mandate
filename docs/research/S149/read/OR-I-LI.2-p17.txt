Headquarters  State  of  South  Carolina,
April  19,  1861.
[Brig.  Gen.  G.  T.  Beauregard:]
My  Dear  General  :  I  inclose  you  the  within  and  I  desire  prompt
action.  I  therefore  suggest  Colonel  Gregg’s  regiment  and  Colonel  Pet¬
tigrew’s.  I  desire  to  offer  them  the  honor  and  to  allow  them  to  go  or
not  on  this  special  service.  They  deserve  the  honor.  If  you  approve
of  it,  let  my  aide,  the  bearer  of  this,  transmit  immediately  to  Colonel
Gregg  and  Colonel  Pettigrew  the  inclosed  offer  to  those  regiments,  and
if  they  accept  please  order  them  to  report  immediately  in  the  city  for
instant  departure  to  Korfolk.  Colonel  Pettigrew’s  regiment  is  relieved
to-day  by  Colonel  Jenkins’ regiment,  stationed  on  Sullivan’s  Island,  with
1,150  men — a  fine  regiment;  so  there  is  no  difficulty  there.
In  great  haste,  yours,  truly,
F.  W.  PICKENS.
