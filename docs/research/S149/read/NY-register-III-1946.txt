EIGHTEENTH REGIMENT OF INFANTRY. New York State Rifles ; Riflemen. This regiment, Col. William A. Jackson, was accepted by the State and received its numerical designation May 13, 1861 ; was organized at Albany, and there mustered in the service of the United States for two years May 17, 1861. May II, 1863, the three years' men of the regiment were transferred to the I2ist N. Y. Volunteers. The companies were recruited principally: A and E at Schenectady; B, F, H and I at Albany and immediate vicinity ; C at Fishkill ; D — Walkill Guards — at Middlctown and in Sullivan county; G at Canandaigua, and K at Ogdensburg. The regiment left the State June 19, 1861 ; served at Washington, D. C., from June 21, 1861 ; in 2d Brigade, sth Division, Army of Northeastern Virginia, from July 13, 1861 ; in Franklin's Brigade, Division of Potomac, from August 4, 1861 ; in Newton's Brigade, Franklin's Division, Army of the Potomac, from October 15, 1861 ; in 3d Brigade, ist Division, 1st Corps, Army of the Potomac, from March 13, 1862; in the 3d Brigade, ist Division, 6th Corps, Army of the Potomac, from May, 1862, and, commanded by Col. George R. Myers, was honorably discharged and mustered out at Albany May 28, 1863.
