# Research: name collisions for the product title and a "Mandate: ..." series form

Date: 2026-11-14. Status: research note (not canon). Not legal advice: this reports what public registers and stores showed on this date. **Trademark clearance needs a trademark attorney before launch or promotion.**

**Conventions.**
- Citation keys in [brackets] resolve in the Sources list at the end.
- **[S]** = seen only in a search-result snippet; the page itself was not read.
- **[I]** = our interpretation.
- **Not searched** means the register or store could not be queried automatically; the manual search is listed in section 9.

**Method.** Steam via its public store-search and app-details endpoints; itch.io and GOG via their public search pages and catalog; USPTO through the public "Trademark search" page in a browser (typing into the search box, as a person would); domains by DNS lookup only (no paid service, no registrar query); other media by web search. Nothing was contacted, registered or submitted.

| # | Title | Collision risk | Main reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *By Order of the President* | **Low** | No game, no film, no live mark found anywhere; .com has no DNS records. Two novels carry the title, so web search is mildly crowded. |
| 2 | *Mandate: By Order of the President* | **Medium** | The subtitle is clean, but the front word inherits every "Mandate" collision below. |
| 3 | *Mandate: 1861* | **Medium** | Title itself is unused, but it copies the "Mandate 1600: The East" pattern, a historical grand strategy series on Steam, and "Mandate" is crowded (section 6). |

**The Mandate (2015) is not the main risk.** It is dead, and its US trademark application is abandoned (section 2). The main risk is the many other "Mandate" games.

## 2. "The Mandate" (the 2013 Kickstarter, target 2015)

- **What it was.** A co-operative space-opera RPG with squad and ship combat, by Perihelion Interactive. The Kickstarter ran in 2013 and raised $701,010 from 16,408 backers [GW-DEAD]. The brief's "2015" is the original release estimate, not the campaign year [GW-DEAD], [S] [PCGN-KS].
- **Never released.** VideoGameGeek lists it "UNRELEASED GAME – VAPORWARE" [VGG]. RPGWatch lists it as cancelled [S] [RPGW].
- **Status in August 2017.** No developer update since April 2017, the developer's own website offline, a backer reporting documents that suggest Perihelion was dissolved and "The Mandate trademark not renewed since April", and the project site and forum due to expire on 19 August 2017 [GW-DEAD] (read in full).
- **Corporate status (unverified).** One later thread claims Perihelion was formed in Luxembourg solely for this project [S]. The US trademark owner is "Perihelion Interactive, S.a.r.l. (Luxembourg)", which matches [USPTO-Mandate]. The Luxembourg company register (RCS) was not searched.
- **US trademark.** THE MANDATE, serial 86561595, classes 9, 16, 28 and 41 (computer game programs, video game software, and related goods), owner Perihelion Interactive S.a.r.l.: **DEAD / ABANDONED** [USPTO-Mandate] (read in the register).
- **EU trademark.** Not searched. A Luxembourg owner suggests an EU mark may exist; check TMview (section 9).
- **Still sold?** Not on Steam: the Steam search for "the mandate" returns no such game [STEAM-API]. itch.io has several unrelated entries called "The Mandate" or "THE MANDATE" (a simulation, a solo journaling TTRPG) [ITCH-M], but none is Perihelion's. I found no store listing for Perihelion's game. Whether any rights holder survives is unknown.
- **Residual risk.** Low for the game itself. It still crowds search results for "The Mandate" and has an old 16,000-backer grievance attached to the name [I]. A buyer who remembers it could confuse a new "Mandate" game with it, or suspect the new one of the same fate.

## 4. Trademarks

### 4.1 US register (USPTO "Trademark search", public page, searched 2026-11-14)

**Search quality caveat.** The public search is a loose word match. A search for "mandate" returned 71 records, which I read in full. A search for "war powers", even in quotation marks, returned 3,551 records ranked mainly by the word "POWERS", and the first page showed no WAR POWERS mark. That is **not evidence that no such mark exists**: a person must use the structured or advanced search (section 9). "By Order of the President" and "Mandate 1861" were not run through the register.

**"MANDATE" findings [USPTO-Mandate]:**
- **Live in or near our classes:**
  - **MANDATE OF MAYHEM**, serials 99818758 and 99824434, **LIVE / PENDING**, MANDATE OF MAYHEM LLC (Indiana). Classes 9, 25 and 28, "Downloadable video game programs". Pending applications, so an examiner has not decided on them. Not identical to "Mandate", but it is a game mark with the same lead word.
  - **MANDATE**, serial 50012465, **LIVE / PENDING**, Sean Lee (individual, USA). Class 42, "software as a service". Not games, but the same word as the sole mark.
- **Live, other goods:** MANDATE (class 12, bicycles; Rossignol), MANDATE PLUS (class 5), ELD MANDATE (class 9, logging-device software), MANDATE FOR LEADERSHIP (classes 9 and 16; The Heritage Foundation), and others. None cover games.
- **Dead:** THE MANDATE (Perihelion, section 2); MANDATE (class 9, three abandoned software marks from 1990s and 2000s); MANDATE (class 28, Salomon, cancelled).
- **Pattern:** no live registered MANDATE mark in class 9 for games turned up in this search, but two live pending game and software applications use the word. A single-word MANDATE mark for a game would face an uphill examination against pending filings, plus every owner of a "MANDATE-formative" mark in classes 9, 28 and 41 [I]. The word is also descriptive-adjacent (a "mandate" is a political authority), which makes it weak, and so it is hard to protect exclusively as well as crowded.

### 4.2 EU register (EUIPO eSearch plus, TMview)
**Not searched.** Both are JavaScript applications that need a person at the keyboard. Exact URLs are in section 9. The most useful check is whether Perihelion's *THE MANDATE* was ever filed in the EU and, if so, whether it is still live.

## 6. Other media and searchability

### 6.1 *By Order of the President*
- **Books:** a thriller by Michael Kilian (1986) in which the president is shot at Gettysburg, reissued; and the first novel (2004, G.P. Putnam's Sons) in W.E.B. Griffin's Presidential Agent series, with print and audio editions [S] [PW-BO], [S] [GRIFFIN-BO]. No film, TV series or game found in the same search [S].
- **Searchability:** medium. A web search for the exact phrase returns mostly book listings. A game would not rank first on launch but is easy to tell apart ("game" in the query). The phrase is long (five words), and it describes exactly what the player does, which suits the design [I].

### 6.2 *War Powers*
- **Politics and law:** the War Powers Resolution, War Powers Clause, War Powers Act of 1941 and a textbook *War Powers: The Politics of Constitutional Authority* all share the phrase [WIKI-OS]. A search returns almost entirely news and law about the Resolution, which is in the news whenever a US president uses force.
- **Media:** a 2011 educational video titled *War Powers: The President and Congress* in a Constitution series [S] [ALLMOVIE]. No novel, album or film with the exact title found [S].
- **Games:** none found on Steam, itch.io, GOG or in a BGG web search. Searchability: **high** problem, since the phrase is a live news term and a game would sit beneath it for years [I]. It would be a poor search target even with no collision.

### 6.3 *Mandate* and the combinations
- "Mandate: 1861" and "Mandate: By Order of the President" have no exact matches on any store checked. A search for "mandate game" returns the games in section 3 plus The Mandate (2015).
- The Steam pattern *Mandate 1600: The East* (a **year plus subtitle**) is what a "Mandate: 1861" would look like, from the same genre.

### 6.4 Political baggage [I]
In the US, "Mandate" is the name of a recent political report: *Mandate for Leadership* (The Heritage Foundation), registered in classes 9 and 16 [USPTO-Mandate]. Search results for "mandate" plus "president" will lean toward politics and elections rather than games, and an American-politics strategy game called "Mandate" may read as taking a side. The 2020s "mask mandate" and "vaccine mandate" usage also pollutes the word (an itch.io game is literally called *Mask Mandate*) [ITCH-M].

## 7. Recommendation

**Use *By Order of the President* as the public title; keep "Mandate" as the engine's codename and do not build a public series brand on it.**

- It is the only candidate with **no game, no live mark and no obvious domain collision** in everything I could search. Its only competition is two thrillers in a different medium.
- It fits the design (hard rule 1): the player gives orders to people, and the phrase names exactly that.
- A strong subtitle carries the 1861 setting where needed (for example *By Order of the President: 1861*), and no word in the title is shared with a crowded game genre.
- If the owner insists on the "Mandate: X" form, **Mandate: 1861 is the weakest of the "Mandate" options on Steam**, because *Mandate 1600: The East* occupies the same slot; *Mandate: By Order of the President* is the safest of the three Mandate forms, since the long subtitle is unique.
- **For a later series brand**, prefer a coined or composed word over the bare noun "Mandate". The engine can keep its codename internally.
- Before spending on art or promotion, take the shortlist to a trademark attorney. They can run the structured US search, EUIPO and WIPO, and advise on classes 9, 28 and 41.

## 8. Findings outside my scope

- **The studio name's domain is parked.** pyrrhic.com resolves to the same aftermarket address as mandate.com, so it is registered and probably for sale. pyrrhicgames.com and pyrrhicstudio.com have no DNS records. The Pyrrhic name itself was not checked in the trademark registers or game stores.
- **A pending "MANDATE OF MAYHEM" video-game filing** (serials 99818758 and 99824434, Indiana LLC) is the nearest live game mark. Worth watching even if the series idea is dropped.
- **Political reading of "Mandate" in the US** (section 6.4) bears on the owner's brand voice, not only on clearance.
- **The Mandate (2015) carries a crowdfunding-failure reputation** [GW-DEAD]. A new game of the same name may attract comparisons or hostile comments from its backers.
- **The Steam and itch.io search results are time-sensitive.** *Mandate 1600: The East* and *MANDATE* (ZiBa) are unreleased and may change names or ship before the owner's launch. Re-run the Steam search before committing.

## 9. Manual searches for the owner

Run these by hand; each page needs a person at the keyboard.

1. **USPTO:** <https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/>. Use the "Wordmark" dropdown or the advanced search rather than the quick box. Run: `war powers`, `by order of the president`, `mandate 1861`, `mandate war powers`, and `mandate` limited to classes 9, 28, 41. Check serial numbers 99818758, 99824434 (MANDATE OF MAYHEM) and 50012465 (MANDATE, class 42).
2. **EUIPO eSearch plus:** <https://euipo.europa.eu/eSearch/>. Same terms, Nice classes 9, 28, 41. Also look for *THE MANDATE* by Perihelion Interactive.

6. **MobyGames:** <https://www.mobygames.com/search/?q=mandate> (and the other two terms).
7. **Console and other stores:** PlayStation Store, Xbox, Nintendo eShop, Epic, App Store and Google Play, each by title.
8. **Steam, on the day you decide:** <https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=mandate> (and the other terms), to catch new releases.

## Update, 2026-11-16: the register searches for the title

Run by the lead on the owner's go-ahead, in a browser, for *By Order of the President*. These are searches, not a clearance: the attorney's opinion is still needed.

**No mark "BY ORDER OF THE PRESIDENT" was found in any register searched.**
- **USPTO** (tmsearch, field search on the combined mark): nothing for `CM:"by order of the president"` or `CM:"order of the president"`. Controls: `CM:"executive order"` returns 11, and `CM:"the mandate"` finds serial 86561595.
  - **Caution:** the USPTO quick search drops "by", so `CM:"by order"` returned nothing although USPTO marks beginning "BY ORDER OF THE" exist (below, found through TMview and WIPO). Use the other registers for phrases starting with "by".
- **TMview** (EUIPO, UKIPO, USPTO and most national offices): no rows for "by order of the president" or "order of the president". Control: "peaky blinders" returns 70.
- **WIPO Global Brand Database:** the closest marks for "by order of the" are listed below, and none adds "president".
- **Steam** (store search API): nothing for "by order of the president", "order of the president" or "president civil war". "by order" finds only *By Strange Orders* (app 4029890).
- **BoardGameGeek:** no items for "by order of the president".
- **MobyGames:** not checked. Its bot check stopped the browser, and it was not bypassed. A person should run it.
- **Domains** (DNS only; nothing registered or reserved): byorderofthepresident.com and .net, byorderofthepresidentgame.com, pyrrhicgames.com, pyrrhicstudio.com and pyrrhic.games all have no DNS records.

**The one matter for the attorney: the *Peaky Blinders* "BY ORDER OF THE" family.**

| Mark | Owner | Office and number | Classes | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| BY ORDER OF THE | Garrison Drama Limited | UKIPO UK00003362841, filed 21 Dec 2018 | 3, 8, **9**, 14, 16, 18, 21, 24, 25, **28**, 29, 30, 32, 33, 34, **41**, 43, 44 | registered 12 Apr 2019 |
| BY ORDER OF THE | Caryn Mandabach Productions Limited (WIPO) / Garrison Drama Limited (TMview) | USPTO 6156320 (serial 88482071) | 25, 32, 33 | registered 22 Sep 2020 |
| BY ORDER OF THE SHELBY COMPANY LIMITED | Garrison Drama Limited | EUIPO 018191012; UKIPO UK00003418070; USPTO 88563707 | clothing, food, drink and similar; no class 9 or 41 | registered |

- **The UK registration covers class 9** (which includes computer and video game software) and classes 28 and 41. It is built on the series' catchphrase "By order of the Peaky Blinders". Whether *By Order of the President* for a video game is confusingly similar in the UK, in the light of that phrase's reputation, is exactly the question for the trademark attorney.
- **In the US and the EU,** the family is not registered in classes 9, 28 or 41, per these searches.
- **Nearby but different:** "Order of the Prince" (Koei Tecmo Games, Japan 6028041, class 41).
