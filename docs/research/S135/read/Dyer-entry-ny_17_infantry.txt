17th REGIMENT INFANTRY.— ("WESTCHESTER CHASSEURS.") Organized at New York City and mustered in for two years' service May 28, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, June 21. Attached to Mansfield's Command, Dept. of Washington, June to August, 1861. Garrison, Fort Ellsworth, Defences of Washington, to October, 1861. Butterfield's Brigade, Porter's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
