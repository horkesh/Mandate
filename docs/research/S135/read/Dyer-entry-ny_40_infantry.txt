40th REGIMENT INFANTRY— "MOZART REGIMENT," "CONSTITUTION GUARD." Organized at Yonkers, N. Y., June 27, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, July 4, 1861. Duty near Alexandria till August 4. Attached to Howard's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. Sedgwick's Brigade, Heintzelman's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
