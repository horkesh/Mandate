# Brief addendum: sourcing a person's bloc (lane 1.11r3, decision 0087)

Same rules as BRIEF-resource.md (read it and BRIEF-common.md first), applied to the `bloc` column of the cast table. A bloc is a start-class judgment: the pack puts each man in a political or institutional bloc at its first start (5 March 1861), and the bloc needs a citation of a CONTEMPORARY PRIMARY document dated BEFORE 5 March 1861 that shows the affiliation (not a later memoir, not a reference work's summary). Your batch file lists the people and the bloc the pack gives each (the bloc vocabulary: `army`, `confederate`, `radicals`, `moderates`, `war_democrats`, `border_unionists`, `peace_democrats`). Meaning: `army` = the regular army's officer corps and its interests (a man serving as an officer of the United States Army or Navy on 5 March 1861); `confederate` = the secession side's bloc (a man who by 5 March had taken his state out or joined the Confederate government or forces); `radicals`, `moderates` = the Republican wings; `war_democrats` and `peace_democrats` = Democratic wings; `border_unionists` = Unionists of the border slave states.
Evidence that counts, by bloc:
- `army`: the Official Army Register for 1861 (the printed register of 1 January 1861, officers by regiment), the Navy Register of 1861 (`NavyReg1861` is already a pack source), General Orders and Army Lists of Jan-Feb 1861: a man listed in service on those dates. This is a fact of service dated before the start: use it, dated `1861-01-01` or the register's own date.
- `confederate`: a resignation of commission dated before 5 March (Army Register changes, Cullum, Heitman give dates but are reference works: prefer the resignation letter or the Official Register's list of resignations, a state convention's ordinance and roll, a state legislature's act, Congress members' farewell speeches in the Congressional Globe of January 1861, membership of the Confederate Provisional Congress (JCCS, Feb 1861), a Confederate commission or nomination dated before 5 March (JCCS pp. 73, 112 and others)). A man who joined the Confederacy only in April or later has NO pre-start leg for `confederate`: say so and propose HOLD (or propose the bloc supported by his pre-start position if there is one, e.g. army).
- `radicals`, `moderates`, `war_democrats`, `peace_democrats`, `border_unionists`: his own vote or speech or office before 5 March (Congressional Globe on the Crittenden Compromise and the Committee of Thirteen/Thirty-three votes, the Chicago Republican convention proceedings of 1860 (`CHI-1860` is a pack source), the Charleston/Baltimore Democratic convention proceedings, a state legislature journal, a governor's message, his letters), showing the wing. A contemporary newspaper naming his faction counts if it is dated before 5 March.
For each person: KEEP (a pre-start contemporary leg supports the pack's bloc), CHANGE (the leg supports a different bloc: name it), or HOLD (none found). Output CSV `docs/research/_resource/<batch>.csv` with header exactly: `person,bloc,decision,proposed_bloc,bloc_cites,basis,prior_bloc`; `bloc_cites` is PROVENANCE-style (`KEY locator dated YYYY-MM-DD` joined with `+`; existing keys from `packs/union-1861/PROVENANCE.json` where one fits, else `NEW:<KEY> ...` described in the note); no `;` in any cell. Also the note `<batch>.md` and the 200-word report as in BRIEF-resource.md.
