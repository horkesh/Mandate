## What differs
- Your batch file lists cells that still rest on no contemporary leg because they cite ONLY later reference works: the Biographical Directory (BDAC), Appletons', Heitman, Cullum, Callahan, Wright, the registers. **A reference work alone is never a leg for a judgment** (a reputation, competence, nerve, boldness, candour, regard, a regard toward another person, a value, a trait). It may state a FACT (a rank, an office, a service, a date), and nothing here needs a fact. Find a contemporary primary document, or HOLD.
- **Reputation** is how OTHERS judged the man, in a document dated before 5 March 1861: a newspaper of the day, a letter or diary of the time about him, a speech or report of someone else about him, a vote of an assembly of others about him (an election to a trust is the electors' judgment). His own words are not a reputation.
