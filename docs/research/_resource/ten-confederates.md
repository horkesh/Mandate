# Ten coded Confederate blocs at 5 March 1861

2026-11-19. R45 research recommendations for the lead. No pack data changed.

**Recommendation [I]: none for all ten at the start.** Here *none* means leave the personal bloc unassigned because the required fact of having joined this side by the start is not established. It does not mean neutral political convictions, opposition to slavery, or lifelong Unionism. Nor does holding a US office by itself prove loyalty. No alternative factional bloc is proposed without its own eligible evidence.

Facts of office/service may use the permitted factual reference works. Judgments of political character may not be derived from those works. A later appointment proves that appointment, not the earliest date of allegiance. These distinctions matter especially for Polk, Floyd and Bulloch. Their precise personal commitment on 5 March remains less fully documented than their later service. The recommendation is deliberately conservative rather than an assertion that they had made no private commitment.

## Source copies and limits

- [JCCS volume 1](https://archive.org/details/journalofcongres00conf), *Journal of the Congress of the Confederate States*, official later printing of proceedings. Read the documents themselves, especially pp307,473,631. Raw OCR retained by the earlier lane at `gaps/gaps-D1-confed/journalofcongres00conf.txt`. These nominations are official documents, distinct from the banned Davis memoir. They are used only for dated service facts.
- [Cullum volume 1](https://archive.org/details/biographicalregi01cull) and [volume 2](https://archive.org/details/biographicalregi02cull), factual service entries, not reputation evidence. Read no477, no1119 and no1216 directly in the retained OCR. See A1 for the retained source root.
- Appletons, *Cyclopaedia of American Biography*, volumes 2-4, retained OCR `gaps-D1-confed/app2.txt` to `app4.txt`. The Hunter and Letcher **office facts only** above rest on the named printed entries. No character assessment from Appletons is adopted. The raw OCR contains substantial errors in dates and words, which are not silently corrected into new facts.
- [Rebellion Record volume 1](https://archive.org/details/rebellionrecord01moor), Diary entry of 29 December 1860. Read in retained `rs1/rr1.txt`. The date-entry locator is used because this extracted calendar's printed running page was not securely recovered.
- BDAC entries are unpaginated official government reference entries, cited by person/entry and exact URL rather than invented page numbers. The Hunter and Letcher House URLs failed direct retrieval. Their search snippets are not used as factual evidence. The Army Register PDF also failed web retrieval, and A1 uses the already retained copy instead.

## Proposed ruling

**[I] Require positive dated evidence of joining the side for a start `confederate` fact bloc.** Leave these ten blank in the apply until the lead rules on this table. Reject (a) preserving all ten automatically because a fact bloc needs no judgment leg, (b) treating later service as timeless, and (c) replacing the blank with a speculative political faction. This is a recommendation to the lead, not an amendment of the hand-over's existing rule that fact HOLDs stand as coded.

The present `apply.js --dry` still leaves all ten coded Confederate. **These recommendations are not yet implemented by the apply.** Its output must not be described as having adopted them.
