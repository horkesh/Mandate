[Inclosure.] At  a  county  court,  lielcl  for  the  county  of  Putnam  at  the  court-house thereof  on  Monday,  the  22d  of  April,  1861,  it  was  ordered  that  the sum  of  $3,000  be  appropriated  for  the  purpose  of  purchasing  arms  for the  people  of  said  county,  to  be  used  for  defending  themselves  and  the State  of  Yirginia,  and  that  said  sum  be  levied  for  and  collected  in  the usual  manner  in  which  the  county  levies  of  said  county  are  made  and collected,  and  also  the  sum  of  $200  to  pay  the  expenses  of  a  special messenger  to  Eichmond  City,  to  be  levied  and  collected  in  the  same manner.  But  this  levy  of  said  $3,000  is  only  to  take  effect  in  the  event that  the  Governor  of  the  Commonwealth  of  Yirginia  fails  or  refuses  to supply  said  arms.  And  it  is  further  ordered,  that  Maj.  L.  L.  Bronaugh be,  and  he  is  hereby,  appointed  special  commissioner  to  wait  upon  the Governor  of  Yirginia  for  the  purpose  aforesaid.
