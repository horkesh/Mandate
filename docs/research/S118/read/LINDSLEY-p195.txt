Henry  county  furnished  to  the  Confederate  armies  two  regiments  of  infantry, the  Fifth  and  Forty-sixth,  and  Co.  F  of  the  One  Hundred  and  Fifty-fourth  Regi- ment of  Infantry,  and  companies  of  cavalry  to  various  regiments  sufficient  for  an- other regiment. The  Fifth  Infantry  was  organized  at  Paris  on  the  20th  of  May,  1361,  by  the  elec- tion of  W.  E.  Travis  as  Colonel:  J.  D.  C.  Atkins,  Lieutenant-colonel:  \Vm.  C.  Swor. Major.  Robt.  T.  Clark,  M.D.,  and  F.  F.  Porter,  M.D.,  were  commissioned  as  Surgeon and  Assistant  Surgeon,  and  assigned  to  duty  with  this  regiment.  Calvin  D.  Venable was  appointed  Adjutant,  and  Hugh  P.  Dunlap,  Sergeant-major;  Samuel  E.  Barbee was  commissioned  as  Captain  in  the  commissary  department,  and  O.  H.  Edwards as  Quartermaster  with  the  rank  of  Captain,  and  assigned  to  the  Fifth  Regiment; Surgeon  Clark  after  two  months  of  faithful  service  resigned,  and  was  succeeded  by Jo.  H.  Porter,  M.D.,  Captain  of  Co.  H,  who  was  commissioned  as  Surgeon;  Assist- ant Surgeon  F.  F.  Porter,  resigning  soon  thereafter,  was  succeeded  by  Thos.  C. Harcourt,  M.D.     The  Rev.  E.  E.  Hamilton  was  appointed  Chaplain. The  regiment  was  mustered  into  the  service  with  twelve  companies,  viz.:  A, Captain  B,  B.
