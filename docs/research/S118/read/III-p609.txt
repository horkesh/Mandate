I  desire  to  call  attention  to  the  provision  by  which  the  State  of  Ar- kansas agrees  to  furnish  the  troops  of  the  State  of  Arkansas  in  the  serv- ice of  the  Confederate  States  with  clothing  equal  in  amount  to  that required  by  the  regulations  of  the  Confederate  States  (the  same  as  that in  the  U.  S.  service),  and  for  which  the  State  of  Arkansas  is  to  receive from  the  Confederate  States  the  commutation  allowance  allowed  to soldiers  for  clothing  in  the  Confederate  service.* I  shall  leave  for  Pocahontas  to  day.
