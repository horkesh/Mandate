The Heitman work began with 223 unique selected entry extracts, then manual entries where OCR names or the index range missed a candidate. All nine numbered extract batches were read. Cullum's selected prewar passages were read in nine batches; truncated or ambiguous continuations remain qualified. Fresh manual readings supplied additional Cooper, Longstreet, Johnston and Rosecrans details. Callahan's selected entries and ambiguous alternatives were read separately. This is passage-level reading: a scan retained because an entry spans it does not mean every neighboring entry was read. No source's later praise is imported into another stat.

The complete cited-page inventory is the authority for locators; the ranges above describe reading, not a claim that each entire page was read. Reused S12/S22 page scans were copied to S38 only after matching their retained hashes. Every copied/downloaded cited page was hashed again. Earlier S12/S22 research statements were checked against the reread source where re-used in the new later-service tables; their original rows were not edited.

## Acquisition limits and corrections

- **1868 Cullum not read.** The two located Google volume IDs returned identical “image not available” placeholders, including corrected requests without the initially invalid signature. They are excluded from the source manifest. No 1868/1891 comparison is claimed.
- Archive metadata labelled one candidate as an 1868 volume, but the acquired title showed a **1930 supplement**. That candidate is excluded. The retained 1891 vol. III starts with later graduates and supplies no S38 facts.
- An Army 1860 Google result likewise returned a placeholder. A separate two-page GovInfo file is only a January 1861 transmittal letter, not the promised register, and is excluded from service evidence. The substantial official pay transcript above is labelled for what it contains. A complete 1860/January 1861 duty-register collation remains open.
- Archive OCR search offsets are character offsets: UTF-8 is decoded before slicing. Scan leaves, accessible image indices and printed pages are kept distinct. Cullum I printed p395 was checked against image n400; Cullum II p171 against n178. Callahan's raw leaves start at 1, and printed p92 was checked against n99. Wright's unlabelled OCR leaves 13–14 were checked against printed pp9–10. No generic page offset is assumed across books.
- The initial image-manifest extension found that an older OR page index lacked `image_index`. The correction uses the already retained, hashed page locator for that volume. Existing source bytes were reused; no expensive validation campaign was retried.
- Meigs's invalid OCR day and Lee's OCR year were corrected from the images and logged in [conflicts.csv](conflicts.csv). Other date disagreements remain disagreements. No guessed day or homonym is silently repaired.

[source-artifacts.json](source-artifacts.json) inventories 117 retained source/index/derivation files outside the repository, separately from the page manifest. Excluded acquisitions are retained for audit, not cited as service authority. Firecrawl returned a credit-limit error; discovery and acquisition continued through headless search/direct HTTP and local text/PDF tools. No browser, desktop or Computer Use was used.
