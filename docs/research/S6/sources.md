# S6 sources and reading record

20 November 2026. This is a document-only source catalogue, not a change to the pack's PROVENANCE registry. All paths below resolve under `C:/Pyrrhic/mandate-research-sources/S6/`. The row prefix `NEW:S6-` maps to the short keys below. Page numbers always mean **printed pages**, not PDF positions, OCR line numbers or archive image indices. A `+` joins distinct citations; their dates apply to their own documents.

The *Official Records* and congressional journal are United States government publications used as public-domain sources under 0033. The Lincoln and Sherman editions were published in 1894 and the Wright directory in 1911. Only the contemporary documents reproduced in those later letter editions supply judgment legs under 0087; editorial narrative does not. Wright supplies office facts only. No Welles recollection, Davis autobiography, Johnston memoir, modern copyrighted biography or search-result summary supplies a judgment here.

## Scan checks and corrections

There are **100 current JPEG page scans**, with sizes and SHA-256 hashes in the manifest. Original OCR, metadata, downloaded XML where available, and scandata remain beside them. Some OCR/XML or first-identifier downloads failed; their retained logs are acquisition evidence, not historical evidence. Search and fetch helper scripts are outside the repository. No scanned book image is committed here.

Critical image checks include OR I-5 p1053; I-9 pp75, 80, 97-98; I-10-2 p4; I-11-1 p61; I-19-2 p590; III-2 p584; IV-1 pp1082,1084; IV-2 pp56-57,59,178; Wright p153; JCCS II p74; Lincoln II pp118,245; and Sherman pp120-121. Other used passages were read in OCR/XML; retaining their scan does not imply every image was visually inspected. In particular, the manuscript originals behind later printed letters were not examined.

- Burnside's **14 February** detailed-report heading was checked at OR I-9 p75. The report's reference to the 10th had initially led to a wrong working date. The rows use the 14th for this report and the 10th for Reno's separate report.
- Lincoln's October heading is **13**, not the OCR's 18. Pettus's November letter is **15**, not the OCR's garbled day. Davis's acceptance of Randolph's resignation is also **15 November**, on the same p178 scan.
- Milton's **18 August** opening is on p56, before the body that the extraction helper could find at p57. The date HOLD was resolved by reading that image and the p59 certification.
- Archive `page/n…` uses the **access-page index**, which can differ from `leafNum` where leaves are excluded from access formats. Initial Lincoln and alternative OR-IX scans were off by one. They are preserved under `scans/earlier/`; they are not the manifest's current cited images. The corrected manifest records both leaf and access index. A failed intermediate namespace/index-variable pass is retained separately and not counted among the 100 usable scans. Duplicate printed labels in inserted material were checked against the body sequence.
- The June McClellan accusation is visibly printed at p61. This resolves the older README's question about the printing, not the separate transmission-history question.

## Preservation and reproduction

Use the manifest's exact download URL to recover a scan, then verify its SHA-256 and printed page. Do not derive an `n` value from the printed page alone. Local `scans.py`, `pages.py`, `extract.py`, `find.py` and `build_cast.py` are retained as research workings. Their extraction output is a finding aid: missing OCR headers are not missing historical documents. A reviewer should follow the printed page rather than trust the helper's page guess.

Command logs, exit records, the focused audit, source-file inventory and baseline runtime run copies are in the same external S6 folder. They are verification artifacts, not additional primary sources. No other sprint's source folder or checkout was written.
