## Proposed modelling rulings [I]

- Use the existing notice, declared temporary-post and institutional succession
  paths for these state acts, conditional on the existing Virginia alignment cause.
  Source dates are earliest dates; the game still owns that cause. Ordinary
  reports determine what each principal receives. No new principal knowledge is
  read from live forces.
- The two named Manassas battalions are represented at the first observed garrison
  date, 15 May [I]. Their ordered destination and identities are sourced, but the
  read documents do not prove each battalion's exact arrival date. This is an
  explicit allocation inference for review, not a new arrival fact.
- Use the existing force-entry path with optional local command and muster point.
  Preserve source act and original service credit. A service-transfer notice can
  only transfer an existing identity and cannot create, count or relocate one.
  A sourced relief ends the separately identified militia without inventing its
  original contractual expiry. A later aggregate return cannot recreate it.
- Neutral civilian successors of a state command can enter through a sourced
  institutional succession only when unheld, serving, without an existing personal
  service choice, and the government was initially neutral and is not self-governing.
  Their opening faction and unread personal ratings remain unknown. No personal
  resignation is fabricated or overwritten.
- These unquantified mixed land contingents use the existing recruitment land
  arm as a representation [I], not a claim of a complete infantry-only roll.
  Separate cavalry/artillery headcounts are not invented.
- Keep Norfolk, Richmond and Peninsula garrisons out of the automatic northern
  sweep. The existing northern concentration may request only its corresponding
  local northern command through ordinary transfers. Magruder's 21 May assignment
  uses the existing appointment path from Richmond, without a forced arrival.

Rejected: a June-only army; copying Harper's militia into Jackson's volunteers;
adding 40,000 to contingents already represented; turning authorized regiment or
company ceilings into mustered men; resetting losses, training or original terms
at transfer; exact strengths from Garnett's conjectures; forced marches, control,
arrival, battle or band changes. These are proposed rulings, not certification.

Remaining evidence limits: exact volunteer company rolls, strength and original
terms for most local contingents; exact Manassas battalion arrival dates; the
full statewide residual allocation and artillery/cavalry detail; naval transfers.
