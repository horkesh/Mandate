## How a citation can be checked

The scan's **printed number** is authoritative. Internet Archive's `scandata` labels for the Adams copies disagree with some actual printed numbers, and OCR page arrays can omit covers, tissue and plates. The local `pages.json` files therefore keep the OCR index, leaf identifier, detected printed heading and text separately. An automatically detected label is a reading aid, not a final proof. The stable census, short-report and main Navy-report offsets were checked separately; OCR can otherwise confuse page 56 with 66. Topic citations were checked against running headers and the particularly important numerical tables were rendered and read visually.

Examples: A1 printed p. 204 is PDF p. 228; A2 p. 64 is PDF p. 80; T62 p. 1 is PDF p. 9 and p. 31 is PDF p. 39; M62 p. 4 is PDF p. 5; OR42 p. 59 is PDF p. 73; N62 p. 26 is PDF p. 39; C60 p. 170 is PDF p. 192. The Google SCH copy has p. 167 at PDF p. 184; the readable Appendix I in the second SCH copy is an **unnumbered foldout opposite p. 312, PDF p. 331**. The foldout is not printed p. 313. These locators were checked against the scans.

Relevant text extracts are retained under `reading/`; additional reading is traceable in `pages.json` and the citation audit. Only relevant passages and tables were read, not every page of every downloaded book. Printed-page citations do not claim whole-book reading.

HTML errors returned with HTTP 200 are retained as failed access evidence and excluded from the source inventory. In particular, `stat12-p473.pdf`, `stat12-p474.pdf` and `stat12-p475.pdf` are **HTML error responses**, not scanned statutes. The full `stat12-p432-4.pdf` supplies those printed pages instead. A Gutenberg maintenance response and a Wikimedia 403 also supplied no evidence. Direct Internet Archive downloads supplied the retained scans; acquisition logs retain the failures rather than obscuring them.

## Boundaries of the evidence

Exact amounts are exact **as printed**, within the source's accounting perimeter; they are not uncertainty-free measurements. A rounded historical estimate does not acquire precision through calculation. A source's observed range is different from a proposed simulation acceptance band. All arithmetic, interpretation, proposed observables and modelling recommendations are marked [I]. No coefficient or daily foreign-pressure increment is asserted to have been measured by these sources.

These notes do not use Jefferson Davis's *Rise and Fall* or Joseph E. Johnston's *Narrative* as evidence. Early studies sometimes cite them; that does not make those memoirs independently read or acceptable evidence here. Sensitive content remains research for the separate gate reviewer, with no situation authored and no sign-off added.
