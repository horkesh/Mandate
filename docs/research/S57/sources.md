## Acquisition and image checks

The initial candidate identifiers `warofrebellion03unit` and `warofrebellion04unit` resolved to **series IV, volume III** and **series III, volume IV**, not the desired series I volumes. Their title OCR exposed the mismatch before any delivered fact used them. Corrected series I III/IV editions above supply the facts. The XVIII acquisition was a discovery lead and supplies no delivered row. All candidates and failure records remain external; a filename or catalogue search result is never treated as proof of a volume's contents.

Page-separated OCR, archive page-number metadata and scan XML are finding and reading aids. Raw leaf and accessible image index are distinct. The recovered Confederate journal pages use the earlier hashed locators; a namespace-blind XML attempt and a fresh OCR-index timeout supplied no facts. The local OCR is retained beside the image corrections; the image governs the stated correction, rather than an unrecorded guess.

Source scans were inspected headlessly as local images. [Image checks](image-checks.json) records 34 selected factual body/column checks. [Page inspection](page-inspection.json) records locator inspection of all 196 cited pages: header/footer contact strips for the first 190, direct views for unclear or exceptional pages, and full-image checks of the six later Seven Pines report pages. This does not certify every line of every page. The manifest retains scans for citations in both tables and the conflict notes, including the almanac p67 rejected dating lead. Source scans stay under the external S57 folder; only text, tables and manifests are committed. Discovery used Firecrawl, then direct headless archive requests and local reading tools. No browser, desktop or Computer Use was used.

The locator inspection found **19 incorrect image links**: Lincoln pp114, 115, 137, 203, 252; OR I XVII part II's eleven cited pages; OR I XXI pp82–83; and OR III I p823. [Locator corrections](locator-corrections.json) records each replacement. The current manifest points to images whose printed numbers were checked; `prior_locator` retains the original metadata and the path/hash of the old image saved as incorrect-locator evidence. Four of these are first-commit civil locators; their CSV facts and citations are unchanged. Earlier S12/S22/S38 files and scans are not rewritten. An inherited `leaf` remains a source-index finding aid, not a substitute for the verified accessible image index.

For Capers the archive offers a 101,297,281-byte zipped scan-data bundle rather than the expected standalone XML. That download was stopped and supplies no evidence. The selected-page locator instead combines retained page-number metadata (raw leaf 430), the read OCR passage (index 429), and direct image verification (**accessible image n425 is printed p404**). A first n429 inspection proved to be p408 and is excluded from the name citation. This source-specific correction is retained externally in `capers-locator-resolution.json`; no common page offset is applied to other volumes.

## Findings outside my scope

Original manuscripts and full annual-register collation have not been read. Appointment, confirmation, oath, rank, assumption and actual availability must be reconciled by the implementing lane against these separate records; this source list supplies no automatic conversion among them.
