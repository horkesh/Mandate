# S57 sources and reading limits

22 November 2026. Factual passages only; later editorial praise is excluded under 0087. Citations use `NEW:S57-<key> p<printed page>`. The [manifest](scan-manifest.json) records each page’s original URL, retained bytes, SHA-256, raw leaf and accessible image index. The external `page-readings.json` retains the OCR passages for comparison; images resolve the stated checks. No universal image/page offset is assumed.

| Key | Edition and use |
|---|---|
| HEITMAN | Francis B. Heitman, *Historical Register and Dictionary of the United States Army*, I, GPO, 1903; [scan](https://archive.org/details/historicalregist01heitrich). A private compilation published by Congress. The p17 image was rotated for reading: Stanton’s service entry and the commanding-general table/footnotes were read directly. |
| CALLAHAN | Edward W. Callahan, ed., *List of Officers of the Navy of the United States and of the Marine Corps from 1775 to 1900*, Hamersly, 1901; [scan](https://archive.org/details/listofofficersof00unitrich). Read the Secretary/Assistant Secretary lists and bureau organization/chiefs, pp1–5. Images pp1, 4, 5 were checked for column alignment. Later ranks and bureau titles in these tables are not backdated. |
| NATIONAL | *The National Almanac and Annual Record for 1863*, first year; [scan](https://archive.org/details/nationalalmanac01unkngoog). Read pp68–70 for year-level cabinet tenures; pp68 and 70 images checked. The p67 snapshot already names Usher and is not evidence of the cabinet in November 1862. An unrelated page-index entry labelled 220 whose header reads 320 was noticed during discovery and is excluded. |
| CONGRESS | *A Biographical Congressional Directory, 1774 to 1903*, GPO, 1903; [scan](https://archive.org/details/biographicalcong00unit). Read Caleb Blood Smith’s entry, p803; it gives 1861–62 service but no exact resignation day. |
| LINCOLN | John G. Nicolay and John Hay, eds., *Complete Works of Abraham Lincoln*, II; [edition scanned](https://archive.org/details/completeworksofa02linc_0). Read reproduced orders/correspondence pp87–88, 114–115, 137, 203, 252. Only the dated documents supply contemporary evidence. |
| WRIGHT | Marcus J. Wright, *General Officers of the Confederate Army*, Neale, 1911; [scan](https://archive.org/details/generalofficers00wrigrich). Executive directory pp151–154 read. Directory dates are reference facts; omissions and overlaps are kept. |
| JCCS-II | *Journal of the Congress of the Confederate States of America*, II, GPO; [scan](https://archive.org/details/journalofcongres02confiala). Read pp72–74, March 17 nomination message and March 18 executive proceedings. The retained plain OCR’s printed headers and the earlier hashed page locators recover these passages; no whole-volume OCR-index reconstruction is claimed. A fresh archive OCR-index download timed out and is not evidence. |
| OR-IV-1 | *War of the Rebellion: Official Records*, series IV, I, first scanned section; [scan](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079579524). Walker’s September resignation letters pp602–603 and Davis’s acceptance p613 read. No surrounding assertion about policy is adopted. |
| OR-IV-2 | *Official Records*, series IV, II; [scan](https://archive.org/details/cu31924080778909). Read Davis/Randolph p178, Campbell p179 and Shorter’s address p196. The rest of the letters’ policy and quantity claims are outside this research table. |

Earlier S6/S12/S22/S38 scans are copied only after matching their recorded SHA-256 and then hashed in the S57 folder. Reuse saves a download; it does not turn the earlier research prose into new historical evidence. New locators are checked against the same edition’s index and available printed header. An archive timeout is retained as an acquisition failure, not treated as a missing historical event.

## Findings outside my scope

Original manuscripts and full annual-register collation have not been read. Appointment, confirmation, oath, rank, assumption and actual availability must be reconciled by the implementing lane against these separate records; this source list supplies no automatic conversion among them.
