## Coverage and reading limits

The civil table covers both cabinets and the Union Navy bureau chiefs. The War table adds selected Union central staff offices. The command table follows principal eastern and western handovers, Kentucky/Tennessee, coastal departments, Trans-Mississippi organization, Kansas/Missouri, New Mexico and George Wright's Pacific reference interval. The rank/availability table adds selected regular, volunteer and Confederate promotions, resignations, wounds and deaths. This is an additive research batch, not a claim to have collated every general, brigade, staff assistant, squadron or temporary detachment in the window. Its rows cannot establish continued service on every intervening day.

The next evidence to seek is the original execution record for the held handovers, a complete contemporary appointment/confirmation collation, and notices of wounds, leave, recovery and return to duty. The casualty rows do not estimate recovery time or the day news reached an office. Naval operational command successions, Confederate bureau staffs, the pre-October Pacific handover and the remaining uncollated departmental interim commands have no new complete chronology here. Existing S12/S22/S38 evidence remains available and unchanged; an omission from S57 is not a deletion or a negative historical finding.

Native nations are not treated as factions of either belligerent, and geographical military titles do not establish civil sovereignty or policy. Source descriptions of civilians and Native peoples, editorial praise and accusations are not imported into character, regard or temperament. These tables make no sensitive-history placement proposal and create no automatic historical event.
