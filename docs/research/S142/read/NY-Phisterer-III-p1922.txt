SEVENTEENTH  REGIMENT  OF  INFANTRY.

Westchester  Chasseurs.

This  regiment,  Col.  Henry  S.  Lansing,  was  accepted  by  the  State  and  numerically
designated  May  14,  1861 ;  it  was  organized  in  New  York  city  and  there  mustered  in  the
United  States  service  for  two  years  May  28,  1861.  The  i8th  State  Militia  furnished
men  for  one  company  of  this  regiment.  March  16,  1862,  Company  G  was  consolidated

with  Companies  A,  B,  C  and  F,  and  replaced  by  Company  A,  53d  N.  Y.  Volunteers,  first
organization,  transferred  to  the  regiment.  May  13,  1863,  the  three  years'  men  of  the

but  June  23,  1863,  transferred  to  the  I46th  N.  Y.  Volunteers.

The  companies  were  recruited  principally:  A  at  Yonkers;  B  at  Portchester;  C  at
Morrisania;  D  and  E  at  New  York  city;  F  at  Sing  Sing;  G  —  Nyack  Volunteers  —  at
Nyack;  H  at  Norwich;  I  at  Newark,  Arcadia  and  Sodus;  K  at  Warsaw;  and  the  second
Company  G  at  New  York  city.

The  regiment  left  the  State  June  21,  1861 ;  served  at  and  near  Washington,  D.  C,
from  June  23,  1861 ;  at  Fort  Ellsworth,  D.  C,  from  August  4,  1861 ;  in  Butterfield's
Brigade,  Porter's  Division,  Army  of  the  Potomac,  from  October  15,  1862;  in  same  brigade
and  division,  3d  Corps,  Army  of  the  Potomac,  from  March  13,  1862;  in  the  3d  Brigade,
ist  Division,  5th  Corps,  Army  of  the  Potomac,  from  May,  1862,  and  was  honorably  dis-
charged and  mustered  out,  under  Colonel  Lansing,  June  2,  1863,  at  New  York  city.
