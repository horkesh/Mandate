The  following  "Record  of  Events"  is  given  on  the  muster  roll of  October  31,  1862: Left  Hanover  Junction  August  27th —  marched  to  Leesburg  via  Orange, Oulpeper,  Warrenton  &  near  CentreyUle  crossed  the  Potomac  opposite  Lees- burg, marched  to  Fredrick,  Md,  thence  to  Middletown  and  to  Brownsville near  Maryland  Heights.  Assended  the  heights  on  the  12th  of  Sept. advanced  along  the  ridge,  skirmishing  all  day.  On  the  18th —  in  conjunc- tion other  regts  from  our  brigade  we  drove  the  enemy  from  the  heights after  a  sharp  engagement  with  a  heavy  loss  on  the  part  of  the  regt,  but  no casualties  in  the  company.  On  the  15th.  we  came  down  from  the  mountain &  on  the  morning  of  the  16th  crossed  to  Harpers  ferry :  On  the  morning  of the  17  recrossed  the  river  at  Shepheards  town  and  entered  to  the  battle  of Sharpsburg  at  10  or  11  o'clock.  In  this  engagement  the  company  lost  one killed  and  12  wounded,  5  of  whom  we  were  compelled  to  leave  when  we recrossed  the  on  the  19th —  On  the  days  succeeding  we  changed  camps  to difiTerent  places  separated  by  short  distances  only.
