Under  the  head  of  "Remarks"  the  following  information  was entered  on  the  muster  roll  of  this  company  of  May  23, 1861 : "My  Company  was  organized,  on  the  7*^..  day  of  January 1861,  at  Sumter,  So.  Ca.  under  the  act  of  the  General  assembly  of said  State,  to  raise  an  armed  Military  force,  passed  Dec  1860 — were  called  into  service  on  the  8**»..  April  1861  and  served  at Morris  Island  until  the  26**» . .  April,  when  by  order  of  Gov Pickens  they  were  ordered  to  Virginia,  under  Col  Kershaw. J.  S.  Richardson  Jr.  Capt Sumter  Volunteers on  detached  Service  at  Wilmington  N.  C." This  company  was  mustered  into  Confederate  service,  at  Camp Davis,  near  Richmond,  Va.,  by  John  Scott,  Captain,  C.  S.  A., May  23,  1861.
