Secretary  of  War,  Washington  : SIR  :  I  am  directed  by  His  Excellency  the  Governor  of  this  State  to inquire  of  you  whether  the  troops  called  for  in  your  requisition  of  the 15th  instant,  when  mustered  into  the  service  of  the  United  States,  will be  furnished  with  arms  and  accouterments,  clothing,  camp  equipage, and  all  other  equipments  necessary  for  active  service  by  the  United States.  If  not,  what  portion  will  be  furnished  by  your  Department? As  this  State  is  poorly  provided  with  serviceable  arms  and  accout erments,  &c.,  an  early  reply  is  requested,  that  we  may  provide  our troops  with  everything  necessary  preparatory  to  their  taking  the  field at  as  early  a  day  as  possible,  in  obedience  to  the  orders  from  your Department. I  am,  very  respectfully,  your  obedient  servant, LEWIS  PERRINE, Quartermaster-  General  New  Jersey  Militia. CONCORD,  X.  H.,  April  16,  1861. Hon.
