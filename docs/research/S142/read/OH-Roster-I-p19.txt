1 SECOND OHIO VOLUNTEER INFANTRY. THREE MONTHS’ SERVICE. This Regiment was organized at Columbus, Ohio, April 18, 1861, in response to the call of the President dated April 15, 1861, for volunteers to serve three months. It was composed of companies enrolled from different parts of the State as follows: Company A (Rover Guards), Company D (Zouave Guards) and Company E (Lafayette Guards), Cincinnati, Ohio; Company B (Columbus Videttes) and Company C (Columbus Fencibles), Columbus, Ohio; Company F (Springfield Zouaves), Springfield, Ohio; Com- panies G and K, Pickaway county, Ohio; Company H, Steubenville, Ohio, and Company I (Covington Blues), Miami county, Ohio. Most of which were former militia organizations of the State. The Regiment was ordered to Washington City at once and left Columbus on the morning of April 19, 1861, for its destination but was delayed on its way several days. It, was mustered into service April 29, 1861, at Lancaster, Pa., and on its arrival] at Washing- ton early in May was assigned to General Schenck’s Brigade of General Tyler’s Division, then a part of. the force in defense of the capital. It was engaged in the battle of Bull Run, Va., July 21, 1861, losing two men killed, four wounded, eight captured and one missing. The Regiment was mustered out on expiration of term of service, July 31, 1861.
