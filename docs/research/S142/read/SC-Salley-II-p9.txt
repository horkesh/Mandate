Under  "Remarks,"  the  following  entry  occurs  on  a  muster  roll of  this  Company,  dated  May  22, 1861 : "My  company  was  an  existing  volunteer  company  and  was accepted  as  a  volunteer  company  by  the  Governor  of  the  State of  South  Carolina  under  the  act  of  General  assembly  of  said State,  to  provide  an  armed  military  force,  on  the  5*^-day  of  Jan- uary 1861 — on  the  8*^-day  of  April  1861  we  were  order**,  to  ren- dezvous at  Charleston  and  served  at  Morris  Island  untill  the  23^ day  of  april  when  by  order  of  Governor  Pickens  we  proceeded  to Richmond  Va.  under  Col  J.  B.  Kershaw W.  H.  Casson Cap  of  Gov  Guards 2nd  Regt" The  Company  was  mustered  into  Confederate  service  at  Camp Davis,  near  Richmond,  Va.,  by  John  Scott,  Captain,  C.  S.  A., Mustering  Officer,  May  22,  1861.
