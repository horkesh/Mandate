H.  to  the  present  camp  in  Spottsylvania  County  Va The  muster  roll  of  December  31,  1863,  contains  the  following "Eecord  of  Events:" The  Company  left  camp  near  Chattanooga  the  4th.  of  Novr  1863  marched in  direction  of  Knoxvllle  arrived  near  there  the  evening  of  17tii  Novr. Skirmished  with  Enemy  the  evening  of  17tii  &  18tii  Novr.  lost  (4)  four  men wounded  left  Knoxvllle  the  night  of  2nd  Deer,  marched  to  near  Rodgers- ville  camped  near  that  place  for  (3)  days  then  marched  back  to  Beans station.  Skirmished  with  the  Enemy  at  that  place  13th  Deer  without  loss to  the  Company  from  there  marched  to  the  present  camp.  Near  Russel ville Tenn. The  following  "Record  of  Events"  is  given  on  the  muster  roll of  December  17,  1864: Left  Oordansvllle  Va  4th  May  1864  and  entered  the  battle  of  the  Wilder- ness Va  on  the  6tii.  .  in  which  the  Co  lost  very  heavily,  on  the  8th  at  Spot- sylvania d  H  Va.  lost  slight,  on  the  23rd  at  North  anna  River  Va.  lost  a few  prisoners. On  Ist  June  64  were  engaged  at  Cold  Harbor  Va.  lost  a  few.  from  thence to  Petersburg  Va  and  even  in  a  skirmish  with  the  enemy  on  the  19th  in which  we  sustained  no  loss. The  following  note  is  given  on  the  muster  roll  of  April  27, 1865: Greensboro  N  C May  2d—  1865 In  accordance  with  the  terms  of  a  Military  Convention  entered  Into  on the  26th —  day  of  April  1865  between  General  Joseph  B  Johnston  Command- ing Confederate  Army  and  MaJ  General  W  T  Sherman  commanding  United States  Army  in  North  Carolina.
