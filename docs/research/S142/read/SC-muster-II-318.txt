COMPANY B (WILLLOIS GUARDS). This company joine^ for duty and was enrolled at Newberry, April 14, 1861, for twelve months' service, and was mustered into Confederate service, at Columbia, by Lieutenant Colonel B. E. Bee, Mustering Officer, June 6, 1861, for ten months and eight days. It reentered the service at the Reorganization in May, 1862.
