THIRD REGIMENT, SOUTH CAROUNA VOLUNTEERS. The 3rd Eegiment, South Carolina Volunteers, was one of the ten regiments organized under the direction of Governor Pickens early in 1861 under authority of An Act to provide an Armed Military Force, passed by the General Assembly of South Caro- lina, December 17, 1860. It was called into the service of tiie Confederate States in the Provisional Army thereof, by Presi- dent Davis, imder the provisions of an Act of Congress, passed February 28, 1861. It was mustered in by B. E. Bee, Lieutenant Colonel, S. C. I., Mustering Officer, at Columbia, June 6, 1861. Much of the history of the Regiment is given in Chapman's Annals of Newberry^ in Dickert's History of Kershaw^s Brigade and in the Official Records of the Union a^nd Confederate Armies, In 1908 the Historical Commission of South Carolina published a Tentative Roster of the Third Regiment for the purpose of aiding the survivors thereof to improve the "unofficial" compila- tion of the roster of the regiment, which, at that time, was all the State possessed on the regiment. FIELD AND STAFF. The following muster rolls of the Field and Staff of the Regi- ment are available : June 6, 1861, at Columbia; October 31, 1862, near Brucetown, Va.; December 31, 1862, Fredericksburg, Va.; February 28, 1863, near Hamilton's Crossing, Va., a part of that of February 29, 1864, showing a part of the non-commissioned staff, and June 30, 1864. The following regimental returns are available: December, 1861, January 21, 1862, camp near Centre ville, Va., and one undated, but prepared just subsequent to the Reorganization, May 13, 1862.
