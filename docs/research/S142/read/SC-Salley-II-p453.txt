COMPANY  G. This  company,  known  as  the  Laurens  Briars,  joined  for  duty and  was  enrolled  at  Laurens,  April  14,  1861,  and  was  mustered into  Confederate  service,  at  Camp  Johnson,  Columbia,  June  6, 1861,  by  B.  E.  Bee,  Lieutenant  Colonel,  South  Carolina  Infantry, Mustering  Officer. The  following  muster  rolls  of  the  Company  are  available  and from  them  the  succeeding  muster  roll  has  been  compiled :  June 6,  1861,  at  Camp  Johnson,  Columbia;  June  13,  1861,  at  Camp Johnson,  Columbia;  October  31,  1862,  camp  near  Winchester, Va.,  received  at  the  Adjutant  and  Inspector  Gteneral's  Office, January  29, 1863 ;  December  31, 1862,  camp  near  Fredericksburg, Va.,  received  at  Adjutant  and  Inspector  General's  Office,  Jan- uary 29,  1863;  Fdbruary  28,  1863,  camp  near  Fredericksburg, received  at  Adjutant  and  Inspector  General's  Office,  March  20, 1863;  June  30,  1863,  camp  near  Chambersburg,  Pa.,  received  at the  Adjutant  and  Inspector  General's  Office,  March  9,  1864; August  31, 1863,  near  Waller's  Tavern,  received  at  Adjutant  and Inspector  General's  Office,  March  9,  1864;  December  31,  1863, camp  near  Eussellville,  Tennessee,  received  at  Adjutant  and Inspector  General's  Office,  February  15,  1864;  June  30,  1864, camp  near  Eichmond,  Va.;  December  15,  1864  (Voucher  No.  690.
