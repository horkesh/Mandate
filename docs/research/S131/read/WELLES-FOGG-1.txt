Hartford 31 Jany 1861
My Dear Sir
I have yours of the
27th from Cincinnati, and the
information, on several points,
is peculiarly gratifying. The decision
and firmness of the President elect
begin already to inspire the
people with confidence, and,
place him in position, as he will
be on and after the 4th of March, &
he will rally the country around
him. Our friends hardly realise
the change that will take place
in public sentiment with a change
of administration. No men of
any party have the least confidence
in the present Executive; but the
President will himself be a tower
of strength for the country and
the Union after the 4th of March.
