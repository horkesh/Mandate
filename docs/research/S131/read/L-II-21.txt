Opinion  on  Fort  Sumter  from  the  Attorney- General,

The  President  of  the  United  States  has  requested  my  opinion,  in  writ-
ing, upon  the  following  question :

"Assuming  it  to  be  possible  to  now  provision  Fort  Sumter,  under  all
the  circumstances  is  it  wise  to  attempt  it  ?  "

dence and  patriotism  only.  The  right  is,  in  my  mind,  unquestionable,
and  I  have  no  doubt  at  all  that  the  government  has  the  power  and  the
means  not  only  to  provision  the  fort,  but  also,  if  the  exigency  required,
