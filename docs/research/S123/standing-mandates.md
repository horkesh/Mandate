# S123: executable standing declarations, separate from the catalogue

23 November 2026. S123 only. The executable instruments are
`packs/union-1861/pack.json:rules.mandates`; S117's catalogue is a research
input, not executable authority. [Reading receipts](source-reading.json)
identify 23 retained primary texts actually read by this lane, with printed
pages, URLs and hashes. No new scan inspection is claimed.

All executable permissions below are **[I] pack declarations under 0124**.
The instruments do not enumerate kernel actions or transport modes. Existing
field goals, explicit plan review and available movement modes apply only to
the command's own assigned troops, inside the declared regions. Onward grants
stay inside that command. Appointments, levies, political verbs, peer compulsion,
civilian treatment and another command's forces remain excluded. The ceiling is
a reference to assigned forces, never a new number or troop balance. No guard
or duration is manufactured; no historical receipt is assumed.

The runtime principal is the model's root of authority. That is not a claim
that Lincoln authored the 1841 order, that Davis signed Cooper's letter, or
that a staff signature makes its writer a granting principal. Regulations
pp.58–60, paras.425–429 and 438–441, distinguish the commander and transmitting
staff. Johnston's original assignment remains unrecovered in this reading;
his read assumption and contemporary government letters attest the bounded
command (0124 item 5).

## GO12 and GO23

GO12's issuer text governs: Pennsylvania, Delaware and Maryland outside the
Washington and Annapolis departments; its Annapolis exclusion is twenty miles
either side of the railroad to Bladensburg (OR I-II p.607). GO23 instead says
twelve miles and describes Maryland east of Bladensburg (p.611). Both readings
are recorded; neither is silently substituted for the other.

The pack's county-group regions cannot trace either corridor. The conservative
standing mapping contains `cumberland_valley_pa`, `harrisburg`,
`pennsylvania_north`, `philadelphia`, `pittsburgh`, `southeast_pa`, `york_adams`,
`delaware` and `western_maryland`. The mixed regions `southern_maryland`
(including Anne Arundel/Prince George's), `baltimore_county` (including Howard),
`baltimore`, and `frederick_md` (including Montgomery) are withheld rather than
claiming the whole region lies outside the exclusion. These county memberships
are read pack data, `source/world/regions.csv`, not newly researched boundaries.
This is a conservative model gap, not a historical prohibition of all those
places. Both corridor readings give the same deliberately withheld regional
set here. A specific direction may supply an operational scope; it must not be
manufactured from rank or a source date.

## Named gaps and unsupported terms

- **Lee's actual placement and assigned state-command lineage.** The sourced
  address is declared as an unopened template under the governor. A mandate
  cannot appoint Lee or reparent forces. The grant therefore waits for a known
  held command; no calendar opens it. After actual transfer, the governor's
  force reference cannot authorize national-chain forces (pp.911–912). The
  additional national-force control in Walker's 10 May telegram (p.827) also
  needs that placement and exact assigned membership; it is not silently folded
  into the state root.
- **Scott's 24 May direction (U10, p.652).** Its remaining Pennsylvania/New
  Jersey quotas, a demonstration toward Harpers Ferry, and a self-relying
  Cumberland garrison cannot together be expressed by a regional `take` or an
  unquantified guard. No partial unlimited grant is substituted. The specific
  instrument remains named here, with no invented guard, new unit selection or
  date-only concentration.
- **Wallace's 6 June terms (U14, p.668).** The safe detachment, armed-gathering
  report, bridge-local guard and return lifecycle belong to the later sortie
  lane. Civilian searches/arrests and political organization are not granted.
- **Jackson's refusal (C06, p.872).** His receipt and refusal pending Letcher or
  Lee's instructions remain verbatim in the retained paper. No runtime Jackson
  receipt/answer or consent is fabricated: the distribution/publication request
  needs its own producer and current state-command address. It is not an
  enacted grant to Johnston or a troop transfer.
- **Detailed copies and administrative routing.** The declarations retain the
  Regulations' reporting duties, including copies to the Adjutant-General
  (p.59, para.438). Existing plan submissions, acknowledgments and operational
  reports travel normally. A distinct administrative copy-of-every-order
  producer and staff-bureau filing path remain a named gap.
- **Legacy July doctrine.** Existing principal-issued field orders are not
  relabelled as Scott's without the appropriate received specific mandate and
  source terms. This build reports its actual issuers; it does not claim the
  whole June chain or the sortie has been reconstructed.

The paper dates are earliest bounds. Actual dispatch, relay, receipt and answer
come from ordinary simulation execution. An unopened post, refusal or missing
paper is not replaced with a historical arrival date. Formal review and any
1A credit remain with the lead.
