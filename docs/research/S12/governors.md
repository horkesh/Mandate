# Governors and competing authorities in 1862

Research proposal only. [Office inventory](governors.csv) contains **55 records**, including civil incumbents, acting officers, January predecessors, Confederate claims and five federal military appointments. **27 records** have separately cited prewar career facts; the others expressly retain the missing history. [Common judgment fields](governor-judgments.csv) give competence, nerve, experience, reputation, boldness, candour, regard, organiser and duty for each record: **4 proposed dated observations, 491 HOLDs**. S6's existing positive observations remain separate and intact. These tables do not apply a cast or erase earlier evidence.

The [six boundary records](governor-boundaries.csv) keep five prospective 1863 governors separate from incumbents, and expose the unresolved Reynolds succession lead. [Assessments](governor-assessments.csv) and [relationships](relationships.csv) identify who says what about whom. Dates identify the actual document or a reference interval; an election, appointment, confirmation, arrival and assumption of duties are distinct events. No table row implies possession of every part of the named state.

## Civil offices and early-year handovers

The **19 May 1862** federal request identifies the Northern governors by state and desired number of regiments. The named recipients and **30 May** circulation on p99 establish offices, not personal delivery promises or organising gifts. **J. B. Temple** is President of Kentucky's Military Board in the separate reinforcement address; he is not made governor. [OR III-2, pp44, 99](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079575241/page/44); [Annual Cyclopaedia 1862, p791](https://archive.org/details/americanannualcyv2dapp/page/791).

|---|---|
| California | State compilation dates Stanford's inauguration **10 January 1862**. Its roster gives Downey governor 1860–62. The Congressional Directory instead says Stanford served from December 1861: keep that conflict visible and prefer the state's specific inauguration record for this event. [California Blue Book 1907, pp582, 693](https://archive.org/details/californiabluebo1907cali/page/693); [Congressional Directory 1903, p816](https://archive.org/details/biographicalcong00unit/page/816). |

## Confederate governors and rival claims

Davis's **26 November** circular supplies a broad Confederate governor address list, including Hawes for Kentucky and Jackson for Missouri. It also still names **Rector** for Arkansas. The annual volume reports **Flanagan** elected in August and **Fletcher**, Senate president, performing the duties on the first Monday in November because the governor-elect was absent with the army. The list therefore cannot certify every recipient's current tenure. Flanagan's full-name/spelling join, actual assumption day and Fletcher's given name/end of acting interval remain HOLD. [OR IV-2, pp211–212](https://archive.org/details/cu31924080778909/page/211); [Annual Cyclopaedia, pp11–12](https://archive.org/details/americanannualcyv2dapp/page/12).

**Clark and Vance** are kept separate in North Carolina. Clark's July and August letters discuss exemptions, partisan companies and a disputed account of state recruiting assistance. Vance signs as executive by **11 September**; his **29 October** reply to Stanly rejects a separate settlement and offers to forward humanitarian proposals to the proper authorities. These are attributed political positions, not evidence of unusual administrative competence or private affection for Davis. [OR IV-2, pp17, 31, 67–69, 81](https://archive.org/details/cu31924080778909/page/67); [OR III-2, pp845–847](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079575241/page/845).

**Pickens and Bonham** are also distinct. Bonham's **30 December 1862** State headquarters letter encloses the 18 December act for coerced enslaved labor in coast defense. The Congressional Directory says elected in December; McPherson's later compiled footnote instead says January 1863. The December letter prevents treating Bonham as wholly outside 1862, while the exact accession day remains unproved here. This coercion evidence is an unsigned sensitive-history draft; sending an act is not an organising gift or sufficient proof of a personal value centre. [OR IV-2, pp266–267](https://archive.org/details/cu31924080778909/page/266); [Congressional Directory, p400](https://archive.org/details/biographicalcong00unit/page/400); [McPherson, p402](https://archive.org/details/politicalhistory00mcph/page/402).

**Missouri and Kentucky** require rival-authority records. Gamble and acting lieutenant-governor Hall are separate from Jackson's Confederate claim; the annual obituary dates Jackson's death **6 December**. The Reynolds boundary remains unresolved: Thompson's reproduced **2 September 1861** proclamation calls Reynolds acting governor, which alone does not date a December-1862 succession. Kentucky's George W. Johnson and Richard Hawes are separate from Magoffin/Robinson. The directory dates Hawes's installation **4 October 1862** and withdrawal on federal advance. Neither an installation nor a Confederate address means continuous control of Frankfort. [Annual Cyclopaedia, pp591, 680](https://archive.org/details/americanannualcyv2dapp/page/680); [Congressional Directory, pp572, 585](https://archive.org/details/biographicalcong00unit/page/585); [McPherson, p245](https://archive.org/details/politicalhistory00mcph/page/245).

## Military appointments and the limits of control

| Appointee | Separate events and limits |
|---|---|
| Andrew Johnson, Tennessee | Annual volume gives Senate confirmation **5 March** and Nashville arrival **12 March**. Official June correspondence says his commission terms matched Stanly's. These are separate events. Annual Cyclopaedia p763; OR III-2 p106. |
| Edward Stanly, North Carolina | Instructions dated **2 May**; annual volume gives New Bern arrival **26 May** and restricts the jurisdiction to federal-held territory. The annual spelling Stanley is not silently turned into a different person. Annual Cyclopaedia p662; OR III-2 p106. |
| George F. Shepley, Louisiana | Stanton says already appointed on **3 June**; transmittal/instructions dated **10 June**. New Orleans expressly included. Prospective confidence is an expectation, not evidence of accomplished administration. OR III-2 pp141–142. |
| John Smith Phelps, Arkansas | Annual volume records appointment and departure for Helena **19 August**; projected move on Little Rock did not follow in that account. Appointment alone does not establish an effective state government. Annual Cyclopaedia p12; Congressional Directory p741. |
| Andrew J. Hamilton, Texas | Directory says Lincoln appointed him military governor in **1862**, separately from the 1865 provisional appointment. Exact 1862 appointment day and effective reach remain HOLD. Congressional Directory p573. |

These passages can be read at [OR III-2, pp106, 141–142](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079575241/page/141), [Annual Cyclopaedia, pp12, 662, 763](https://archive.org/details/americanannualcyv2dapp/page/662) and [Congressional Directory, pp573, 741](https://archive.org/details/biographicalcong00unit/page/573). Governors are civil actors unless a military commission is separately sourced; no generic brigadier grade is assigned to a civil officeholder.

## What clears, and what stays held

Buckingham on **19 May**, Blair on **10 June** and Salomon on **3 June** disclose that requested recruiting deadlines cannot reliably be met. **[I] Candour above** is proposed for those conversations. Their explanations do not certify eventual completion, an organising gift or the fault of the officers they criticize. [OR III-2, pp44, 106, 142–143](https://archive.org/details/cu31924079575241/page/106).

Beauregard's **11 April** report describes provisional governor **George W. Johnson** losing a horse, entering the ranks and being mortally wounded. **[I] Nerve above** has a specific personal-exposure leg. This re-reads a report used in S6, rather than manufacturing another independent witness; the observation is posthumous and cannot make Johnson available for promotion. [OR I-10-1, pp385, 389](https://archive.org/details/cu31924077730160/page/389).

Davis's **25 October** explicit confidence runs **Davis toward Harris**. It cannot fill Harris's regard toward Davis. Davis's thanks to Vance on **1 November** is also kept in its recruiting-grievance context. No historian's praise in the later directory or state biography clears a gift. [OR IV-2, pp148, 154](https://archive.org/details/cu31924080778909/page/148).
