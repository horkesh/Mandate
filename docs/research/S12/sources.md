`scan-manifest.json` retains the exact URLs, SHA-256, bytes and access-image indices. Chase's `page_numbers.json` is mapped to its OCR page index and checked against the printed headings; the p100 and p105 images were visually read. The printed headings correct the October 5/6 OCR problem. Image retention does not mean every retained image was visually inspected. The original manuscript diary has not been examined. Heitman p340, Wright p25, Lee p77 and OR I-19-1 p803 images were also visually read to check page mapping and key ambiguities. Pemberton's confirmation month is struck through in the p25 scan, so its day stays HOLD.

An initial plain-text Heitman download timed out; an attempted ZIP-member scandata URL returned 503; the first Chase scan request had no usable scandata. Those logs remain acquisition failures. The smaller official Archive page-number and OCR-index files supplied a usable path, with cited headings checked. Alternate almanac search hits with the wrong year are retained leads and do not source facts.

Initial Wisconsin/California image-index mappings were rejected after visual inspection and retained in `rejected-state-scan-mapping.json` and `rejected-scan-mapping/`. Corrected images are the only ones in the delivery manifest. The Missouri messages volume supplied OCR but no page-index file; no unpaged extraction from it is used as evidence. Internet search snippets and unused modern summaries do not source delivered historical facts.
