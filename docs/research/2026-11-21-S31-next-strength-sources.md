The [Navy muster guide](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/navy/muster-rolls), “Muster Rolls of Ships, 1860-1900,” identifies RG 24 entry PI-123 132, series 563603, as digitized Civil War ship rolls. The [NARA muster-survival discussion](https://www.archives.gov/publications/prologue/2001/fall/black-sailors) cautions about early-roll survival. The [RG45 guide](https://www.archives.gov/research/guide-fed-records/groups/045.html), sections 45.8.6/45.9, supplies related Confederate naval account/subject-file routes. No demographic or complement estimate is made from these guides.
