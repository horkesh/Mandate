WAR  DEPARTMENT,
Washington,  May  15,  1861.
Hon.  HAMILTON  FISH,
Chairman^  &c. :
DEAR  SIR:  The  Governor  of  New  York  has  been  requested  to  send
immediately  to  this  city  five  regiments  and  to  Fort  Monroe  nine  regi
ments  of  troops,  to  serve  during  the  war.  Should  the  Governor  decline
to  do  so,  you  are  hereby  authorized  to  do  it.  These  troops  are  to  be
made  up  of  the  fourteen  regiments  now  in  New  York  City,  which  are  to
be  designated  by  the  Union  Defense  Committee  of  said  city.
Yours,  respectfully,
SIMOX  CAMERON,
Secretary  of  War.
WAR  DEPARTMENT,
May  Id,  1861.
ABRAHAM  VAN  VECHTEN,  Esq.,
Albany.  N.  T.:
That  there  may  be  no  further  misunderstanding  on  the  subject,  the
Secretary  directs  me  to  inform  you  that  this  Department  can  accept
only  eleven  regiments  to  serve  for  two  or  three  years,  or  during  the  war,
making  twenty-eight  regiments  from  Xew  York  in  all;  that  is  to  say,
seventeen  for  three  months  and  eleven  for  the  war.
