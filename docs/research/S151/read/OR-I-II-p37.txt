To  Colonel  GARNETT.
STEAMER  LOGAN,
Yorktown,  Va.,  May  24,  1801—11.30  p.  m.
SIR  :  Since  arriving  at  this  point  I  have  learned  that  the  news  which
was  forwarded  to  you  from  West  Point  was  incorrect.  Only  one  thou
sand  tioops  were  marched  into  Hampton,  which  they  only  occupied  fora
short  time.  A  small  surveying  party  was  landed  at  Newport  News.
There  is  no  cavalry  between  this  and  Hampton.  One  company  there.
Not  a  horseman  nor  a  wagon  here.  Mr.  Anderson,  the  bearer,  will  give
further  particulars.
I  am,  sir.  very  respectfully,  your  obedient  servant,
J.  B.  MAGRUDER,
Colonel,  Commanding.
Colonel  GARNETT.
