Sir: Your letter of yesterday’s date by which the

effect a junction with the main column,
is
just received.*
I
shall
endeavor to pass the river and comply with the instructions of the
major-general commanding ; but my command being entirely destitute
of cavalry or artillery the passage of the river aud advance through a
country full of hostile cavalry will be necessarily slow and somewhat
hazardous.
If it be intended that I should act on Leesburg, I should
cross at or near Noland’s Ferry, three miles below Point of Rocks, on
the south of the ridge and below the Shenandoah River.
