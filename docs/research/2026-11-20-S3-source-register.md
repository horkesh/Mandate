## Working method and limits

- Facts in earlier project notes were treated as leads, not as newly read evidence. Snippets, search results, inaccessible links and unconsulted footnotes do not become facts in this packet.
- Historical actors' letters, speeches and editorial claims are attributed. Their claims about motives or another person's conduct do not become the narrator's findings.
- All proposed bands and classification choices are marked [I]. They are neither confidence intervals nor existing acceptance criteria, and none changes a pack.
- Download responses are checked by file signature and PDF opening. Some govinfo interior-page URLs returned HTML with HTTP 200: those are failed retrievals, not scans. The complete volume or a valid act granule supplies the affected pages.
- OCR is a finding aid. For tallies and state returns, read the scan, preserve incomplete-return qualifications and check arithmetic. Do not silently repair a source's printed number.
- No protected population is converted into a stock, a price or a player allocation. Sensitive historical material stays subject to separate gate review.
