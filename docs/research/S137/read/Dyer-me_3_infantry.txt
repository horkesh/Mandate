3rd REGIMENT INFANTRY. Organized at Augusta and mustered in June 4, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, June 5. Attached to Howard's Brigade, Heintzelman's Division, McDowell's Army of Northeastern Virginia, to August, 1861. Howard's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. Sedgwick's Brigade, Heintzelman's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
