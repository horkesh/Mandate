11th REGIMENT INFANTRY. Organized at Readville and mustered in June 13, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, June 24. Attached to Franklin's Brigade, Heintzelman's Division, McDowell's Army of Northeast Virginia, to August, 1861. Hooker's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. 1st Brigade, Hooker's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
