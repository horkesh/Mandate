18th REGIMENT INFANTRY. Organized at Albany, N. Y., and mustered in May 17, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, June 19. Attached to Davies' Brigade, Miles' Division, McDowell's Army of Northeast Virginia, June to August, 1861. Franklin's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. Newton's Brigade, Franklin's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
