2nd REGIMENT INFANTRY. Organized at Burlington and mustered in June 20, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, June 24. Attached to Howard's Brigade, Heintzelman's Division, McDowell's Army of Northeast Virginia, to August, 1861. W. F. Smith's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October. 1861. Brook's Brigade, Smith's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
