1st REGIMENT INFANTRY.— (3 MONTHS.) Organized at large April 14 to April 29, 1861. Mustered in April 17, 1861. Moved to Washington, D. C, April lit and duty in the Defences of that city till Julv Attached to Schenck's Brigade, Tyler's Division, McDowell's Army of Northeast Virginia. Actions at Vienna. Va„ June 17 and July 9. McDowell's advance on Manassas, Va., July 16-21. Occupation of Fairfax Court House, Va., July 17. Battle of Bull Run, Va., July 21. Cover retreat to Washington. Ordered to Ohio and mustered out August 2, 1861, expiration of term.
