4th REGIMENT INFANTRY. Organized at Adrian, Mich.', and mustered in June 20, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, June 26. Attached to Wilcox's Brigade, Heintzelman's Division, Mc- Dowell's Army of Northeastern Virginia, to August, 1861. Sherman's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. Morell's Brigade, Porter's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
