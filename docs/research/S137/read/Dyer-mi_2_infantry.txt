2nd REGIMENT INFANTRY. Organized at Detroit, Mich., May 25, 1861. ("The first three-years Regiment from Michigan." ) Moved to Washington, D. C, June 6-10. Attached to Richardson's Brigade Tyler's Division, McDowell's Army of Northeastern Virginia, to August, 1861. Richardson's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. Richardson's Brigade Heintzelman's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March 1862
