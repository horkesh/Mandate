31st REGIMENT INFANTRY ("BAXTER'S LIGHT GUARD," "MONTEZUMA REGIMENT"). Organized at New York City and mustered in May 14, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, June 24, 1861. Attached to Davies' Brigade, Miles' Division, McDowell's Army of Northeast Virginia, to August, 1861. Franklin's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. Newton's Brigade, Franklin's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
