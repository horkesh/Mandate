3rd REGIMENT INFANTRY.— (3 YEARS.) Organized at Camp Olden, Trenton, N. J., and mustered in June 4, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. 0, June 28, 1861. Attached to 2nd Brigade, Runyon's Reserve Division, McDowell's Army of Northeast Virginia, to August, 1861. Kearney's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. Kearney's Brigade, Franklin's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
