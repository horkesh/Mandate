27th REGIMENT INFANTRY.— ("WASHINGTON BRI- GADE.") Organized at Philadeuphia January, 1861. Moved to Baltimore, Md., April 18. Attacked in streets of Baltimore April 19. Returned to Philadelphia and reorganized for three years. Mustered in May 31 to date from May 5, 1861. Moved to Washington, D. C, June 17-18. Attached to 1st Brigade, Miles' Division, Mc- Dowell's Army of Northeast Virginia, to August, 1861. Blenker's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. Stahel's Brigade, Blenker's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
