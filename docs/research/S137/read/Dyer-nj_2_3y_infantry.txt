2nd REGIMENT INFANTRY.— (3 YEARS.) Organized at Camp Olden, Trenton, N. J., May 27, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, June 28, 1861. Attached to 2nd Brigade, Runyon's Reserve Division, REGIMENTAL HISTORIES McDowell's Army of Northeast Virginia, to August, 1861. Kearney's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. Kearney's Brigade, Franklin's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
