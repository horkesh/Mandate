2nd REGIMENT INFANTRY. Organized at Madison, Wis., and mustered in June 11, 1861. Moved to Washington, D. C, June 20-25. Attached to Sherman's Brigade, Tyler's Division, McDowell's Army of Northeast Virginia, to August, 1861. Fort Corcoran, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. King's Brigade, McDowell's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
