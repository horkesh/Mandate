8th REGIMENT INFANTRY.— ("1st GERMAN RIFLES.") Organized at New York City and mustered in April 23, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, May 26. Attached to Blenker's Brigade, Miles' Division, McDowell's Army of Northeast Virginia, June to August, 1861. Blenker's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. Blenker's Brigade, Hooker's Division, Army of the Potomac, to January, 1862
