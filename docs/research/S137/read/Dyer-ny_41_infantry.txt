41st REGIMENT INFANTRY— "DE KALB REGI- MENT," "2nd YAGER REGIMENT." Organized at Yorkville, N. Y., and mustered in June 6, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, July 8. Attached to 2nd Brigade, Runyon's Reserve Division, Mc- Dowell's Army of Northeast Virginia, to August, 1861. W. T. Sherman's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to September, 1861. Martindale's Brigade, Porter's Division, Army of the Potomac, to November, 1861. Unassigned, Blenker's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
