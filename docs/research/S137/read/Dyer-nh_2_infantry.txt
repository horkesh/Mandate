2nd REGIMENT INFANTRY. Organized at Portsmouth May 31 to June 8, 1861. Moved to Washington, D. O, June 20-23, and duty there till July 16. Attached to Burnside's Brigade, Hunter's Division, McDowell's Army of Northeastern Virginia, to August, 1861. Hooker's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. 1st Brigade, Hooker's Division, Army of the Potomac, to March, 1862
