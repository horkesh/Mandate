29th REGIMENT INFANTRY ("ASTOR RIFLES," "1st GERMAN INFANTRY"). Organized at New York City and mustered in June 6, 1861. Left State for Washington, D. C, June 21, 1861. Attached to Blenker's Brigade, Miles' Division, McDowell's Army of Northeast Virginia, to August, 1861. Blenker's Brigade, Division of the Potomac, to October, 1861. Blenker's Brigade, Hooker's Division, Army of the Potomac, to January, 1862
