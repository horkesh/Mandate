If  he  is  not  released  in  the  morning  I  shall  make  a  more  formal  demand
under  a  flag  of  truce.  As  it  will  take  my  messenger  nearly  all  night  to
reach  the  river  connection  in  the  morning,  I  must  bring  this  to  an
abrupt  close,  craving  your  indulgence  for  its  desultory  character.
