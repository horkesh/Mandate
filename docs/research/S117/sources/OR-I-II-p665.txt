WASHINGTON,  June  4, 1861.
Gen.  E.  PATTERSON,  Chamber  sburg,  Pa.  :
General  Scott  says  do  not  make  a  move  forward  until  you  are  joined
by  a  battery  of  the  Fourth  Artillery  and  a  battalion  of  five  compa
nies  Third  II.  S.  Infantry,  to  leave  here  the  6th  instant  for  Carlisle.
Company  F,  Fourth  Artillery,  is  the  one  to  be  mounted.  Orders  have
been  given  to  purchase  horses  and  collect  the  guns,  equipments,  &c.,  as
soon  as  possible,  at  Carlisle.  It  will  require  some  days,  but  the  General
considers  this  addition  to  your  force  indispensable.  If  two  Ohio  regi
ments  come  to  you,  retain  them.  Also  halt  the  first  two  regiments  that
may  pass  through  Harrisburgfrom  the  North  to  this  city,  and  add  them
to  your  force.  You  will  receive  a  letter  from  the  General  before  you
move.
E.  D.  TOWNSEKD,
Assistant  Adjutant- General.
