## National count limits

Vincent's dated24November statement gives **527,679 present for duty from army-command returns**, with **595,196 total present** and **775,336 total present and absent**. A separate muster/pay-roll/regimental series gives **666,185 volunteers present for duty** and790,197volunteers on its aggregate. He explicitly attributes differences to return dates, many rolls being31October. The printed26,255Regulars is an aggregate, not an additional verified Regular pfd count. It must not be added unqualified to666,185as a new observed army pfd total. Source: OR III-II p.868, image.

Livermore's January1862 national comparison is on the rolls/present-and-absent, and the March comparison uses returns drawn from different spring dates with exclusions. HisJanuary1863column falls outside S14. No complete November Confederate national pfd total was established by this reading; partial Eastern/Western/coastal returns remain partial, and a hold must not silently become zero or a January1863backfill. Sources: L pp.41–47; OR I-XIX.2 p.713; OR I-XXI p.1025; OR I-XX.2 p.412.
