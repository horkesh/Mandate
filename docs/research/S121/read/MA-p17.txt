The  Third,  shortly  after its  arrival,  was  ordered  on  board  the  United  States  war  vessel Pawnee,  and  conveyed  that  evening  to  Norfolk,  where  the  men took  an  active  part  in  destroying  the  Navy  Yard,  to  prevent  it from  falling,  with  its  valuable  stores,  into  the  hands  of  the rebels.  Colonel  Wardrop's  report  to  your  Excellency,  giving  a full  account  of  this  expedition,  and  of  the  conduct  of  our  troops, was  never  received,  being  unfortunately  lost  on  its  way  hither. The  Fifth  Regiment  was  stationed  near  Washington.  It behaved  gallantly  at  the  battle  of  Bull  Run,  on  the  21st  of July.  Colonel  Lawrence  was  slightly  wounded.
