Two-thirds  of  the  company  officers  of  the  regiments  to  be  raised under  this  plan  will  be  appointed  at  the  commencement  of  the  organi zation  of  each  regiment,  and  the  remaining  one-third,  when  the  regi ment  shall  have  its  full  complement  of  men,  will  be  appointed  from  the ranks,  to  be  taken  from  among  the  sergeants  on  the  recommendation of  the  colonel  of  the  regiment,  approved  by  the  general  commanding the  brigade. After  the  completion  of  the  organization  of  a  regiment  of  cavalry  or infantry,  one-half  of  all  the  vacancies  which  may  occur  in  the  lowest grade  of  commissioned  officers,  by  promotion  or  otherwise,  will  be appointed  as  above  from  the  ranks.
