INFANTRY. Eight  regiments  of  infantry,  in  addition  to  those  now  in  service,  will be  raised. Each  regiment  will  consist  of  two  or  more  battalions. Each  battalion  will  consist  of  eight  companies. Each  company  will  be  organized  as  follows:  Minimum — 1  captain, 1  first  lieutenant,  1  second  lieutenant,  1  first  sergeant,  4  sergeants,  8 corporals,  2  musicians,  04  privates;  aggregate,  82.
