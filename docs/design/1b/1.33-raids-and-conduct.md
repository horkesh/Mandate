Split two M briefs [I]: raids/repair/report regressions; gated conduct/pressure and its real consequences. ## Mechanisms and typed terms

a: Extend the planner's military target union to a named edge or depot only through a goal for a holder. `RaidTerm` records passage, reported military target, opportunity, damage limit, stores, withdrawal and repair need [I]. Operations execute; infrastructure alone applies bounded condition changes. A repair request goes to a named competent holder, consumes accepted ordinary resources/capacity and can stall or fail. No direct “restore edge” click or immortal free repair timer.

The raid's actual damage may be unknown to the enemy. 1.23 reports carry edge/depot condition and route disruption to each relevant seat with latency; planners read only those beliefs. Actual supply/movement finds the true disruption and produces its own report. Disruption and repair are idempotent event proposals with typed clamps, not a one-off flag that can be farmed for political effects.

b: `ConductReason` distinguishes accepted policy, attributable actor practice, reported incident, institutional reply and locked consequence. An atrocity-class act is an event/text, never a legal initiative or option. Presidential responses restrict, suspend, refer or revoke; no confirm or “let stand” option. Inaction is explicit and watched. The accepted R6 can model lawful conduct through a person who can resist, but cannot authorise killings, civilian-home burnings, community assessments, banishment or hostages as profitable levers.

`PressureTerm` names region, actor/event, affected measure, direction, elapsed window, delta and agency reason if required. Pressure changes loyalty/supply only within an accepted rule and sourced/design-labelled bound. An atrocity-related term must be non-positive on its acting side's favourable measures, including indirect repair/capacity/will effects. Preserve 0057/0060 as well: an atrocity notice only worsens its acting side's measures and leaves every other side unchanged. A tainted response decision needs a real deciding-side locked cost, cannot improve any side and marks an actor-lowering option watched under 0063. Appointment, a leaked report or replacing the actor cannot cancel its locked cost. Native nations act for themselves under 0069/0070; another side's raid cannot manufacture a national decision.

## What the seat sees

The principal sees received raid/repair requests, commander plans, disruption reports and attributed conduct notices. No exact enemy damage, unreported regional pressure or private practice value appears as truth. A report may claim success before contrary engineering news arrives. The player cannot choose a civilian destruction target or manage a nation's population through a military goal.

## Gate topics

R3/R6, guerrilla war, civilian conduct, occupation and protected agency are explicit. Relevant facets must be accepted, registered and signed before real content loads. Permanent collective-reprisal and protected-target refusals remain even after a hold is lifted. An incident's text-only placement cannot acquire a hidden positive supply effect.

## Tests and liveness

Military target accepted; civilian/protected target rejected through every goal/initiative/effect route; raid damage once; repair costs/refusal; neutral passage; duplicate/replayed outcome. Paired worlds with identical received reports but different hidden damage yield identical drafts; failed actual travel later changes knowledge. Test no repair duplication or free resupply.

Conduct probes cover option, inaction, delegate, practice, appointment and chained event routes. Sign checks follow pressure to loyalty/supply/economy/wills; a benefit cannot hide in a second owner or direction. Test nation's own authorised act and reject a foreign principal's proxy act. Save/replay during damage, repair and policy consequence. Count raids/repairs, actual condition magnitude, reports, pressure changes, lawful responses and refusal classes; D6 requires all reachable consequences measured, not a damage count alone.

## Dependencies and proposed ruling

a needs 1.23 knowledge, 1.30 passage/capacity and 1.31 operation contracts. b also needs 1.16/R3/R6 and 1.24 accepted practices. Real raids precede only the content cases using them; b's held facets need not stop unrelated repair work. 1.39 consumes both paths' coverage evidence.

Recommend two M briefs and military infrastructure targets only [I]. Reject guerrilla counters the player moves, true-damage planning and a harsh-commander appointment used to evade permanent refusals.
