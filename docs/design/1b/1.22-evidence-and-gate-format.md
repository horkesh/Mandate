## Mechanisms and typed terms

Propose `EvidenceDate = exact(day) | month(year,month) | year(year) | range(earliest,latest) | unknown(reason)` [I]. Comparisons return `before_or_equal`, `after`, `overlaps`, or `unknown`. A bound wholly before the cell cutoff qualifies on date; wholly after fails; overlap/unknown cannot silently establish a contemporary leg. Retain warning-versus-error behaviour required by 0087; any strengthening needs an explicit lead record. Gregorian bound conversion uses existing pure calendar helpers and integer day ordinals, never runtime `Date`.

`SourceDocument` identifies the document's author/date/pages separately from its container author/publication. A primary letter printed in a secondary work qualifies only for its own words. Memoir weakness and banned-source policy remain inherited. A quoted document does not promote the surrounding biography.

`SignatureSubject` is a tagged union of existing World id namespaces and content kinds. Resolve the exact subject in its namespace; reject a person id used as a post, a deleted source or an unknown facet. Canonical hash input includes precisely the newly accepted fields. Inventory old signed subjects before changing any schema or hash recipe; preserve their bytes where compatible or return an exact re-review list.

`MeasureDirection {measureId, unit, favourable: higher|lower|unordered, actingSide, protected, agencyReasonType}` [I] declares interpretation. A raw increase in debt is not a beneficial term merely because it is positive. Atrocity sign checks compare contributions in the declared favourable direction. Unordered quantities cannot be optimised or used as a beneficial sink. Do not silently reinterpret existing signed fields; the gate reviewer accepts any direction change.

## Gate topics

Source checks and directions can affect every gate topic. Preserve the permanent 0074 refusals and 0069/0070 nation-act guard. A schema migration is not approval of a placement, and an unresolvable old signature is not allowed to pass by omitting its subject. Whole-topic release holds remain independent of source-validator success.

## Tests and liveness

Date matrix: exact cutoff, prior/following day, leap month/year, interval wholly before/after/overlapping, malformed/reversed bounds, unknown. Provenance matrix: printed primary leg, adjacent secondary commentary, self-appraisal as reputation, weak memoir and banned key. Subject matrix: unknown id, wrong namespace, unknown source, stale hash, changed question version, preserved old signature.

Direction tests pair higher/lower measures and verify the acting-side sign with explicit conversion terms; reject protected measure targets and unordered scoring. Snapshot schema regeneration must be deterministic. This format lane has validator acceptance/rejection counts, not simulation firing bands. 1.39 later counts live consequence readers; a successful schema check cannot certify them.

## Dependencies and proposed ruling

Depends on 1.15's contract, draft-only 1.16 field needs and 1.17/1.19 examples. It precedes 1.16 registry implementation and 1.23/new stocks, but does not wait for completed gate signatures. Publish source/date and direction contracts separately so unrelated consumers can proceed.

Recommend four-valued date comparison and namespace-resolved signatures [I]. Reject fabricated exact days, whole-container primary promotion, raw-sign assumptions and silent invalidation/re-signing of accepted content.
