## Mechanisms and typed terms

a: `ProcurementTerm` records lawful payment, ordinary goods ordered/delivered, quality/compatible arms, delivery delay, stores use, capacity shortfall and refusal, with unit and origin [I]. Paying a contract and receiving goods are distinct events. A delivery cannot issue equipment twice after save/resume. `economy` debits goods; `forces` alone changes `fbEquipment`/stores. Validate the whole proposed transaction before any owner applies it; no paid-but-duplicated delivery or half-created force.

b: A call reaches the governor, whose answer creates `postLevies`; a muster proposes create/place/assign under the existing three owners. Refill requests instead name an existing eligible unit and travel through the War Department; a governor's new regiment is not a refill. `RecruitmentReason` includes `new_call`, `answered`, `muster_due`, `term_expired`, `refill_eligible`, `not_eligible`, `equipment_shortfall`, `already_applied`. Keep national volunteer allocation and Regular enlistment distinct where S1 has accepted their paths; do not repair them here by counting transfers as recruits.

c: Extend only the restricted reader of 0078's two-cause derivation: resistance/flight within enslaving regions, and people's flight to Union lines, including slave states held by that side. `CapacityTerm` carries the accepted agency type, region, affected ordinary capacity/supply measure, bounded signed contribution and source rule. It does not transfer people or labour to Union stocks. Arrivals remain solely a historical State record owned by `regions`; they cannot be read as recruit availability, paid labour, a spending balance or a UI target.

Any capacity reduction must reach an actual production/procurement/supply constraint and thence operations or politics; an explanation-only term does not complete c. No elected policy is a direct rate dial on protected people. Accepted institutional policy changes conditions; people act through the derived mechanism. Unknown census/coverage remains explicit and never silently expands into national precision.

d: R2 must accept an agency-safe formation contract before implementation. Recommend a pure `ServiceOfferResponse` derived from source-backed voluntary offers, service conditions and lawful institutional acceptance [I], then an ordinary whole create/place/assign proposal. `ServiceOfferReason` distinguishes `offer_made`, `policy_known`, `conditions_unmet`, `offer_declined`, `institution_accepted`, `capture_boundary_held` and `already_acted`. Its numbers describe the offered service, with source/uncertainty, not a protected population remaining to recruit. The president can set lawful acceptance policy through holders but cannot request a number by race, spend a cohort or issue a governor call to a protected group. The derivation cannot read protected census/arrivals under a new alias; 0078's reader restriction remains unless an exact superseding R2 rule explicitly changes it. Preserve an accepted offer's causal identity in existing occurrence/enlistment history so the same offer cannot form another unit after expiry/disband. Force ids derive from the accepted origin; source-held strength stays unknown and cannot pass a battle-strength criterion. Exact admissible offer data, authority, placement and Black-voiced evidence remain R2 research/review prerequisites. d is not hidden inside ordinary call implementation.

## Tests and liveness

Procurement: paid/unpaid/refused/late/partial delivery, incompatible arms, duplicate id and multi-owner atomic rejection. Recruitment: new call→answer→new levy→muster; refill changes only the existing eligible unit; terms expire without recreation; transferred/detached men are conserved. Agency: both causes, Union-held slave-state case, unknown census, no arrivals-to-Union-stock path and actual downstream shortfall. Save/replay on each pending stage.

For d test that a policy alone creates no force, an eligible independently sourced offer can be accepted or declined, unmet conditions do not become consent, first protected-unit data is refused before R2/R4, duplicate/expired/disbanded origin cannot form twice, and race/count/arrival targeting is rejected. Exercise both holder refusal and actual owner-applied formation with replay/save. Count requests, answers, new levies, musters, refills, voluntary offers/acceptances/formations, goods delivered/equipped, capacity reductions and downstream shortages with distinct units. The post-policy border case must exercise a **new levy on an answered call**, not a transfer. A protected contribution without a real consumer remains unmeasured for D6; an unexercised recruitment chain remains unmet under 0099.
