## Mechanisms and typed terms

Propose one `WaveStageRow` per actual stage, with case/stage/occurrence predecessor, source pages, earliest supported window, causal condition, received surfacing, legal choices, inaction, default, advice/absence, effect proposals, relevant gate facets and proof [I]. `StageReadiness` is `held_source`, `held_gate`, `held_mechanism`, `authored_draft`, `driven`, or `accepted`; a report-only draft cannot skip to accepted.

Inventory S31–S45 as their causes arise, earlier S27–S31 continuations, S46 stage A's July draft and S55's July authorising-act stage. These are catalogue pointers, not historical dates newly certified by this note. Source-backed gaps such as DC policy and Congress's own acts belong in the inventory rather than disappear because they lack a numbered catalogue entry.

Institutional causality matters: a bill introduced is not passed law; a recruitment closure/reopening is a holder's act with its actual authority; a new command needs lawful posts/chains; a naval plan needs actual carriage/support. A political letter remains an attributed argument, not true wisdom or automatically a trait. Choose current holders under source-backed speaker rules; do not fire a historical man's act after he leaves the relevant office.

Each consequence names its owning proposal and receipt boundary. A relief can change a holder before its news arrives; a command to a now-retired post cannot be silently readdressed. A joint river case can wait for support/refill and remain visibly incomplete. Policy conditions expire illegal choices by actual accepted law; group/deferral choices carry all locked floors. History mode selects only sourced defaults, never scripts capture or obedience.
