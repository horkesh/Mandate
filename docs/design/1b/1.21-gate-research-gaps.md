## Mechanisms and typed terms

Propose `FacetDossier {topicId, facetId, window, claims, voices, acts, conflicts, missingEvidence, intendedConsumer, disposition}` [I]. Each `Claim` has source key, printed page or document entry, date precision, exact scope and inference marker. Each `Voice` identifies speaker, relationship to the event, mediation/translation and publication limits. A source about a people is not automatically a source from those people.

`NationActEvidence` names the acting nation, authorised body, signatories, constituency, institutional authority and territory actually covered. A disputed or factional act cannot silently bind every nation or every member. Keep civil jurisdiction, military control and diplomatic recognition separate.

`ConditionEvidence` distinguishes policy prescribed, conditions documented, personal experience and later recollection. Missing camp or prison evidence does not mean benign conditions. Numbers disputed by the sources remain ranges with competing citations; the research lane does not select an exact number to satisfy a renderer. Quotation excerpts retain the coercive context; omit neither slavery content nor the source's mediation.
