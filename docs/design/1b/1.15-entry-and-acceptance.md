## Mechanisms and typed terms

Propose an external `AcceptanceRow` with `criterionId`, `milestone`, `binding`, `measureId`, `unit`, `population`, `asOf`, `window`, `sourcePages`, `uncertainty`, `owner` and `evidencePath` [I]. A bound is either `known {low, high}` or `hold {reason}`. Zero is a measured zero, never a spelling of unknown.

A `Disposition` is `satisfied`, `unmet`, `carried {owner, decision}`, or `outside-window {nextOwner}`. “Carried” does not mean passed. Its reason records the acceptance row and the evidence that remains owed. A `StageRow` identifies catalogue case, stage, causal predecessor, receiving seat, consequence owners, gate facets and next-window owner. It prevents one November/December case being counted twice or disappearing at the cutoff.

Sampling dates in the parent proposal remain suggested design dates [I], not discovered historical thresholds. Define election-held, return-received, certified and seated measures separately before research supplies numbers. Describe every comparator's inclusions and exclusions; enrolled and present-for-duty strength cannot share a band merely because both count people.

The inventory audit must map each in-window stage to exactly one primary lane, every inherited finding to an owner, and each post-window stage to a named continuation. Reject a numeric band without units, covered population, page evidence and binding status. Audit the sub-brief dependency graph for cycles; a draft contract and its completed consumer must be separate nodes.

This documentation lane has no mechanic firing band. Its coverage denominator is the full inventory: every stage has a disposition, including HOLD. 1.39 later supplies runtime counts, magnitude bands and explicit quiet reasons. Freeze this contract before its calibration budget starts; a later change needs a lead disposition, not an author silently widening a bound.
