## Mechanisms and typed terms

Propose `EconomicObservation {seriesId, subject, measure, unit, scale, low, high, asOf, periodStart, periodEnd, grossOrNet, nominalOrReal, sourceKey, pages, publicOn, knownBy, uncertainty}` [I]. Different currency issues, book balances, authorised borrowing, subscriptions, proceeds and expenditures remain separate measures. A derived conversion names its input rows and rounding rule [I]; no source-incompatible subtraction is allowed.

`ForeignActEvidence` records actor, institution, communication, requested response, dispatch/receipt, expiry and the source's claimed cause. Public opinion, cabinet discussion and an executed policy are different kinds. A `LagCandidate` is observed, bounded or unknown; a selected pack lag is labelled a design parameter where observation does not establish it.

`RailInput` names a line, gauge, route endpoints, capacity unit/time basis, rolling-stock limitation and transfer requirement. `BlockadeObservation` identifies station/coverage/window, service or interception measure and uncertainty. Distinguish stocks from flows and a nominal blockade declaration from supplied service. Directions of future clocks are declared by 1.22/1.27; a positive historical observation is not automatically a beneficial game term.

## What the seat sees

The future seat receives Treasury returns, diplomatic dispatches, contractor answers and Navy reports with dates and sources. Private diplomatic intentions, true line allocation and present station stores remain unavailable until a relevant report. A confidence/gold-premium observation is a reported market condition, not a truthful forecast. A foreign ultimatum cannot expire before the recipient could lawfully respond unless the authored evidence explicitly supports that condition.

## Pack data and source hand-off

Consume reviewed S5 `sprint/research-foreign-economy`, pinning its actual commits and retained pages. Join S4 `sprint/research-military` for naval stations, river/rail routes and stores; S3 `sprint/research-politics` for laws and authority; S6 for holders; S7 for protected agency and policy terms. This design note supplies no freshly verified external fact.

Needed where missing: both sides' borrowing/issue/appropriation histories, usable proceeds, dated prices and gold-premium definitions, procurement schedules and delivery evidence, named rail capacity, cotton/trade series, blockade station service, foreign response instruments and contemporary receipt dates. S56's later bond-sales observation cannot become an earlier autumn cause; acquire an earlier observation or keep that stage held.

## Gate topics

Blockade hardships, slavery-linked policy, impressment, coerced labour and protected populations touch the register. Do not source a player-spendable labour stock from census or arrivals. Any agency-derived capacity explanation goes through 1.16/1.29 with an agency reason. No numeric “pay saving” rewards unequal Black service conditions.

## Tests and liveness

Audit period/scale consistency, source/page resolution, series revisions, duplicated flows and stock/flow distinctions. A reconciliation reports its residual and exclusions; unexplained balancing is a failure. Each input identifies which future mechanism consumes it and which data still cannot support a band.

Research liveness is coverage of requested series and institutions, with explicit HOLDs. 1.27–1.32 later count actual holder refusals, issues, deliveries, shortages and station service; input completeness cannot replace those driven cases. A missing market series remains unknown rather than a favourable zero.

## Dependencies and proposed ruling

Depends on 1.15 and reviewed S5; physical crosswalks use 1.18. Feeds 1.27 clocks, 1.28 Treasury, 1.29 procurement, 1.30 rail and 1.39 bands. Accept a small unit/authority contract first so implementation need not wait for all secondary series.

Recommend retaining incompatible observations side by side [I]. Reject a universal currency total, an export-to-recognition conversion and treating a declaration as actual blockade strength.

## Findings outside my scope

Pressure weights and election/economy feedback remain proposed design parameters for their owners. Missing contemporary receipts or physical capacity measures require research, not a coefficient chosen to fit a campaign.
