## Mechanisms and typed terms

Propose `PressureTerm {powerOrSide, kind, sourceEvent, period, raw, coefficientPermille, delta, direction, reason}` [I], with closed kinds `blockade_service`, `trade_condition`, `received_campaign_claim`, `policy_act`, `diplomatic_act`, `decay`, `floor`, `ceiling`. Each input states whether the relevant foreign institution acts on public/received information or an actual administrative condition. Enemy battlefield truth cannot leak into a foreign decision simply because the clock can read State.

Declare directions in 1.22's measure registry: higher intervention pressure is not automatically “better” for every side, while blockade pressure burdens the affected side. Explanations identify whose measure moves. Sum and clamp with returned terms and deterministic rounding. No personality roll chooses recognition, compliance or release of a detained ship. If exogenous trade/weather noise is later justified, it uses a separately named seeded stream and never replaces the authored institution's decision rules.

Foreign demands are authored institutional acts with source-backed cause, intended recipient, dispatch/receipt and expiry. The Secretary of State receives them and proposes a response; a principal's order returns through that holder's compliance. Outcome/inaction/delegation are separate. Threshold crossing can make an act eligible, but recognition is not a universal pressure-at-1000 switch. Exact historical defaults remain hidden and need sources.

`StationObservation` is a pure input contract: station id, side, window, active supplied service, coverage and explanation references. Fictional tests can supply it to a before carriage exists; that is a contract fixture, not a real blockade implementation. b replaces that fixture with 1.32's actual service and a received Navy account; no permanent default-full-strength adapter remains.

## What the seat sees

The seat sees diplomatic dispatches, reported foreign opinion, actual demands once received and dated Navy returns. It does not see an exact private foreign clock, future recognition, hidden station stores or undisclosed diplomatic motives. Staff estimates can explain known pressures with uncertainty. The existing mandate and the eventual verdict use only their accepted projections; clocks do not grant a new oracle screen.

## Gate topics

Policy/emancipation, blockade hardship, coerced labour and foreign responses touch 1.16/R7 and the Navy/economy register. Protected agency contributes only through an accepted derived reason. Atrocity-class conduct cannot improve its acting side's foreign measure under the declared direction. A foreign lever cannot make a forbidden domestic act profitable indirectly.

## Tests and liveness

Test independent British/French reactions, direction/clamp sums, no noise deciding a response, delayed/filtered demands, expiry, holder refusal and no effect before its actual institutional cause. Two worlds with the same diplomatic knowledge but different hidden battles must produce equal knowledge-dependent acts. Station tests compare supplied active service, empty stores, relief and overlap; duplicate stations cannot double-count coverage.

Count pressure updates and magnitudes per power, demands eligible/delivered/answered/expired, policy-linked terms and actual blockade service. Appropriate reference acts need researched opportunity windows and bands; a silent foreign clock is not live because a fixture can set it. Final b evidence needs real carriage/station inputs, save/resume and forward-channel D6 coverage.
