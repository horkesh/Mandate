# 1.24: People who change during the campaign

Status: proposed for the lead, 2026-11-20; S11 design only [I]. Refines [the 1B proposal](../PHASE-1B-PROPOSAL.md). This proposes mechanisms, not new historical centres or a policy that backdates later evidence.

## Purpose and boundary

Make battles, recorded stances, relief and policy grievances matter through the people already in play. Split into three M briefs [I]: battle/earned traits/assessment/recovery; stance reversal/relief; ladder/practice consequences. Each contains distinct hidden truth and reported effects; the earlier three-step L estimate omitted their separate integration evidence.

Read [0020](../../decisions/0020-people-hidden-stats-and-assessment.md), [0079](../../decisions/0079-the-people-verbs-as-built.md), [0082](../../decisions/0082-the-value-map-in-play.md), [0087](../../decisions/0087-evidence-rules-as-checks.md) and [0105](../../decisions/0105-1as-first-calibration-round-continue-or-park.md). A victory can raise reputation without raising nerve. Personality responses remain deterministic; setup draws do not authorise new obedience dice.

## Mechanisms and typed terms

`PeopleEvolutionTerm` identifies person, originating event, trait/stat, input, coefficient, signed delta, clamp and closed reason (`experience`, `trait_response`, `wound`, `recovery`, `stance_reversed`, `relief_mode`, `practice_clash`) [I]. Apply one event once. Separate actual battle experience from public standing moved by received news. Nerve response uses the person's accepted traits/context; no universal win-to-boldness rule. New innate or later-entry evidence policy is held for the lead rather than smuggled into this mechanism.

Assessment groups claims by independent `claimOrigin` from 1.23 before weighting. Reprints add readership, not independent evidence. A missing report widens/retains uncertainty; it cannot be filled from `personTrue`. Return stated confidence/range, origin count, age and bias terms. Adviser credibility remains separately derived from the principal's received advice/outcomes.

A stance reversal compares a received new position to the same subject's earlier recorded stance. The `stance_reversed` consistency cost is on that person, not an automatic moral judgement by the narrator. Relief creates mode-specific grievance and proposed political reaction; 1.26 owns bloc changes and the press owns received publicity. Multiple aliases of one relief cannot pay or apply the same cost twice.

Grievances must reach willingness, actual response, ladder conduct and an operational or political consequence. `LadderReason` identifies the underlying grievance, threshold, current rung, available act and legal refusal. A mere nonzero grievance variable is not D6 coverage. Practice changes need an accepted actor/event and retain previous practice history for already-issued orders; they cannot retroactively make a locked policy cost vanish. `costOnly` values never give positive willingness, regard, standing or grievance relief.

## What the seat sees

The principal sees staff assessments, recorded public stances, received objections, rumours and the commander's stated explanation. Hidden true stats, actual rungs/weights, unreported recovery and private grievance totals do not appear as truthful sliders. A public act may be reported differently by rival sources. The separate reckoning may compare accounts only under 1.38/R5.

## Gate topics

Policy grievance/practice touches fugitive policy, emancipation, civilian conduct and service. Gate-sign exact value maps under 1.16. Preserve 0074's cost-only and permanent refusals; an appointment or changed practice cannot make atrocity-class conduct improve the acting side. Recovery never paroles/exchanges a captured person; R4 owns that boundary.

## Tests and liveness

Drive a battle update once, opposing nerve responses, no-report standing independence, earned-history pruning and wounded return with/without eligibility. Poison true stats in assessment; cloned reprints cannot improve independence. Test same-subject reversal, different-subject non-reversal, each relief mode, ladder threshold/cooldown, blocked illegal conduct and practice change after an old order. Save/replay before and after each event.

Count actual requests separately from initiatives; battle updates, recovery reviews/returns, stance costs, relief reactions, ladder actions and changed compliance/operations have separate fire/magnitude bands. A grief-only score or a forced refusal fixture does not certify campaign pushback. Retain 0105's distinct initiative/request calibration and 1.39's forward-channel coverage.

## Dependencies and proposed ruling

Depends on 1.17, 1.22/1.23 and relevant 1.16 practice facets. Relief deltas feed 1.26b; initiative eligibility feeds 1.31; 1.37 uses the ordinary machinery. Politics already owns a support-delta interface, so battle/stance work need not wait for complete elections.

Recommend three M briefs and origin-based assessment [I]. Reject success-as-nerve, captured-status recovery, private truth in advice, arbitrary extra personality rolls and a grievance count used as a substitute for consequences.
