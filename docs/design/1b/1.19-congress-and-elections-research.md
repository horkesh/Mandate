## Mechanisms and typed terms

Propose `ElectionLawRow {stateId, officeKind, districtId, legalInstrument, electorateDefinition, soldierRule, heldOn, returnProcess, certificationProcess, seatingRule, sourcePages, uncertainty}` [I]. `soldierRule` is permitted, restricted, absent or HOLD with the exact jurisdiction/date; it is not one federal toggle.

`ElectionObservation` separates voted, unofficial-return, certified, contested and seated stages with their own dates and source identities. A `PartyCrosswalk` maps period labels into model categories for a specified contest; an unresolved coalition remains distinct. Record seat-count denominator, vacancies, contested returns and pending elections before summing composition.

For Congress, `InstitutionClaim` distinguishes convening authority, chamber sitting, session identity, committee remit, bill introduction, passage, assent and execution. `PoliticalCase` records who resisted whom, the received request, actual act, private/public evidence and a lower-bound counting rule. Design ceilings remain explicit [I] and do not turn a vote margin into regard or competence.

## What the seat sees

The future seat receives papers and returns at their real routed stages. It may know an election is scheduled while lacking a result. A reader sees “pending” with its institutional reason, not a losing result guessed from a national swing. Committee accusations and newspaper claims remain attributed reports, not true private motives.

## Gate topics

Confiscation, emancipation, Black service, slavery-linked voting exclusion, arrests and committee detention touch the register even when represented as legislative history. Link exact facets to 1.16; do not suppress exclusion from the description of the lawful electorate. Protected people do not become a managed political bloc. An investigation is not authority for a free arrest verb.

## Tests and liveness

Audit every jurisdiction required by 1.15, source/page joins, non-overlapping stages, party crosswalk completeness and count denominators. Flag uncertain or conflicting law explicitly; a HOLD cannot be converted into the modal state's rule. Check that a pending contest is not scored as a zero-seat party result and an eventual return is not backdated.

Research coverage is the count of jurisdictions and stages resolved or held. Runtime liveness belongs to 1.26: sessions open/close, bills can fail, requests receive answers, elections occur and returns travel. Research completeness alone proves none of those mechanisms fires.
