## Mechanisms and typed terms

a: A person can submit a kernel-generated candidate without being the eventual executor. `ProposalTerm` distinguishes origin, source knowledge, objective, method, supply/capacity gate and outsider-plan friction [I]. Draft once on the proposal day from that person's lawful knowledge; save identity and content. The principal adopts by attaching its reference to a goal for a holder. That holder receives it like an order, may object, and drafts within his own knowledge and compliance. Adoption neither teleports the original plan into execution nor forces historical obedience.

b: Add a bounded siege method to generate/filter/score. `PlanTerm` extensions include `siege_supply`, `works_progress`, `reported_resistance`, `garrison_requirement`, `capacity_wait` and `weather_delay`, with explicit units and gate reasons. A siege consumes time/stores, requests support, reports progress and can pause, assault, withdraw or end under the commander. No player inputs approaches/mines or exact tactical effort. Planner-authored garrison phases choose blocks through the commander, propose detachments at lawful locations and preserve parent identity/men/stores. Garrison strength is not a direct president's slider.

The river candidate contract identifies required carrier group, support holder, route, loading/landing windows, ordinary supply demand and gates. It uses 1.30 passage/capacity and 1.23 reported obstacles. A candidate is not proof that embarkation, sailing or Navy combat exists. 1.32 consumes this immutable contract; c later integrates actual outcomes.

c: Capacity-aware planning uses **known** allocations and produces requests rather than reading the global true allocation ledger. Actual execution obeys settled grants. A self-directed initiative proposes a goal for the commander's own post through a kernel-validated initiative path and its saved draft. It is not an `OrderCommand` from a non-principal and grants no authority over other holders. 0052's principal-only order rule remains; the lead must accept the exact initiative boundary before c is built. `InitiativeReason` includes `eligible`, `cooldown`, `conflicting_order`, `no_legal_candidate`, `support_pending` and `forbidden_target`. No initiative may violate neutral permission, permanent gate refusals or a prior command merely because it is called freelancing. Existing judgement/initiative weights remain distinct from requested-order compliance.

Candidate scoring returns all typed contributions and every hard rejection. Maintain per-side drafting caps, one passage rule and one-level methods. Send-back marks and arrow-edit grammar retain 0102 semantics; a new method does not make marks binding or expose actual note weights to the principal.

## What the seat sees

The seat sees the received proposal, the recipient's submitted plan, arrows, stated conditions, progress/shortage reports and legal review choices. It does not see present hidden obstructions, the full candidate search, true assessment inputs, actual initiative thresholds or a promised tactical result. Editing remains a counter-order to a commander. Repeated preview/open operations do not regenerate proposal identities or draw randomness.

## Gate topics

Siege hardship, civilian conduct, Black service and protected nations use exact 1.16 facets. A siege/raid goal cannot name a protected population or civilian destruction as its objective. The interim Confederate rule applies to conduct. A self-directed act does not bypass event-only placement or sign rules.

## Tests and liveness

Proposal→receipt→adoption→recipient refusal/conditional reply→saved draft→approval→execution; changed map cannot regenerate the original proposal. Siege: sustained stores, shortage pause, support refusal, progress reports and withdrawal. Garrison: created by planner, parent/detachment conservation, later transfer/rejoin and no direct unit command. River: withheld support, two-carrier group, blocked actual edge despite unchanged belief and eventual landed operation after 1.32.

Freelancing: cooldown, conflicting orders, legitimate initiative, illegal target rejection and deterministic id ties. Test score sums, all rejections, frozen State, preview/arrival on unchanged knowledge and save/replay through adoption/assembly/siege. Count proposals/adoptions/refusals, siege stages and magnitudes, planner garrisons, capacity waits and initiatives separately from orders. A drafted-but-never-executed river/siege case remains unmet.
