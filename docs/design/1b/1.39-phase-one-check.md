# 1.39: Binding Phase 1 checks and bounded calibration

Status: proposed for the lead, 2026-11-20; S11 design only [I]. Refines [the 1B proposal](../PHASE-1B-PROPOSAL.md). Proposed acceptance and score extensions require a lead record; existing failed criteria and gate requirements remain binding.

## Purpose and boundary

Measure the completed campaign against a source-backed contract without replacing that contract with a convenient proxy. Split into three M briefs [I]: harness/observability; integrated references and complete sweep coverage; at most eight measured one-change calibration runs with dispositions. The earlier M underestimated instrumentation, the longer references and the separate calibration budget.

## Mechanisms and typed terms

`CriterionResult {id, milestone, binding, status, evidence, problems}` uses `pass`, `fail` or `unresolved`, where unresolved is non-passing when binding [I]. `ObservationMeasure` declares unit, population, as-of, unknown coverage and source comparison. Unknown strength is a failure; incompatible present/enrolled measures are not silently converted. Preserve every still-binding Phase 0/1A criterion beside the new contract.

`ChannelCoverage` follows all forward readers of selected effects, policy rows and set/clear flags to a fixed point. Include people grievance→compliance/ladder→operations/politics, agency→actual capacity/supply, foreign acts, Treasury/procurement, wills, recurring/grouped decisions and law expiry. Record every distinct reader and sink. A channel without a real measured consequence is unmeasured, and D6 evidence for that decision is invalid even if the watched move is best in zero seeds.

The victory-only bot tests options, lawful compatible groups, inaction, delegation to the actual delegate pick, paid deferral and causal reopening. Atrocity-class inaction is watched. Retain ties-as-best, the more-than-a-quarter rule, the defined eight-seed sweep and every unpaid floor as a breach. Follow any post-policy border chain until a **new call is answered into a new levy** within the accepted bound; transfers, existing levies and unexercised chains cannot substitute.

Propose `ScoreTerm {measure, inputIdentity, unit, raw, coefficient, contribution, coverage}` [I]. Extending 0099's score needs acceptance, with a no-double-count rationale: spending cash and its resulting equipment, or a policy and its entire bloc/will effect, cannot both be counted simply because both fields exist. Protected agency is never scored as a spendable quantity. A poor proxy is a design finding to resolve before tuning, not a target to optimise.

## What the seat sees

Harness diagnostics are developer artifacts. The game sees the accepted report/mandate/verdict interfaces, never checkpoint target bands, bot scores, hidden defaults or a true enemy-state debug panel. Terminal verdict wording remains R5; a runner's November stop is a labelled milestone, not the player's victory. Raw private truth needed for audit stays outside the ordinary view boundary.

## Gate topics

D5/D6, floors, E7 directions, all relevant R2–R7 and camps remain independent of military success. Loader/signature/release checks each report their own result. A held whole topic is not made passing by excluding its decisions from the sweep. A signature certifies reviewed content, not that every downstream mechanism is measured.

## Tests and liveness

Cheap gates first: workspace links, compiler, fixture opening, relevant owner tests, generated/schema freshness, gate/refusal probes and one-day startup. Unit-test scorers on missing/incompatible observations and a deliberately unmeasured reader; prove the graph finds every alias/clear/set route actually used. Add a mutation-sensitive case for each new consequence/criterion rather than tests that repeat its implementation.

Before long work, record head, command, seeds, question, exact pass bounds, estimated time and stop rule. Run one full reference before multiplying cost into the defined sweep. Same-day hashes, replay, saves across institutions/elections/embarkation/groups, frozen State, retention bounds and Linux/Windows comparison remain required. Timing uses all active work with the unchanged 40 ms median/160 ms p95 limits unless superseded; local measurements do not certify hosted CI.

Each mechanic has fire and magnitude bands plus an explicit quiet reason/driver where no opportunity exists. Schema readiness, nonzero counters and a default exit cannot replace required opportunity/consequence evidence. Keep logs/artifacts and failed categories. After a failure stop expensive retries, diagnose all categories, cheaply verify correction, then rerun only the affected evidence.

## Dependencies and proposed ruling

Harness work begins with 1.15 and accepted owner contracts; final measurement waits for 1.16 clearance/holds honestly reported, 1.36/1.37 integration and 1.38 complete scripts/retention. Eight measured calibration changes are a separate budget from eight sweep seeds. One change per measured run, then a continue-or-park recommendation per unmet criterion. Another campaign needs the lead's concrete scope/cost/stop decision. Parked remains failed.

Recommend three M briefs, complete channel coverage and binding milestone reporting [I]. Reject tuning questionable proxies, raised bounds for convenience, partial D6 clearance and extra seeds/runs for reassurance.
