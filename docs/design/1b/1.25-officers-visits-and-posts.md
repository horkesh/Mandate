## Mechanisms and typed terms

a: `DiscoveryReason` distinguishes real roster mention, invented eligible mention, already surfaced, unresolved identity and name collision. Derive a fictional id from report origin/mention identity; any seeded generation uses a named exogenous identity stream once, never new personality decisions on every inquiry. Check generated names/background combinations against the real roster and previously generated identities. A real-person match or uncertain identity returns HOLD, not an invented biography under that name.

`NominationTerm` names lawful rank kind, vocabulary rank, seniority comparison, authority, session and attention cost. The kernel validates every amended candidate, including rank-kind compatibility and unresolved seniority; it cannot trust the UI list. Approving as drafted is free; each amendment uses the accepted attention table. Confirmation/refusal is Congress's act; session lapse does not automatically change rank. `people` applies the confirmed rank; `command` separately appoints to a lawful post. A civilian commission does not universally imply army-bloc membership; 1.17 supplies facts and the lead rules on any new membership policy.

b: `VisitReason` covers travel, away, arrived, return, delegation-unavailable and report-received. Days advance normally while the principal travels; cabinet/Congress business follows the accepted inaction/delegation policy. Visits yield several independently originating officer accounts with bias, not a truth view. Availability and attention are recomputed from `seatVisits`; no second calendar is saved. Honour changes public/people consequences through the existing owners with evidence and pack costs; it is not a hidden stat reveal or certainty of obedience.

c: `PostLifecycleProposal` names institutional act, affected posts, successor chain, holder dispositions, force-assignment mapping and old-address treatment. Validate all ids, authority, acyclic chain, side compatibility and every assigned block before applying. Creation, merger and abolition keep stable retired identities for command-log/replay references; no old dispatch is retargeted silently. A forwarded letter retains its original addressee, routing and new forwarding receipt. Corps remain posts without adding the deferred inside-army relay mechanic. Houston's office-service compatibility and personal post-office eligibility are separate facts; source the latter rather than inventing a refusal trait.

Use one `personServiceRestrictions` contract for actual sourced legal restrictions [I], with `RestrictionReason` kinds `appointment_bar`, `custody`, `parole_bar` and `released_by_act`, each with actor/authority, causal origin, effective day and separately evidenced release. `people` exports the one availability derivation from status, service, ranks and these restrictions; do not save the resulting eligibility boolean. A research HOLD is not a legal bar. Appointment menus use received/public evidence and revalidate actual availability on arrival. Committee custody is not combat capture; ending a restriction never resurrects a killed person or clears captured status outside accepted R4. Publish this generic contract in c before its later custody/parole consumers, without letting an accepted generic schema authorise a held facet.

## What the seat sees

The seat sees received mentions, public identities, nomination lists, lawful amendment costs, confirmation/lapse notices, travel status and visit reports. It sees known vacancies/changes, not hidden present service or exact junior ability. Routine lists, promotions and returns enter the inbox and do not end a turn under FOUNDATION section 9. A visit does not let the president move troops or bypass every ordinary command restriction.

## Gate topics

Real identities and attributed motives obey evidence policy. Black service, Native actors, appointments affecting civilians and slavery-related honours require exact 1.16 facets. No pool of protected recruits, no real-person generated likeness, and no honour that rewards atrocity-class conduct. Post jurisdiction proposals always use the 0069/0070 nation-act guard where relevant.

## Tests and liveness

Discovery: same origin is stable across inquiry/replay; real/generated collisions reject; different reprints do not generate new men. Lists: rank vocabulary, seniority, repeated amendment charges, approval free, confirmation/refusal/lapse, vacancy and changed service on arrival. Visit: travel with defaults, no delegation target, multiple biased origins, return, save midway, no truth leak and routine-return non-stop.

Lifecycle: merge occupied posts, abolish with assigned force, invalid successor/cycle, old-holder delayed letter, forwarding and one refused delta causing zero partial application. Restrictions: evidence HOLD is not a bar, sourced bar affects actual eligibility, delayed report does not leak, release requires its lawful act, and custody release cannot clear killed/captured status. Count discoveries, lists, confirmations/lapses, amendments, visits/returns, honours, post changes and refused changes with magnitudes. Each reference needs an eligible case or quiet reason plus driven test; an empty candidate list cannot pass as officer-pool coverage.
