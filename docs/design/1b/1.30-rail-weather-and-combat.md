## Mechanisms and typed terms

a: `RailDemand` identifies requester post, line, time window, traffic kind (`movement` or `supply`), capacity unit and priority. The holder receives requests, can object and returns a reservation; the principal sets priorities through that person. `RailTerm` identifies nominal capacity, damage/season reduction, earlier reservation, transfer/break-of-gauge cost, granted/refused share and stable id tie-break. Allocate in priority order, not maximum flow, under 0054.

Before actual movement/supply consumption, `economy` settles one per-window budget from accepted reservations and requests. Both consumers read the same grant/use identity. The same slot cannot carry a division and replenish its stores independently. Define release/expiry and unused capacity at the accepted phase boundary; rejected or duplicate use cannot debit a second time. The owner already ruled that gauge breaks are modelled (OPEN_QUESTIONS, “Track gauge on rail lines”): static named-line data must carry the sourced gauge, and both movement and supply pay the pack's transfer cost at a break. Research supplies the actual crosswalk and parameter basis; unknown gauge cannot silently mean compatible. This is not a new whether-to-model ruling. Preserve neutral passage permissions.

b: `WeatherTerm` records zone/day, stream key, condition, edge kind and length/capacity effect. Declare zones/distributions/season windows in pack rules; setup and each day's update have a fixed phase before actual consumers. Commanders read 1.23's reports/priors, while actual movement can find worse conditions. A winter campaign's resistance is an explained people response, not another weather die.

Combat supply quality derives from actual stores/equipment and accepted rules; only `forces` changes those stocks. Return distinct `BattleTerm` reasons for supply quality, weather, loss clamp and phase opportunity. Tests must establish which effect is already in the current formula to avoid applying weather or supply twice. No hidden environment coefficient lives outside World rules.

c: R93's confined approach is a proposed **amendment to 0076**, not permission to raise all assault losses. Distinguish men fighting abreast from men exposed over successive efforts and cap opportunities by actual defender fire. Proposed `ContactTerm` kinds are `frontage`, `rotation_exposure`, `defender_fire_bound`, `works`, `phase_duration` and `clamp` [I]. Research must justify the bounded definition and units before production changes. Preserve ordinary assaults and the Fredericksburg/Franklin fixtures. The later Wagner case is a labelled out-of-window diagnostic, never a 1B checkpoint or an invented in-window event. If the lead parks the amendment, retain that unmet scope explicitly while a/b can complete.
