## Mechanisms and typed terms

`FacetRegistration` identifies topic, placement, exact window, actor/direction, source ids and permanent refusals [I]. Registration cannot mean “lift every held-until-R4 pattern.” Preserve each unrelated refusal and add positive and negative probes for the exact facet.

Recommend R2's policy through institutions with locked political consequences; do not bank unequal pay as a saving. R2 also defines the agency-safe service-offer/formation contract which 1.29d implements, distinct from calls on ordinary governors. R3 needs nation-specific authority and Native-voiced evidence; retain 0069/0070's act guard. Recommend a sourced review/suspension/information process with every offered Dakota review choice watched and no execution-count or selected-person control [I]; exact options and inaction remain held for R3. R6 permits only accepted conduct and presidential restriction/referral/revocation, never a confirm or “let stand” option for an atrocity-class order. Letting it stand is inaction and remains watched. Preserve 0057/0060's stronger event rule: atrocity notices can only worsen their acting side's measures; other sides stay unchanged. Tainted decisions have real deciding-side costs, no favourable gain for any side, and any actor-lowering option retains 0063's watched mark. R7 drops `fund-a-scheme`, separates the shooting clause from emancipation and expires forbidden return policy by the accepted law condition. R5 supplies factual state/agency clauses, not a prediction of peace. Camps remain agency-centred events/text or accepted derived facets, never labour supply [I].

If c is authorised, propose `ServiceReturnProposal {originLossId, originPersonOrForce, actId, eligibleDay, restriction, destinationPost, evidenceRef}`. The closed reasons are `held`, `release_unverified`, `parole_bar`, `duty_authorised`, `already_applied`, `origin_mismatch`, and `not_serving`. A missing origin, double return, total exceeding the originating eligible loss, dead person or unresolved legal restriction rejects the complete proposal. Return to duty does not promise a particular command; assignment is independently lawful.

Apply after validating all affected owners against one pre-state; either all deltas apply or none. Keep source-loss truth with `combat`: a minimal immutable origin survives 0080's full battle-record pruning only as long as accepted service-return validation needs it. `forces` stores applied return dispositions referencing that origin, not a copied independently mutable loss count. The remaining eligible bound is derived from the original loss, lawful restrictions and already-applied returns; it is never a captor's or player's stock. The accepted R4 record must specify this narrow retention extension and its bound before code. Replays and reloads cannot reclaim the same loss twice. Any return quantity is the originating side's bounded service change, not a positive measure of prisoners held by the other side. Exact permitted release evidence and aggregate eligibility remain held for R4 to decide [I].
