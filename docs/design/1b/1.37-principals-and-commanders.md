## Mechanisms and typed terms

Propose `DoctrineCandidate {jobId, person, legalCommand, prerequisites, knownEvidence, priorityTerms, rejectionReasons}` [I]. `DoctrineReason` includes `office_vacant`, `holder_unavailable`, `known_threat`, `call_due`, `refill_need`, `authority_pending`, `capacity_pending`, `support_answer`, `proposal_ready`, `review_ready`, `knowledge_missing`, `cooldown`, `gate_refused` and `already_satisfied`. Return every contributing priority and every failed prerequisite, including all readers of a flag. Deterministic id ties replace personality dice.

Principal jobs include lawful appointments and command structures, new calls, refills, Treasury/procurement priorities, support/operations and responses to situations. Commanders receive goals, choose saved drafts, request support and answer through their own compliance. The principal may replace a lost commander only through the appointment verb and current eligibility; loss news and real status stay separate. A capital move uses accepted command legality and its received threat basis, not unseen enemy ownership.

For each job define trigger, eligible holder, report required, legal command, actual completion observation, retry/cooldown rule and quiet reason. An answered call's new levy, not a transfer, proves recruitment work. A drafted plan is not a fought operation. Supplies cannot be assumed infinite while the seat issues a river goal. Adopted proposals still pass recipient compliance, and an unsolicited commander initiative remains distinguishable from a principal's request.

Consume one integrated content wave at a time. Wave one tests institutions/foreign/finance and appointment paths; wave two adds accepted command restructuring, capacity/siege/river work and summer institutions/policy; wave three adds autumn returns, relief and late-window duties. These are proposed groupings, not new historical claims. Avoid full-row cyclic waits: content supplies a complete fixture, doctrine exercises it, then the next content slice proceeds.

The Confederate rule seat uses the same legal people/transport/economy machinery with its own pack doctrine and pushback. Under 0046's interim rule, protected-population and atrocity-class Confederate acts remain authored events, not available policy choices. No Union-only doctrine exception gives another side perfect obedience or a free resource.

## What the seat sees

An AI/rule seat has only its lawful view and menus, as the player does. It may act on stale or biased news and receive refusal. The principal's view sees actual outgoing commands and received answers, not the rule seat's private score or future intended path. Explanations for developers remain external run diagnostics; player strings state the holder's concrete act/reason in plain English.
