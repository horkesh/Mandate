## Mechanisms and typed terms

Part a distinguishes authority from execution. `InstitutionReason` includes `not_sitting`, `wrong_chamber`, `not_introduced`, `pending`, `passed`, `rejected`, `assented`, `vetoed`, `override_pending`, `override_passed`, `override_failed`, `authority_expired` and `request_answered`. Bills follow the pack's institution-specific sequence, including sourced assent/veto/override authority. These acts use the ordinary validated decision/institutional path, not a new free-standing verb; the authority rules reject any delegation the sourced process does not permit. No presidential click makes a chamber agree. The existing accountability ledger still meets acts taken while away; do not replace it with another bill ledger. A committee request travels to its subject; testimony, detention and command relief are different consequences with different authority. The a1 session/authority contract is the first merge step; a2's complete investigation/custody path waits for 1.25c's generic people-owned restrictions. Custody and release use separately accepted civil-liberties/prisoner facets and actual acts, never a report-only arrest or the captured combat status. Duty consumers call the one availability derivation; receipt controls the principal's knowledge. Inventory remaining convention/legislature receipt-delay stand-ins after S1/0104, replacing only the live approximation with sourced institutional causes.

Part b chooses from an editor's received claims. `PressTerm` identifies origin, selection/slant, reach, attributed commander, claimed result and support/standing delta. Slant can change selection/emphasis within accepted truth/voice bounds; it cannot manufacture a true battle outcome. A leak is a person's attributable report to an editor, with disclosure scope, route and deterministic stated/private reasons. Enemy readership needs transit and filters; one origin remains one origin across reprints. Political actors can appeal, leak and oppose through the same people/ladder rules, never become an election bonus because their name is famous.

Part c proposes `ElectionTerm {contest, sourceMeasure, electorateScope, asOf, weightPermille, raw, contribution, reason}` [I]. Each state uses its own law/calendar and lawful soldier-vote arrangement. The response function must preserve researched bounds and disclose design coefficients; it cannot use a universal swing to override law. Resolve votes, unofficial return, certification, contest and seating separately. A November checkpoint can contain pending elections and returns. Freeze an actual result once its applicable process resolves; later received news affects knowledge, not the past vote.

`WillTerm` has side, closed reason kind (`blocs`, `losses`, `territory`, `armies`, `blockade`, `policy`, `event`, `floor`, `ceiling`), unit, coverage, contribution and input identity. Sum once and expose clamping as a term. Avoid adding an event again if its entire effect is already represented in blocs/losses; each retained direct term needs a distinct rationale. Two derived wills govern termination under 0028; the 1862 midterms are a checkpoint, not automatic campaign defeat. Evaluate all terminal causes from the same end-of-day state before freezing the verdict. Recommend a typed `simultaneous_break` disposition, retaining every cause and awarding no exclusive winner by id order, if both wills break on that day [I]; the lead/R5 must accept its exact wording and outcome policy before implementation. The 1864 losing-election rule remains defined for the later campaign and predicts no winner's peace.
