## Mechanisms and typed terms

`OccurrenceId` derives from case/chain, stage and its distinct causal event [I]. A recurrent case needs a new actual cause, not merely a read or the passage of a cooldown. `SituationReason` includes `cause_held`, `not_received`, `new_occurrence`, `already_resolved`, `option_appeared`, `option_expired`, `deferral_wait`, `deferral_released`, `incompatible_group` and `law_forbids`. Preserve true cause day, received surfacing day and clock origin separately.

Options can form a compatible set subject to declared exclusivity groups and lawful constraints. Validate the **whole set** before costs/effects; stable neutral display order is not an endorsement. Delegate uses the same legal sets as the principal, with actual versus stated response kept through `proxy`. Hidden historical defaults can select a valid set only in history mode. Advice remains an authored report or typed cited absence under 0100; provenance is not a recommendation.

Choosing a deferral is a real outcome: pay its own locked costs once and record its stance. Its predicate later reopens a defined occurrence/stage with the allowed options; it does not refund the wait or erase prior decisions. Every selected option's consequences/floor apply once per actual resolution. Deduplicate by occurrence/option/consequence identity, not by equal numerical amount. A shared group floor exists only if an accepted gate record explicitly defines it; otherwise combining choices cannot collapse costs. Repeated same-resolution commands are idempotent; a genuinely new recurrent policy decision pays again.

Expire illegal options by the accepted law/condition and revalidate on actual resolution/arrival. A previously displayed option is not an enduring legal permission. No deferral waits on hidden enemy truth when its stated condition is receipt. Surfacing/clock thresholds produce the accepted stops once, not every day a condition remains true.

Keep collective acts distinct from grouped choices. A petition or cabinet paper carries a typed `CollectiveActClaim` with participant ids, each signature/origin reference, author and as-of day [I]. `messages` carries the immutable body and `knowledge` owns it on receipt; declare those nested fields with the report extensions, not a new group-decision State engine. Each attributed participant must meet the sourced speaker/office condition in this playthrough. One person's report cannot give another a stance, knowledge or signature. A bargaining offer to leave office is not 0101's service-side resignation. Later content waves reuse this contract for the actual sourced case.

Choose the complete early case after source review [I]; S25's appointment chain is a candidate because it can exercise report→choice→holder change→received result without waiting for sea carriage. Do not create an appointment if its authority/holder source is held. A fictional mechanism fixture is not the real case. The proposed 1861 inventory is S22–S30, S31's late-December opening and continuations of S16–S18/S20, with every cross-window stage passed to 1.35 by identity.
