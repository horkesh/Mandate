# 1.28: Treasury, borrowing and lawful expenditure

Status: proposed for the lead, 2026-11-20; S11 design only [I]. Refines [the 1B proposal](../PHASE-1B-PROPOSAL.md). Proposed money units, instruments and market functions need an accepted record and researched input bands.

## Purpose and boundary

Make Congress authorise and Treasury execute, with actual holders who can delay or object. Split into two M briefs [I]: stocks/instruments/accounting; appropriations/holder execution/returns. The earlier M did not allow separate conservation and institutional integration. A Treasury menu is an order/request to a person, not a screen that buys a victory.

Read [FOUNDATION sections 7 and 10](../FOUNDATION.md#7-politics-the-press-and-the-home-front), [0023](../../decisions/0023-the-home-front.md), [0040](../../decisions/0040-first-month-revisions.md) and [0084](../../decisions/0084-politics-first-cut-as-built.md). The existing Congress ledger remains accountability for executive acts; it does not become a second cash balance.

## Mechanisms and typed terms

`MoneyAmount` names currency, integer minor unit, scale and amount [I]. Arithmetic stays in checked safe integers; pack validation bounds products and accrual periods. No floating currency conversion in the kernel. Incompatible issues or public quotations remain distinct until a sourced/design-labelled conversion is accepted. Ratios and rates use integer numerators/denominators or permille with an explicit rounding term.

`FinanceTerm` identifies `authority`, `issue_proceeds`, `loan_principal`, `interest`, `payment`, `maturity`, `commission`, `confidence`, `premium`, `rounding`, `refused_authority` or `insufficient_cash`, with amount/unit, origin and before/after. A loan's cash proceeds and obligation appear atomically. An appropriation increases legal room, not cash. Authorised borrowing is not subscribed money; subscriptions are not usable proceeds until their accepted settlement condition holds. Payments cannot silently make cash negative or bypass a restriction.

A holder receives a borrowing, issue, payment or bond-agent instruction through ordinary messages/compliance. Agent appointment is `command`'s act; Treasury's accounts remain `economy`'s. Reject stale authority and duplicate transaction ids before applying. Periodic interest carries its remainder so splitting a period cannot create or erase money. Taxes/other revenues enter only through researched institutional authority and accepted receipt rules; do not expand into a complete industrial-market simulation.

Derived confidence/premium terms reflect the chosen light economy and must disclose their inputs [I]. A reported quotation may differ from the model's internal condition. Foreign pressure can influence a declared term through 1.27a; neither clock nor will also writes the balance. Procurement under 1.29 requests a lawful payment reference and receives actual settled capacity, not an automatic unlock.

## What the seat sees

The principal sees dated Treasury returns, bills, agent replies, reported quotations and stated objections. A displayed account is the last received balance with its as-of date, not hidden current cash. Approval does not announce a loan's success before its holder/settlement returns. No private compliance score or predicted market certainty is shown.

Tax, appropriation, impressment, slavery-linked policy and pay facets require the relevant 1.16 boundary. Unequal Black pay cannot generate spendable savings; no protected labour resource enters a revenue account. Money cannot fund a refused colonisation scheme or cancel locked policy costs. ## Tests and liveness

Accounting fixtures: loan cash/debt equality, insufficient cash, stale/expired authority, duplicate settlement, differing currencies, integer bounds, interest remainder, maturity/default and refused holder. Institutional fixtures: bill passed without cash, cash without authority, session adjournment, delayed report, agent appointment refusal and partial procurement failure. Save/replay across issue, interest boundary and settlement.

Count requests, lawful/refused execution, settled amounts, outstanding/matured obligations, interest and delivered returns by unit. Required real references need sourced opportunity windows and compatible bands; one printed balance does not prove Treasury can execute. 1.39 follows financial consequences to capacity/politics/will without scoring both cash and the same purchased equipment twice.
