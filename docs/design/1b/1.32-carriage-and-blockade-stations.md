Make a supported multi-carrier axis load, sail, eat, land and return news, and derive blockade service from ships that can actually remain on station. Split three M briefs [I]: carriage/conservation; stores/stations; Navy encounters/reports and planner integration. ## Mechanisms and typed terms

a: `CarriageProposal` names operation/axis, carrier group, selected troops, legal loading/landing regions, capacity unit and support responses [I]. Validate capacity for **all carriers**, access, force availability, carrier location, route and every delta before applying. The commander chooses forces; the principal orders a holder. A ship's refusal or missing capacity keeps the transport waiting with a typed reason rather than sailing only the first carrier or abandoning the remaining troops.

`CarriageTerm` includes `load_capacity`, `carrier_missing`, `support_refused`, `assembly_wait`, `embarked`, `sailed`, `landing_blocked`, `landed`, `break_of_bulk`, `conservation` and explicit amounts/units. Loading cannot create men, stores or assignment. Troop and ship records both travel; an axis succeeds only after every required carrier's legal stage completes. Retain zero-strength parent shells where identity/rejoin needs them; do not “clean up” by deleting referents.

b: Existing stores are described as milli-days per block, not a common barrel stock. Consume ship stores for service and troop stores while afloat under pack norms, and explain any norm/unknown assumption. Refill through actual supplied port/tender/delivery capacity using 1.29/1.30's ordinary-goods contract. There is a concrete units seam: `forces/detachments.ts` currently splits and sums `fbStoresMilli`, as 0081 accepted. Recommend a ration-equivalent units audit and an explicit proposed 0081 amendment if physical split/rejoin accounting must change [I]. Preserve the accepted convention until that ruling; do not silently replace it with weighted days or claim it already proves physical ration conservation. Unknown supplies cannot grant perpetual full service.

`StationTerm` records station window, present lawful carrier, active service, supply shortfall, repair/relief and non-duplicated coverage. Derive effective service from actual presence and support; a posted ship at empty stores is not full blockade strength. `navalStationService` retains only the bounded periods needed for cumulative clocks/reporting, with deterministic aggregation. 1.27 receives actual station service separately from the Navy's reported claim.

c: Define a bounded Navy encounter at the existing block/graph scale; do not send ships through the land casualty formula by changing an arm tag. Proposed `NavalEncounterTerm` identifies support, obstacle, fire/exposure opportunity, stores, damage/loss and withdrawal. The lead must accept the precise extension to 0076 before execution changes; no detailed fleet tactics are in scope. Navy-authored reports pass through the Navy Department and ordinary routes, including a loss where no local sender remains. Joint land support still has independent compliance. A constrained encounter/report fixture is required before real river operations count complete.

## Tests and liveness

Two-carrier load/sail/land, one refused/late support, overloaded group, lost carrier, blocked landing, empty parent and men/stores/assignment conservation. Preserve 0083: a counter-suffixed detachment id cannot rejoin the day made. Test a men-transfer which includes its following detachment in the requested total. Reload while loading, aboard, landing and on station; replay matches all hashes.

Stores tests consume both ships and troops, run out on a long route, refill only at a supported source and prevent duplicate delivery. Station tests stop service when unsupplied, preserve overlapping coverage without double counting and feed a real 1.27 clock. Navy fixtures cover encounter loss/withdrawal, independent support refusal and routed news. Count embarked/landed men, all carriers moved, consumed/refilled stores, supplied-station days and encounters/reports. Retire the two afloat-stores stand-ins only after their actual paths pass; a labelled norm is not one of those perpetual-supply stand-ins.
