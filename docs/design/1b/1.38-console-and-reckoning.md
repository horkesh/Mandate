## Mechanisms and typed terms

Menus come only from the seat API and legal kernel verbs. `ConsoleActionResult` identifies accepted/logged command, validation refusal, queued arrival and stated reply [I]. No direct unit selection/control is introduced by presenting capacity, ship, garrison or officer views. Amendments use the pack attention table; approval/send-back free rules remain. Promotion lists, routine returns and ordinary inbox traffic do not end a turn; decisions, report thresholds and changed/expired options use the kernel's stop reasons.

`CoverageStep` names a legal verb path, input report, expected received outcome and invariant checked. The continuous script begins at the original March start and reaches the accepted endpoint without `--at-war` or later-start shortcuts. It must exercise an unchanged-map preview/send/arrival equality case, a visit with business defaults and return, a nomination/list, a live sourced audience declaration, proposal adoption, a siege/garrison, a supported joint river operation and a raid, where accepted fixtures supply the opportunity. Distinguish a local deterministic fixture from full-history evidence; do not force every feature into a single unsourced bot command sequence.

For reckoning, propose `ReckoningScope = closedBattle(id) | terminalCampaign` with explicit timing validation [I]. A separate driver requests the retrospective reader; prospective `viewFor`, desk presenters, menus and rule seats cannot import it. Player-facing retrospective components receive only a dedicated immutable projection type, never a module whose import closure reaches State/truth; preserve 0052's boundary rather than exempting a screen. Recommend that optional post-battle comparison reveal only the closed battle's factual outcomes and received accounts; hidden people values and private compliance weights wait for the terminal scope [I]. Human memory carries disclosures between screens, so import isolation alone cannot protect continuing play. R5 must accept this disclosure policy before shipment. Terminal campaign means an actual accepted end/verdict, or a clearly labelled developer milestone audit outside player play; a November test stop cannot unlock a player truth screen automatically.

`ReckoningTerm` identifies event, actual bounded observation, retained or replay-reconstructed origin, report origin/as-of/receipt, claimed range and comparison reason (`agrees`, `contradicts`, `late`, `not_reported`, `unresolved`, `replay_unavailable`). 0020 also requires this game's true people values at the authorised reckoning, not historians' ratings. Under the recommended disclosure policy, the terminal projection may include scoped `personTrue` values at their actual event/day, reconstructed where necessary, never today's values substituted for past ones. The continuing-play closed-battle projection type excludes latent stats and actual compliance weights. Missing/incompatible World or truncated log is explicit and fails required comparison coverage; do not invent an archive result. Reconstruction observes the historical received reports from replay rather than silently updating them with later truth. R5 supplies factual ending clauses and agency terms, not moral grades or predictions of peace.

## What the seat sees

During play the seat sees received accounts, legal choices, stated conditions and actual command validation. It never sees true candidate scores, unreceived service/status, private compliance, historical defaults or newly computed speech on menu read. After an accepted reckoning request it sees the scoped comparison with its explicit limits. UI-only filtering/reading has no simulation consequence.

All strings are pack keys; fictional testbed scripts use fictional people/terms. Read the relied-on pages before writing real player copy. Every displayed policy/service/prisoner/civilian/nation item retains its tags/signature. R5 governs verdict/slavery-status/founding-aim/agency clauses. No clipped slogan, moral verdict, hidden default marker or unsupported quotation enters a menu. Retrospective access is not a way around source, attribution, protected-resource or contested-number rules.

## Tests and liveness

Architecture: presenters import seat contracts only; reckoning is unreachable from prospective view/driver paths except the explicit validated retrospective request. Poison private truth in ordinary scripts. Verb-list equality, attention, routine-return non-stop, received-subject preservation, read purity and unchanged-map preview/arrival equality remain required.

Run local paths cheaply before the continuous campaign. Save/resume during visit, proposal, joint assembly and grouped situation. Compare command-log replay to the same received state. Reckoning tests include changed later reports, no report, disputed range, pruned evidence reconstructed from saved log, event-day versus changed current stats, wrong World/log hash, cache eviction and illegal live scope. Reconstruction cannot mutate live State, ask a seat or change its log/hash. Measure one short replay before accepting a longer reconstruction budget; keep its work outside the tick budget. Count exercised verbs, actual received outcomes, stop causes and retrospective comparisons; an empty page or queued order is not flow completion. The final long script runs once after preflights, reusing unchanged earlier unit evidence.

Menus can begin after 1.23–1.25 contracts and 1.34a1's groups. Full campaign waits for 1.36/1.37 and actual mechanics. Reckoning waits for 1.16/R5 plus the reviewed replay/projection contract; it need not block early prospective menus. Feeds 1.39 and exit review.

Recommend three M briefs, a separately validated retrospective reader and read-only historical replay [I]. Reject truth in ordinary views, all-battle indefinite save growth, an unsourced generated audience and treating the November milestone as the end of the war.
