## Mechanisms and typed terms

Propose `ReviewFinding {id, reviewedHead, lens, claim, evidencePath, severity, disposition, owner, nextAction}` [I]. The independent reviewer covers canon/decisions, determinism/architecture, historical sourcing and realism in one bounded review; separate gate review covers every touched facet. A verdict is approve, approve-after-fixes or block with exact conditions. Review fixes get targeted verification; persistent disagreement returns one consolidated diagnosis and a lead decision, not another unchanged review loop.

`ExitCriterionDisposition` is evidenced-pass, unmet, held-source/gate, or explicitly changed/retired-by-record. Carrying or parking a criterion never means pass. The lead distinguishes milestone integration, full Phase 1 acceptance and scenario release. November checkpoint evidence cannot settle December topic coverage or an unresolved first-Black-unit capture boundary.

The owner playtest asks whether the principal can understand reports, issue a goal, hear a genuine objection, act through institutions, review a plan and see consequences without direct unit controls. It also checks operational friction: routine lists/returns do not repeatedly stop time, and a default useful path is cheap to operate. Record observed playtest evidence, not predicted satisfaction. S8's browser prototype is separate unless the lead explicitly includes it in the tested surface.

Before the final hand-over batch, complete file/package inventories, run diagnostics and preservation checks. Verify unique commits are preserved, own tree clean, no unreviewed main changes and no stopped foreign processes. Then batch final report, accepted route/canon sync and next-phase recommendations once evidence is settled. Git remains the ledger; do not write a second changelog into FOUNDATION.
