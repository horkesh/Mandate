## Mechanisms and typed terms

Propose `MilitaryObservation {subjectId, measureKind, low, high, unit, asOf, period, theatre, population, inclusions, exclusions, sourceKey, pages, confidence, inference}` [I]. The closed `measureKind` distinguishes enrolled, aggregate-present, present-for-duty, engaged, effectives, killed, wounded, missing and captured. An unresolved conversion is `incompatible`, not a scalar with an unexplained multiplier.

`PhysicalInput` distinguishes a sourced observation from a design norm. Capacity has a commodity, route/vehicle, time basis and constraint; a carrying capacity is not a sailing speed. Stores observations state the as-of day and whether the number is a pack norm. Unknown troop strength is unknown, never a zero-strength opening enemy.

A `CheckpointCandidate` gives low/high, sampling date, compared model field, coverage and binding recommendation. Outward rounding and exclusions are marked [I]. Contemporary returns and later compilations may disagree; preserve both and state which comparator each supports. Count researched operations independently from the run's operations.

## What the seat sees

Research informs true setup and model parameters, while reports determine what commanders and the principal know. No exact OOB table appears as current enemy intelligence merely because it exists in a source file. A dated strength return reaches its recipient through 1.23; stale claims remain stale. Actual weather and passage can differ from a commander's account.

## Gate topics

Capture counts remain losses under 0074. Sources about Black units, sailors, teamsters, prisoners, civilians or Native nations need exact 1.16 placements. Gunboat capacity cannot become a protected-person transport or coercion resource. A battle return cannot conceal missing/captured distinctions in an undifferentiated death count.

## Tests and liveness

Audit source/page resolution, exact units, range order, date bounds, non-duplicated units and identity crosswalks. Check force totals only within compatible populations and dates; retain a reconciliation residual rather than force arithmetic agreement. Every proposed battle fixture identifies observation uncertainty and what would falsify its model assumption.

Research has coverage counts, not mechanic firing bands. 1.30–1.32 must later demonstrate weather, siege, loading and shortage changes in driven fictional fixtures and appropriate real references. A reference without an eligible event carries a quiet reason plus its driving test, not a claimed observed pass.
