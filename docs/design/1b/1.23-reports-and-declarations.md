# 1.23: Reported ground, supply and spoken declarations

Status: proposed for the lead, 2026-11-20; S11 design only [I]. Refines [the 1B proposal](../PHASE-1B-PROPOSAL.md). This extends the knowledge contract; it is not permission for a prospective view to read private truth.

## Purpose and boundary

Close the report gap before raids, weather, capacity and a live audience rely on it. Use two M briefs [I], rather than one M for both systems: infrastructure/supply beliefs; declarations/consult/service subjects. ## Mechanisms and typed terms

For each new claim use `ObservedClaim {kind, subject, observedOn, origin, low, high, unit, statedReason}` [I]. Known edge condition, depot availability, weather and capacity allocation are separate kinds. Claim selection uses receipt, as-of, source bias and expiry with deterministic id ties. Unknown damage follows a declared pack prior/unknown path, never a read of `infraEdgeCondition`.

Derive supply reach from the commander's claimed control, reported edge/depot/obstruction conditions and known allocation contracts. Actual supply execution keeps its existing true passage/condition readers. A commander can plan a route that is in fact barred, then learn through an ordinary failed-movement/shortage report. A stale allocation is a belief, not a reservation.

For a live audience, a command obtains a declaration event from the speaker using the existing people/response machinery, then creates a report to its recipient. `DeclarationReason` distinguishes `stated_objection`, `stated_condition`, `offered_counter`, `no_answer` and `subject_unavailable`. Its payload carries a typed consult subject rather than collapsing it to a person id. In-person receipt may be immediate under the accepted route, but opening a menu cannot create speech, advance a clock or spend attention.

A reported service choice carries the choice, subject, as-of and public institutional consequence. Do not rebuild it from current private `personService` after receipt. Received copies remain historically stable if later service changes. Actual agreement, stated agreement and prospective staff interpretation remain separate.

## What the seat sees

The player sees who said what, when it was observed/received, the claimed range, stated reasons and unanswered subjects. The player does not see true damage, present enemy allocation, actual compliance contribution weights, source-policy internals or a regenerated answer. A prospective audience case uses the declaration already obtained, not a retrospective actual-response explanation. Sources printed in internal research are not automatically player-known citations.

## Pack data and source hand-off

1.18 consumes S4 `sprint/research-military` for physical reports; 1.20 consumes S5 for allocation/foreign/economic reports; 1.17 consumes S6 for attributed declarations and service facts. S3 provides institutional subjects; S7 supplies accepted-sensitive wording through 1.16. Require reviewed commits and read page evidence before authoring real declarations. Route types, priors, bias, expiry and unknown handling are pack rules, with fictional testbed cases.

## Gate topics

Claims about prisoners, camps, Black service, slavery policy, civilians and nations retain their exact tags and signatures. Lineage does not authorise quoting an entire source or revealing protected inputs as a meter. Policy response declarations preserve `costOnly` and the speaker's attribution; unknown intent is not fabricated motive.

## Tests and liveness

Paired worlds with identical received reports, lawful own-force inputs and drafter profile must yield identical drafts despite different hidden edge damage, obstructions, weather or enemy supply. After receipt differs, drafts may differ with a named belief term. Verify failed execution reports rather than instant clairvoyant redrafting. Poison truth imports and public render payloads.

Audience tests: repeated menu reads are pure; one requested declaration produces one origin; changing private actual compliance does not alter the received stated account; consult subjects survive transit/save; old service reports do not update themselves. Test immediate and delayed routes, expiry, reprints and missing lineage. Count emitted/delivered/expired claims, changed-belief drafts, declarations and refusals with magnitudes. Required reference quietness needs a driven fixture and explicit reason, never a blanket zero band.

Depends on 1.15, 1.22's schema contract and accepted R70–R88/0101/0103 integration. It feeds 1.24 assessment, 1.25 visits, 1.27–1.33 knowledge and 1.38 audience menus. Part a can precede b. Gate drafts can use the contract before signing; real sensitive declarations await the relevant facet.
