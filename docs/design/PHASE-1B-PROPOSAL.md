# Phase 1B proposal: the campaign through the 1862 midterms

Status: proposed for the lead, 2026-11-20. S10 only, on `sprint/plan-1b`, based on `ceff796b3c793ef28e79b5d51d7dfe5a2dfabf39`. This is a planning proposal under [0106](../decisions/0106-the-codex-sprint-to-22-november.md), not an accepted phase plan, an implementation, or a gate clearance. Lane numbers, merge sizes, the endpoint and every new mechanism below are recommendations [I]. The lead numbers and records accepted choices after review.

## 1. Outcome and limits

Recommend a continuous history run from 5 March 1861 through **30 November 1862** [I], with both principals doing their jobs through the same people, report, command and compliance machinery. The console must make the added choices playable. Its last checkpoint distinguishes elections already held, results actually known and seats not yet elected or seated. Research must establish each state's timetable before that checkpoint can be scored. November is an integration endpoint, not a claim that all elections or the next House's organisation occurred then.

[FOUNDATION section 12](FOUNDATION.md#12-phase-plans) and [0073](../decisions/0073-phase-1-plan.md) already set 1B's scope: the full home front, foreign and blockade clocks, economy, the remaining people systems and verbs, proposals and the planner's second pass, the gate, research, situations, principals, the Phase 1 done check and exit review. They leave sizing and order to 1A's exit review. **Keep that prerequisite.** S10 prepares a candidate for that review; it does not substitute for it.

Do not fold Phase 2's desk, map polish, portraits, a public demo, an AI voice service or a playable Confederacy into these lanes. The first two remain separate work under S8 and Phase 2. The Union game's Confederate rule seat retains [0046](../decisions/0046-gate-checklist-and-situations-catalogue.md)'s interim restrictions. No new release, money, external contact or publishing is authorised by this document.

The November milestone also does not shorten the sensitive-history release window. [SENSITIVE-HISTORY sections 1 and 4](SENSITIVE-HISTORY.md#1-the-placements) govern the scenario actually shipped. December catalogue stages remain accounted for below, and the full 1861–62 slice retains its release blockers. An earlier start or end cannot make a whole topic disappear.

S1's calibration and test gaps and S2's Linux result are inputs to that decision. A green Phase 0 exit alone is insufficient. The sizes below count coherent merge steps, not days or guaranteed throughput. **S** is one step; **M** is two; **L** is three named steps. A suffix is a separate bounded brief within the row, not permission for unlimited work. Each simulation step is reviewed and integrated before the next depends on it. Model and effort are set explicitly at briefing under the owner's current policy: ordinary implementation uses Sol at medium; routine extraction uses Luna; difficult escalations require a stated reason. This table does not launch workers or silently substitute models. The lead should split any L row whose actual interface exceeds the listed three steps.

## 5. Sequence within the normal lane limit

0106 temporarily permits the sprint's long parallel runs and postpones review. This plan recommends returning after the sprint to [LANES](../LANES.md)'s four active lanes, at most two moving the simulation; the standing reviewer and steward remain separate. S10 does not start any lane. The numbering is an address, not a requirement to run every preceding number to completion.

1. **Establish the inputs.** 1.15, then bounded portions of 1.17–1.21, at most four research/docs lanes. 1.16 can inventory and draft while sources arrive; a missing facet remains held. Accept the contract and gate records before the consumers that need them.
2. **Make new knowledge safe.** 1.22 then 1.23. In the other simulation slot, 1.26a may start after its 1.22 contract lands. Research and gate review occupy the remaining slots. This makes the first new measure and institution checkable before it affects a campaign. After 1.27a lands, bring forward 1.34a's schema and one sourced 1861 foreign/institutional case whose effects all have working owners; do not wait for naval carriage or all remaining systems to author that first case.
3. **Add the consequence owners.** Pair 1.24 with 1.27a; then 1.25a with 1.28 (both read the accepted session contract); then 1.29 with 1.26b. Schedule 1.25b/c and accepted R4's 1.16c in the next free simulation slots, serialising their shared `people`/force/command writes. 1.16c and 1.25c must land before content cases that use their effects. None shares a writer while a neighbour is editing it.
4. **Make movement and operations complete.** 1.30 then 1.31 and 1.32, with 1.33 after their interfaces land. 1.26c fits the other slot once 1.27/1.29's terms are available. Blockade's real-pack evidence is completed after station service exists, not calibrated against a perpetual-supply stand-in.
5. **Integrate playable windows.** 1.34 pairs with a disjoint portion of 1.38; then 1.37's first doctrine slice. Repeat with 1.35 and 1.36 and their doctrine slices. Freeze shared content/owner interfaces at each step; do not run several authors against the same situations or rule-seat file. The first full-path case may be brought forward as soon as its actual dependencies exist; it does not require authoring every mechanic first.
6. **Measure and review.** Complete 1.38 and 1.39's harness, freeze the integrated head, spend the bounded calibration budget, then 1.40. Any failed criterion remains visible with its owner. The lead chooses the next bounded action rather than expanding that budget silently.

Gate research → records → registration → signatures is a parallel chain that can block individual cases or the whole release. A held Dakota or camps facet cannot be exchanged for a better military score.

The eight-seed sweep is different from the budget of eight calibration changes; both retain their defined meaning. Estimate the full campaign cost from measured completed references, not an assumed faster host. No implicit extra seeds, hardware campaign or parameter search.

After an unexpected failure, retain and inspect all categories, including gates not yet reached; reproduce cheaply and verify the correction before one affected rerun. Reuse unaffected fixtures, hashes and source audits explicitly. Finish runtime diagnostics, inventories and preservation checks before the final hand-over/report batch. After eight measured calibration runs, give each unmet criterion a continue-or-park recommendation, evidence, proposed smallest next action and cost.
