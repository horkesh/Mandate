# Phase 1B proposal: the campaign through the 1862 midterms

Status: proposed for the lead, 2026-11-20. S10 proposal, refined by S11 on `sprint/plan-1b` from `b9346453738ccc3f19d66e41acfdf9f66b9dbdac`. This is a planning proposal under [0106](../decisions/0106-the-codex-sprint-to-22-november.md), not an accepted phase plan, an implementation, or a gate clearance. Lane numbers, merge sizes, the endpoint and every new mechanism below are recommendations [I]. The lead numbers and records accepted choices after review.

## 1. Outcome and limits

Recommend a continuous history run from 5 March 1861 through **30 November 1862** [I], with both principals doing their jobs through the same people, report, command and compliance machinery. The console must make the added choices playable. Its last checkpoint distinguishes elections already held, results actually known and seats not yet elected or seated. Research must establish each state's timetable before that checkpoint can be scored. November is an integration endpoint, not a claim that all elections or the next House's organisation occurred then.

[FOUNDATION section 12](FOUNDATION.md#12-phase-plans) and [0073](../decisions/0073-phase-1-plan.md) already set 1B's scope: the full home front, foreign and blockade clocks, economy, the remaining people systems and verbs, proposals and the planner's second pass, the gate, research, situations, principals, the Phase 1 done check and exit review. They leave sizing and order to 1A's exit review. **Keep that prerequisite.** S10 prepares a candidate for that review; it does not substitute for it.

Do not fold Phase 2's desk, map polish, portraits, a public demo, an AI voice service or a playable Confederacy into these lanes. The first two remain separate work under S8 and Phase 2. The Union game's Confederate rule seat retains [0046](../decisions/0046-gate-checklist-and-situations-catalogue.md)'s interim restrictions. No new release, money, external contact or publishing is authorised by this document.

The November milestone also does not shorten the sensitive-history release window. [SENSITIVE-HISTORY sections 1 and 4](SENSITIVE-HISTORY.md#1-the-placements) govern the scenario actually shipped. December catalogue stages remain accounted for below, and the full 1861–62 slice retains its release blockers. An earlier start or end cannot make a whole topic disappear.

## 5. Sequence within the normal lane limit

0106 temporarily permits the sprint's long parallel runs and postpones review. This plan recommends returning after the sprint to [LANES](../LANES.md)'s four active lanes, at most two moving the simulation; the standing reviewer and steward remain separate. S10 does not start any lane. The numbering is an address, not a requirement to run every preceding number to completion.

1. **Accept entry and source contracts.** 1.15's three steps, then the bounded 1.17–1.21 research briefs with draft-only 1.16 records. Do not treat concurrent sprint status messages as reviewed inputs. Pin the real commits at intake.
2. **Make knowledge and occurrences safe.** 1.22a/b/c, then the separately bounded 1.23a/b. Bring **1.34a1** forward now: recurrence/group/paid-deferral semantics and fictional probes precede visits, gate group probes and affected content. Registry/signature work follows accepted records/schema; it does not precede its own required format. In a disjoint slot, land **1.26a1**'s session/authority interface after its source/schema/report prerequisites; a2's actual investigation/custody waits for 1.25c's generic people-owned restrictions and its accepted facet.
3. **Open people and ordinary economic paths.** Bring 1.25c's post lifecycle forward for affected appointments, and take 1.24's battle/stance/relief contracts in bounded order. 1.25a reads the early session contract; 1.25b reads declarations/defaults. 1.27a's foreign-act interface precedes 1.28a/b Treasury. Then 1.29a/b publishes real procurement/refill and ordinary supply demand; 1.29c capacity and 1.29d voluntary service formation remain separately gated and are never replaced by a protected manpower proxy. Use the next free slot for accepted 1.16c service eligibility; preserve its 0074/0076 amendment prerequisite. Serialize shared writers.
